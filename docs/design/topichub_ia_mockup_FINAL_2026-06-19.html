Trigonometry

High trend ~12 marks · Sections B/C/D — identities, evaluations and one heights & distances application.
1Label every diagram. Mark vertices and the right angle — an unlabelled figure in heights & distances loses the diagram mark even if the answer is right.
2Write the formula before substituting. CBSE gives a method mark for the correct formula even if the final value slips.
3Watch the standard-angle values. Mixing the 30°/60° ratios is the most common 1-mark loss in this chapter.
4Finish cleanly. State the final answer with units and round exactly as asked — unrounded or unit-less answers drop the last mark.
