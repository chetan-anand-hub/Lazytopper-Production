What is strong

What is not fully there yet

The biggest gap is this:

When the app does AI top-up for missing practice questions, it only generates new question variants with:

* `questionText`
* `marks`
* `difficulty`
* `bloomSkill`
* optionally `section`

It does not generate fresh `solutionSteps`, `finalAnswer`, per-step CBSE marks, or diagram plans for those new AI-created questions  

Even more importantly, when AI variants are mapped back into practice questions, they inherit `solutionSteps` and `answer` from the seed/template question rather than generating a truly matched new solution for the new wording 

That means your current system is safeest for:

* authored bank questions

But riskier for:

* AI-generated “more like this” questions

Because the new question text may differ while the attached steps can still be inherited from the old template.

My audit verdict

As of today, your repo is in a solid state for a bank-first product:

* authored questions already support solutions well
* many diagram-heavy questions now start with a diagram/construction step
* worksheet export is production-usable
* visual explainers are wired

But it is not yet fully ready for your larger vision of:

* 900 questions
* strict step-by-step marking
* diagram/image per step
* CBSE-scheme-aligned marks for every generated variant

The current weakness is the AI top-up path, not the bank structure.
