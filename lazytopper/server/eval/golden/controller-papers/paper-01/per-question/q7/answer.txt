(a) Hormone X is thyroxine. It is secreted by the thyroid gland.
(b) In a scary situation this hormone acts on the heart. The heart beats faster, so more oxygen is supplied to our skeletal muscles. Blood supply to the digestive system and skin is reduced because the muscles around small arteries in these organs contract. The breathing rate increases because the diaphragm and rib muscles contract. So the body is ready to deal with the emergency.
