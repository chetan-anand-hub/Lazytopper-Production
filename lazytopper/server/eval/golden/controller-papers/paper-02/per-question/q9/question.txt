Q9. [4 marks]
Case study: A person allowed a narrow beam of white light from the sun to enter a dark room through a small aperture and placed a glass prism in its path in such a manner that the beam falls on the face AB of the prism. A screen S is placed on the other side of the prism, facing AC. On turning the prism slowly, a beautiful band of colours is obtained on the screen. It is the spectrum of sunlight.
(a) Name the phenomenon due to which a prism splits the incident white light into a band of colours. [1]
(b) State the reason of getting a band of seven colours in the above case. [1]
(c) Draw a labelled ray diagram to show the formation of a rainbow. [2]
