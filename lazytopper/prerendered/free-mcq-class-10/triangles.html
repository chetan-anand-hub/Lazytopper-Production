  1. Q1

    In △ABC, a line XY parallel to BC cuts AB at X and AC at Y. If BY bisects ∠XYC, then:
    • (a)BC=CY
    • (b)BC=BY
    • (c)BC=CY
    • (d)BC=BY
    Show answer

    Answer: (a) BC=CY

    Since XY∥BC, ∠XYB=∠YBC (alternate angles). BY bisects ∠XYC, so ∠XYB=∠BYC. Hence ∠YBC=∠BYC and BC=CY.

  2. Q2

    In △ABC and △DEF, it is given that ∠B=∠E,∠F=∠C and AB=3DE, then the two triangles are:
    • (a)Congruent but not similar
    • (b)Similar but not congruent
    • (c)Neither congruent not similar
    • (d)Similar as well as congruent
    Show answer

    Answer: (b) Similar but not congruent

    Two pairs of equal angles make the triangles similar (AA). Since AB=3DE, the sides are not equal, so they are not congruent.

  3. Q3

    If ABC is a right triangle right-angled at B and M, N are the mid-points of AB and BC respectively, then 4(AN2+CM2)=
    • (a)4AC2
    • (b)5AC2
    • (c)45​AC2
    • (d)6AC2
    Show answer

    Answer: (b) 5AC2

    AN2=AB2+4BC2​ and CM2=BC2+4AB2​. So 4(AN2+CM2)=5(AB2+BC2)=5AC2.

  4. Q4

    The line segments joining the midpoint of the sides of a triangle form four triangles, each of which is:
    • (a)Congruent to the original triangle.
    • (b)Similar to the original triangle.
    • (c)An isosceles triangle.
    • (d)An equilateral triangle.
    Show answer

    Answer: (b) Similar to the original triangle.

    By the midpoint theorem each small triangle has sides parallel to and half of the original's, so each is similar to the original triangle.
