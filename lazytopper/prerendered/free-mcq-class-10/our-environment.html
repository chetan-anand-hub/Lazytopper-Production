Our Environment MCQs

  1. Q1

    Accumulation of non-biodegradable pesticides in the food chain in increasing amount at each higher trophic level is known as:
    • (a)Eutrophication.
    • (b)Pollution.
    • (c)Bio-magnification.
    • (d)Accumulation.
    Show answer

    Answer: (c) Bio-magnification.

    The accumulation of non-biodegradable pesticides in increasing amounts at each higher trophic level is called bio-magnification.

  2. Q2

    Bad ozone is formed in.
    • (a)Atmosphere.
    • (b)Ionosphere.
    • (c)Stratosphere.
    • (d)Troposphere.
    Show answer

    Answer: (d) Troposphere.

    Ground-level (bad) ozone forms in the troposphere when pollutants from vehicles and industry react chemically in sunlight.

  3. Q3

    In an ecosystem: 1. The flow of energy is unidirectional. 2. The flow of materials is unidirectional. 3. The flow of materials is cyclic. 4. The flow of energy is cyclic.
    • (a)(i) and (ii)
    • (b)(ii) and (iii)
    • (c)(i) and (iv)
    • (d)(i) and (iii)
    Show answer

    Answer: (d) (i) and (iii)

    In an ecosystem the flow of energy is unidirectional and the flow of materials is cyclic.

  4. Q4

    In a food chain, the third trophic level is always occupied by:
    • (a)Carnivores.
    • (b)Herbivores.
    • (c)Decomposers.
    • (d)Producers.
    Show answer

    Answer: (a) Carnivores.

    The first trophic level is producers, the second herbivores and the third carnivores.

  5. Q5

    In the food chain comprising of a snake, grass, insect, and frog, the secondary consumer is:
    • (a)Insect.
    • (b)Snake.
    • (c)Frog.
    • (d)Grass.
    Show answer

    Answer: (c) Frog.

    Grass (producer) is eaten by the insect (primary consumer), which is eaten by the frog (secondary consumer), which is eaten by the snake (tertiary consumer).

  6. Q6

    Which of the following are decomposers of dead organisms?
    BacteriaFungiViruses
    (a)noyesyes
    (b)yesnoyes
    (c)yesyesno

    • (c) Bacteria: yes, Fungi: yes, Viruses: no
    • (d) Bacteria: yes, Fungi: yes, Viruses: yes

    Answer: (c) Bacteria: yes, Fungi: yes, Viruses: no
