Control and Coordination MCQs

  1. Q1

    The part of brain which controls the involuntary action such as heart beat, breathing, blood pressure, etc. is:
    • (a)Pons.
    • (b)Medulla.
    • (c)Cerebrum.
    • (d)Cerebellum.
    Show answer

    Answer: (b) Medulla.

    The medulla controls involuntary actions such as heartbeat, breathing, blood pressure and peristaltic movement.

  2. Q2

    Which of the following are often called glands of emergency?
    • (a)Thyroid.
    • (b)Pituitary.
    • (c)Adrenal.
    • (d)Pancreas.
    Show answer

    Answer: (c) Adrenal.

    The adrenal glands are called glands of emergency. They secrete adrenaline, which prepares the body for action.

  3. Q3

    The bending of the root of a plant away from a source of light is caused by a plant hormone called:
    • (a)Cytokinin.
    • (b)Gibberellin.
    • (c)Abscisic acid.
    • (d)Auxin.
    Show answer

    Answer: (d) Auxin.

    The plant hormone auxin is responsible for negative phototropism in roots.

  4. Q4

    A doctor advised a person to take an injection of insulin because:
    • (a)His blood pressure was low.
    • (b)His heart was beating slowly.
    • (c)He was suffering from goiter.
    • (d)His sugar level in blood was high.
    Show answer

    Answer: (d) His sugar level in blood was high.

    In diabetes the blood sugar level becomes high, and in some patients the disease is managed with insulin injections.

  5. Q5

    The gustatory receptors of our body are in one of the following organs. This organ is:
    • (a)Ear.
    • (b)Nose.
    • (c)Tongue.
    • (d)Skin.
    Show answer

    Answer: (c) Tongue.

    The gustatory receptors, which detect taste, are present in the tongue.

  6. Q6

    The plant part which exhibits negative geotropism is:
    • (a)Root.
    • (b)Stem.
    • (c)Branch.
    • (d)Leaves.
    Show answer

    Answer: (b) Stem.

    The stem grows upward, opposite to the force of gravity, so it shows negative geotropism.

  7. Q7

    Which of the following is not caused by a growth movement?
    • (a)Bending of the shoot of a plant in response to light.
    • (b)Closing up of leaves of a sensitive plant on touching with an object.
    • (c)Climbing up of a plant on an object by using tendrils.
    • (d)Movement of the root of a plant towards a source of water.
    Show answer

    Answer: (b) Closing up of leaves of a sensitive plant on touching with an object.

    Closing of sensitive-plant leaves on touch is a non-directional movement in response to touch, not a growth movement.
