Human Eye and the Colourful World MCQs

  1. Q1

    How does the eye change in order to focus on near or distant objects?
    • (a)The lens moves in or out.
    • (b)The retina moves in or out.
    • (c)The lens becomes thicker or thinner.
    • (d)The pupil gets larger or smaller.
    Show answer

    Answer: (c) The lens becomes thicker or thinner.

    The eye lens becomes thicker to focus on nearby objects and thinner to focus on distant objects.

  2. Q2

    The stars twinkle but the planets do not twinkle at night because:
    • (a)The stars are small but the planets are large.
    • (b)The stars are very large but planets are small.
    • (c)The stars are much nearer but planets are far off.
    • (d)The stars are far off but planets are nearer to earth.
    Show answer

    Answer: (d) The stars are far off but planets are nearer to earth.

    Stars twinkle because they are far away. Planets do not twinkle because they are nearer to the earth.

  3. Q3

    Which of the following phenomena contributes significantly to the reddish appearance of the sun at sunrise or sunset?
    • (a)Dispersion of light.
    • (b)Scattering of light.
    • (c)Total internal reflection of light.
    • (d)Reflection of light from the earth.
    Show answer

    Answer: (b) Scattering of light.

    Red light scatters the least and travels farthest. At sunrise and sunset light travels a longer path, so mainly red light reaches us.

  4. Q4

    The sky appears blue because some of the blue component of sunlight is scattered by:
    • (a)Gas molecules present in air.
    • (b)Dust particles present in air.
    • (c)Water droplets suspended in air.
    • (d)Soot particles present in air.
    Show answer

    Answer: (a) Gas molecules present in air.

    Some of the blue component of sunlight is scattered by gas molecules (nitrogen and oxygen) present in air.

  5. Q5

    A student is observing the diagram showing the path of a ray of light passing through a glass prism. He would find that for all angles of incidence the ray of light bends:
    • (a)Towards the normal while entering into the prism and away from the normal while emerging out of the prism.
    • (b)Away from the normal while entering into the prism and towards the normal while emerging out of the prism.
    • (c)Away from the normal while entering as well as while emerging out of the prism.
    • (d)Towards the normal while entering as well as while emerging out of the prism.
    Show answer

    Answer: (a) Towards the normal while entering into the prism and away from the normal while emerging out of the prism.

    Entering glass (rarer to denser) the ray bends towards the normal. Emerging (denser to rarer) it bends away from the normal.

  6. Q6

    The colour of scattered light depends on.
    • (a)Size of scattering particles.
    • (b)Wavelength of light.
    • (c)(Both (a) and (b).
    • (d)None of these.
    Show answer

    Answer: (c) (Both (a) and (b).

    The colour of scattered light depends on the size of the scattering particles: very fine particles scatter mainly blue light, larger particles scatter longer wavelengths such as red.
