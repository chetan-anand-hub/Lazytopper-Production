  1. Q1

    The pair of equations x+2y+5=0 and −3x−6y+1=0 has:
    • (a)A unique solutions.
    • (b)Exactly two solutions.
    • (c)Infinitely many solutions.
    • (d)No solution.
    Show answer

    Answer: (d) No solution.

    Here a2​a1​​=−31​, b2​b1​​=−62​=−31​ and c2​c1​​=5. Since a2​a1​​=b2​b1​​=c2​c1​​, the lines are parallel and there is no solution.

  2. Q2

    If the system of equations 2x+3y=7 2ax+(a+b)y=28 has infinitely many solutions, then:
    • (a)a = 2b
    • (b)b = 2a
    • (c)a + 2b = 0
    • (d)2a + b = 0
    Show answer

    Answer: (b) b = 2a

    For infinitely many solutions 2a2​=a+b3​=287​. This gives a=4 and a+b=12, so b=8, i.e. b=2a.

  3. Q3

    If 4x+6y=3xy and 8x+9y=5xy then:
    • (a)x = 2, y = 3
    • (b)x = 1, y = 2
    • (c)x = 3, y = 4
    • (d)x = 1, y = -1
    Show answer

    Answer: (c) x = 3, y = 4

    Divide by xy and put u=x1​, v=y1​: 6u+4v=3 and 9u+8v=5. Solving gives u=31​, v=41​, so x=3, y=4.

  4. Q4

    Choose the correct answer from the given four options: If the lines given by 3x+2ky=2 and 2x+5y+1=0 are parallel, then the value of k is:
    • (a)4−5​
    • (b)52​
    • (c)415​
    • (d)23​
    Show answer

    Answer: (c) 415​

    For parallel lines 23​=52k​, so k=415​.

  5. Q5

    If x2​+y3​=6 and x1​+2y1​=2 then:
    • (a)x=1, y=32​
    • (b)x=32​, y=1
    • (c)x=1, y=23​
    • (d)x=23​, y=1
    Show answer

    Answer: (b) x=32​, y=1

    Doubling the second equation gives x2​+y1​=4. Subtracting from the first gives y2​=2, so y=1 and then x=32​.

  6. Q6

    If x=a,y=b is the solution of the systems of equations x−y=2 and x+y=4, then the values of a and b are, respectively.
    • (a)3 and 1
    • (b)3 and 5
    • (c)5 and 3
    • (d)-1 and -3
    Show answer

    Answer: (a) 3 and 1

    Then a−b=2 and a+b=4. Adding gives 2a=6, so a=3 and b=1.
