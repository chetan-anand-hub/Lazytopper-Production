How your tests are marked

  • Section A (the MCQs) is marked the moment you submit, free on every account.
  • Sections B–D (B–E in full-length tests): write on paper and upload a photo. The Test Series checks every written answer step by step, 2 checks per paper, like a CBSE examiner, and shows where each mark was lost.
  • Downloading and printing these papers comes with the Test Series and the Board Pass.

Questions

Is there a usage limit?

Fair use: your pass covers heavy daily practice, for example a full mock marked every day. If you go far beyond that in a short time, marking pauses until your usage resets, and then carries on.
