Exemplar Q403 marks
If a plant is releasing carbon dioxide and taking in oxygen during the day, does it mean that there is no photosynthesis occurring? Justify your answer.

Step-by-step solution

  1. [1 mark] Plants always respire (continuous, day and night).
  2. [0.5 mark] Plants also photosynthesise when light is available.
  3. [0.5 mark] Net gas exchange = photosynthesis − respiration.
  4. [1 mark] If respiration > photosynthesis (e.g., low light), net CO₂ released — but photosynthesis may still be ongoing.

Answer: No — photosynthesis may still be occurring but at a slower rate than respiration → net CO₂ release.

Exemplar Q413 marks
Why do fishes die when taken out of water?

Step-by-step solution

  1. [1 mark] Fish gills require water for support (surface area maintenance).
  2. [1 mark] In air: gills stick together / dry out → cannot exchange gases.
  3. [1 mark] Fish suffocates due to lack of O₂ intake → dies.

Answer: Gills only work in water (collapse and dry out in air) — fish cannot get O₂ → suffocates.

Exemplar Q453 marks
Leaves of a healthy potted plant were coated with vaseline. Will this plant remain healthy for long? Give reasons for your answer.

Step-by-step solution

  1. [1 mark] Vaseline blocks stomata → no gas exchange → no photosynthesis (no CO₂ in) and no respiration of leaves (no O₂ exchange).
  2. [1 mark] No transpiration → no water transport from roots; leaves heat up.
  3. [1 mark] Plant cannot make food or stay cool → wilts and dies.

Answer: No — stomata blocked → no photosynthesis, no transpiration, no gas exchange → plant dies.

Exemplar Q473 marks
Match the words of Column (A) with that of Column (B): A: (i) Leech (ii) Paramecium (iii) Deer (iv) Green plant B: (a) Autotrophic nutrition (b) Heterotrophic nutrition (c) Parasitic nutrition (d) Digestion in food vacuoles

Step-by-step solution

  1. [1 mark] Leech sucks blood from host → parasitic → (c).
  2. [0.5 mark] Paramecium engulfs food into food vacuoles → digestion in vacuoles → (d).
  3. [0.5 mark] Deer is a herbivore (heterotroph) → (b).
  4. [1 mark] Green plant makes own food → autotrophic → (a).

Answer: (i)-(c); (ii)-(d); (iii)-(b); (iv)-(a).

Exemplar Q523 marks
What is the significance of emulsification of fats?

Step-by-step solution

  1. [1 mark] Fats enter intestine as large droplets — small surface area for enzyme action.
  2. [1 mark] Bile salts emulsify them → many small droplets → vastly increased surface area.
  3. [1 mark] Lipase can now efficiently digest the emulsified fats to fatty acids + glycerol.

Answer: Increases surface area of fats so lipase can digest them efficiently.

Exemplar Q553 marks

Step-by-step solution

  4. [1 mark] Platelets: blood clotting → (iii).

Answer: (a)-(ii), (b)-(i), (c)-(iv), (d)-(iii).

Exemplar Q603 marks
In each of the following situations what happens to the rate of photosynthesis? (a) Cloudy days (b) No rainfall in the area (c) Good manuring in the area (d) Stomata get blocked due to dust

Step-by-step solution

  1. [1 mark] (a) Light intensity ↓ → rate ↓.
  2. [0.5 mark] (b) Water ↓ → rate ↓ (water is a raw material).
  3. [0.5 mark] (c) Better mineral nutrition → healthier plant → rate ↑.
  4. [1 mark] (d) Blocked stomata → CO₂ ↓ → rate ↓.

Answer: (a) ↓ (b) ↓ (c) ↑ (d) ↓.
