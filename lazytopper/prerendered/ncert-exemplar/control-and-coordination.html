Exemplar Q61 mark
Which of the following statements are true? (i) Sudden action in response to something in the environment is called reflex action; (ii) Sensory neurons carry signals from spinal cord to muscles; (iii) Motor neurons carry signals from receptors to spinal cord; (iv) The path through which signals are transmitted from a receptor to a muscle or a gland is called reflex arc

Step-by-step solution

  1. (i) Correct definition of reflex action.
  2. (ii) WRONG — sensory neurons carry from receptor TO spinal cord.
  3. (iii) WRONG — motor neurons carry from spinal cord TO muscles.
  4. (iv) Correct definition of reflex arc. Answer: (C)

Answer: (c) (i) and (iv).

Exemplar Q71 mark
Which of the following statements are true about the brain? (i) The main thinking part of brain is hind brain; (ii) Centres of hearing, smell, memory, sight etc are located in fore brain; (iii) Involuntary actions like salivation, vomiting, blood pressure are controlled by the medulla in the hind brain; (iv) Cerebellum does not control posture and balance of the body

Step-by-step solution

  1. (i) WRONG — fore-brain (not hind) is the main thinking part.
  2. (ii) Correct — sensory centres are in fore-brain.
  3. (iii) Correct — medulla controls involuntary actions.
  4. (iv) WRONG — cerebellum DOES control posture/balance. Answer: (C)

Answer: (c) (ii) and (iii).

Exemplar Q402 marks
What is a tropic movement? Explain with an example.

Step-by-step solution

  1. Tropic = directional growth response to a stimulus (light, gravity, water, chemicals).
  2. Positive: towards stimulus; Negative: away from stimulus.
  3. Example: phototropism (shoot toward light), geotropism (roots toward gravity), hydrotropism (roots toward water), chemotropism (pollen tube toward ovule).

Answer: Tropism = directional growth response to external stimulus. Example: phototropism (shoot toward light).

Exemplar Q432 marks
Answer the following: (a) Which hormone is responsible for the changes noticed in females at puberty? (b) Dwarfism results due to deficiency of which hormone? (c) Blood sugar level rises due to deficiency of which hormone? (d) Iodine is necessary for the synthesis of which hormone?

Step-by-step solution

  1. Female puberty changes = oestrogen (from ovary).
  2. Dwarfism = deficiency of growth hormone (pituitary).
  3. High blood sugar = insulin deficiency (pancreas).
  4. Iodine needed for thyroxin (thyroid).

Answer: (a) Oestrogen (b) Growth hormone (c) Insulin (d) Thyroxin.
