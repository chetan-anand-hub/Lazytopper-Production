What’s inside a pack?

For each chapter: the chapter’s questions grouped by CBSE section (A to E) with a matching answer key, two chapter tests and one worksheet (each with a key). Mock packs are five full 80-mark papers with keys. Every question has a worked solution.

Can I download again later?

Yes. Sign in and open My packs on this page — download any file, any time, as often as you like.

Whose copy is it?

Packs are for your own use. Each PDF carries your name and email on every page, so please don’t share the files.

Does it renew?

Never. It is a one-time payment and nothing is charged again. If you take the Board Pass within 7 days, the pack price is credited against it.
