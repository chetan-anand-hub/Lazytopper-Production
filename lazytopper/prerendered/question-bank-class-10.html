What is in the Class 10 question bank

The question bank is organised chapter by chapter, for Maths and for Science, and follows the CBSE 2026-27 syllabus. Topics removed from that syllabus are not in it. Across the bank there are 10,000+ questions with solutions, including 2,800+ competency-based questions (CBQs), the kind CBSE now sets for about half the paper.

Questions run from 1-mark MCQs and assertion-reason items to 2, 3 and 5-mark answers and 4-mark case studies, so you can practise each section of the paper. Many questions come with a step-by-step solution, which is how board answers are marked.

How to use the question bank

  1. Read the chapter notes first, so the formulas and definitions are fresh.
  2. Solve a handful of questions from each section on paper, writing every step.
  3. Compare your steps with the worked solution and mark where you lost marks.
  4. Return to the chapter’s CBQs once the basics are steady, since about half the paper is competency-based.
