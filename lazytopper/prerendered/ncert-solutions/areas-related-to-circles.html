NCERT Ex 11.1 Q141 mark
The area of a sector of angle p° (in degrees) of a circle of radius R is
Step 1 By the unitary method, fraction of full circle = p/360.

Step-by-step solution

  1. By the unitary method, fraction of full circle = p/360.
  2. Area of sector = (p/360) × πR².

Answer: (p/360) × πR² — option (d).

NCERT Ex 11.1 Q11 mark
The area of a sector of a circle of radius 6 cm and angle 60° (use π = 22/7) is approximately
Step 1 Area = (θ/360) × πr² = (60/360) × (22/7) × 36 = (1/6) × (22 × 36/7) = (22 × 6)/7 = 132/7.

Step-by-step solution

  1. Area = (θ/360) × πr² = (60/360) × (22/7) × 36 = (1/6) × (22 × 36/7) = (22 × 6)/7 = 132/7.
  2. ≈ 18.857 cm².

Answer: 132/7 cm² — option (a).

NCERT Ex 11.1 Q22 marks
Find the area of a quadrant of a circle whose circumference is 22 cm.
Step 1 Circumference = 2πr = 22 ⇒ r = 22/(2π) = 22 × 7/(2 × 22) = 7/2 cm.

Step-by-step solution

  1. Circumference = 2πr = 22 ⇒ r = 22/(2π) = 22 × 7/(2 × 22) = 7/2 cm.
  2. Quadrant = (1/4) × πr² = (1/4) × (22/7) × (49/4) = (22 × 49)/(4 × 7 × 4) = (22 × 7)/16 = 154/16 = 77/8 cm² = 9.625 cm².

Answer: Area of quadrant = 77/8 cm² (= 9.625 cm²).

NCERT Ex 11.1 Q32 marks
The minute hand of a clock is 14 cm long. Find the area swept by the minute hand in 5 minutes.
Step 1 The minute hand sweeps 360° in 60 minutes, so in 5 minutes it sweeps 360 × 5/60 = 30°.

Step-by-step solution

  1. The minute hand sweeps 360° in 60 minutes, so in 5 minutes it sweeps 360 × 5/60 = 30°.
  2. Area = (30/360) × πr² = (1/12) × (22/7) × 196 = (22 × 196)/(12 × 7) = (22 × 28)/12 = 616/12 = 154/3 cm² ≈ 51.33 cm².

Answer: Area swept = 154/3 cm² ≈ 51.33 cm².

NCERT Ex 11.1 Q5(i,ii)3 marks
In a circle of radius 21 cm, an arc subtends an angle of 60° at the centre. Find (i) the length of the arc and (ii) the area of the sector formed by the arc.
Step 1 (i) Length = (θ/360) × 2πr = (60/360) × 2 × (22/7) × 21 = (1/6) × (44 × 21/7) = (1/6) × 132 = 22 cm.

Step-by-step solution

  1. (i) Length = (θ/360) × 2πr = (60/360) × 2 × (22/7) × 21 = (1/6) × (44 × 21/7) = (1/6) × 132 = 22 cm.
  2. (ii) Area of sector = (θ/360) × πr² = (60/360) × (22/7) × 441 = (1/6) × (22 × 441/7) = (1/6) × (22 × 63) = (22 × 63)/6 = 1386/6 = 231 cm².

Answer: (i) 22 cm; (ii) 231 cm².

NCERT Ex 11.1 Q4(i)3 marks
A chord of a circle of radius 10 cm subtends a right angle at the centre. Find the area of the corresponding minor segment. (Use π = 3.14.)
Step 1 Sector area = (90/360) × πr² = (1/4) × 3.14 × 100 = 78.5 cm².

Step-by-step solution

  1. Sector area = (90/360) × πr² = (1/4) × 3.14 × 100 = 78.5 cm².
  2. Triangle area = (1/2) × r × r × sin 90° = (1/2) × 10 × 10 × 1 = 50 cm² (right-angled at centre).
  3. Minor segment = sector − triangle = 78.5 − 50 = 28.5 cm².

Answer: Minor segment = 28.5 cm².

NCERT Ex 11.1 Q63 marks
A chord of a circle of radius 15 cm subtends an angle of 60° at the centre. Find the area of the corresponding minor segment. (Use π = 3.14 and √3 = 1.73.)
Step 1 Sector area = (60/360) × 3.14 × 225 = (1/6) × 706.5 = 117.75 cm².

Step-by-step solution

  1. Sector area = (60/360) × 3.14 × 225 = (1/6) × 706.5 = 117.75 cm².
  2. Triangle area (equilateral with side r since the chord-angle is 60° and OA = OB = 15): area = (√3/4) × 15² = (1.73/4) × 225 = 1.73 × 56.25 = 97.3125 ≈ 97.31 cm².
  3. Minor segment = 117.75 − 97.31 ≈ 20.44 cm².

Answer: Minor segment ≈ 20.44 cm².
