In-text questions

In-text Q5 · Section 6.12 marks
What is the role of the brain in reflex action?

Step-by-step solution

  1. Reflex arc is formed in the spinal cord, bypassing the brain for speed.
  2. Brain's role is limited — it receives information after the reflex has occurred.
  3. This makes us aware of the stimulus (e.g., we feel the heat) after we have already reacted.

Answer: Brain receives info AFTER reflex; spinal cord controls the actual reflex response.

In-text Q2 · Section 6.32 marks
Why is the use of iodised salt advisable?

Step-by-step solution

  1. Iodine is needed by thyroid gland to make thyroxin.
  2. Thyroxin regulates metabolism (carbs, proteins, fats).
  3. Iodine deficiency → less thyroxin → goitre + metabolic problems.
  4. Iodised salt ensures everyone gets adequate iodine in diet.

Answer: Iodised salt prevents iodine deficiency → ensures normal thyroxin production → prevents goitre.

Exercise Q62 marks
How does phototropism occur in plants?

Step-by-step solution

  1. Auxin is produced at the shoot tip.
  2. When light hits one side, auxin moves to the shaded (away-from-light) side.
  3. Cells on the shady side elongate more due to higher auxin.
  4. Unequal elongation → shoot bends towards the light source.

Answer: Auxin moves to shaded side → cells elongate more there → shoot bends towards light.
