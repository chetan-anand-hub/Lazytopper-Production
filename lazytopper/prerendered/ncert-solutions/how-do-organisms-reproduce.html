In-text Q1 · Section 7.12 marks
What is the importance of DNA copying in reproduction?

Step-by-step solution

  1. DNA carries genetic information for body design and proteins.
  2. Copying ensures offspring inherit parent's traits → species continuity.
  3. Small copying errors → variations → raw material for evolution.
  4. Without DNA copying, no inheritance and no survival of species.

Answer: Ensures continuity of species by transmitting genetic info; copying errors create variations driving evolution.

In-text Q5 · Section 7.22 marks
Why is DNA copying an essential part of the process of reproduction?

Step-by-step solution

  1. DNA holds blueprint for body design (proteins, traits).
  2. Reproduction transfers parent's genetic info to offspring → requires DNA copy.
  3. Without copying: offspring lacks genetic info → cannot form organism.
  4. Copying errors give variations → basis of evolution.

Answer: DNA copying ensures offspring inherit parent's blueprint → continuity of species; errors give evolutionary variation.

In-text Q4 · Section 7.32 marks
How does the embryo get nourishment inside the mother's body?

Step-by-step solution

  1. A special tissue called PLACENTA develops in the uterine wall.
  2. Embryo's side has villi (finger-like projections) → large surface area.
  3. Mother's side has blood spaces around villi.
  4. Glucose and O₂ diffuse from mother's blood to embryo via placenta.
  5. Embryo's waste (urea, CO₂) diffuses back to mother for excretion.

Answer: Through placenta — villi on embryo's side + maternal blood spaces; allows nutrient/gas exchange.
