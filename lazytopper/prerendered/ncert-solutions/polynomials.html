Exercise 2.2

NCERT Ex 2.2 Q1(i)1 mark
The zeroes of the quadratic polynomial x² – 2x – 8 are
Step 1 Factorise: x² – 2x – 8 = (x – 4)(x + 2).

Step-by-step solution

  1. Factorise: x² – 2x – 8 = (x – 4)(x + 2).
  2. Zeroes occur when each factor is zero: x = 4 or x = –2.

Answer: –2 and 4 — option (a).

NCERT Ex 2.2 Q1(ii)2 marks
Find the zeroes of the quadratic polynomial 4s² – 4s + 1.
Step 1 4s² – 4s + 1 = (2s – 1)². Treat as a perfect square.

Step-by-step solution

  1. 4s² – 4s + 1 = (2s – 1)². Treat as a perfect square.
  2. Setting (2s – 1)² = 0 gives 2s – 1 = 0 ⇒ s = 1/2 (a repeated zero).
  3. Both zeroes are 1/2.

Answer: Zeroes: 1/2 and 1/2 (repeated).

NCERT Ex 2.2 Q1(v)2 marks
Find the zeroes of t² – 15.
Step 1 t² – 15 = 0 ⇒ t² = 15 ⇒ t = ±√15.

Step-by-step solution

  1. t² – 15 = 0 ⇒ t² = 15 ⇒ t = ±√15.
  2. Hence the zeroes are √15 and –√15.

Answer: Zeroes: √15 and –√15.

NCERT Ex 2.2 Q1(iii)3 marks
Find the zeroes of 6x² – 3 – 7x and verify the relationship between zeroes and coefficients.
Step 1 Write in standard form: 6x² – 7x – 3. Factorise by splitting middle term: 6x² – 9x + 2x – 3 = 3x(2x – 3) + 1(2x – 3) = (3x + 1)(2x – 3).

Step-by-step solution

  1. Write in standard form: 6x² – 7x – 3. Factorise by splitting middle term: 6x² – 9x + 2x – 3 = 3x(2x – 3) + 1(2x – 3) = (3x + 1)(2x – 3).
  2. Zeroes: x = −1/3 and x = 3/2.
  3. Sum: −1/3 + 3/2 = (−2 + 9)/6 = 7/6 = −(−7)/6 = −b/a. ✓
  4. Product: (−1/3)(3/2) = −1/2 = −3/6 = c/a. ✓

Answer: Zeroes: −1/3 and 3/2; relationship verified.

NCERT Ex 2.2 Q2(ii)3 marks
Find a quadratic polynomial whose sum of zeroes is √2 and product of zeroes is 1/3.
Step 1 Polynomial form: x² – (sum)x + product = x² – √2·x + 1/3.

Step-by-step solution

  1. Polynomial form: x² – (sum)x + product = x² – √2·x + 1/3.
  2. Multiply by 3 to clear fractions: 3x² – 3√2·x + 1.

Answer: 3x² – 3√2·x + 1 (or x² – √2·x + 1/3).
