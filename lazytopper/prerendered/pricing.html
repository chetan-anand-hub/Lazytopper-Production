Questions parents ask

How is the Board Pass different from Question Bank + Mocks?

The Board Pass is online: your child writes answers on paper, uploads a photo, and LazyTopper marks every step like a CBSE examiner. Question Bank + Mocks is for offline practice: printable questions and full papers with worked solutions and the marking scheme. LazyTopper does not mark these; your child checks their own answers.

Is it a subscription?

No. You pay once. Nothing renews.

More questions
Is Basic really free?

Yes. Basic is free forever: study, practise and open papers without paying. Checked answers, your personal tutor and Mistake Intelligence come with the Board Pass.

What is the Board Pass?

The Board Pass is one payment that covers you until your first board paper. There are no renewals and nothing is charged automatically.

How is my answer marked?

Our marking follows the CBSE step-marking scheme: each step earns its marks, units and keywords count, and an error carried forward is not punished twice. It uses an AI language model set up to mark the way a CBSE examiner would, and every mark comes with the reason.

What happens when the founding offer ends?

From 1 December the Board Pass is ₹2,499 + GST. A pass you have already bought is never re-priced.

Are there any usage limits?

Basic has daily limits on opening tests and mocks. The Board Pass has generous fair use: your usage bars show what is left and reset every day and every 30 days. The Test Series has the same fair use. The 7-day trial has daily and weekly limits, listed under "What's free on Basic?".

What happens after the trial ends?

You keep using the free plan. Nothing is charged automatically; you choose when to upgrade.

Can I pay here?

Yes. Tap Pay, finish in Razorpay's secure checkout (UPI, card or netbanking), and the Board Pass turns on straight away.

Is this only for CBSE Class 10?

Currently yes. We're expanding to ICSE, State Boards, and Class 12 soon!
