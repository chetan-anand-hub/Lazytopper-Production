Frequently Asked Questions

Is Basic really free?
Yes. Basic keeps browse-first access and limited practice tools available without paid activation.
Should I pay monthly or till my boards?
Till boards is one payment that covers every month until your first board paper, at 20% less than paying monthly. The price shrinks each month as the boards get closer.
What happens after the first 200 students?
The founding offer closes. New members then join at the regular price — ₹999 / month, or 20% off when you pay once till your boards, which is what we publish today alongside the founding rate. Your rate is locked. Once you subscribe as a founding member you keep that rate for as long as your subscription stays active. We never change the price of an active subscription.
Are there any usage limits?
Basic includes limited practice and worksheet generation. Premium includes stronger practice and worksheet quotas, subject to fair use.
What happens after the trial ends?
You keep using the free plan. Nothing is charged automatically; you choose when to upgrade.
Can I pay here?
Not yet. Payment checkout is not connected in this build, so Premium activation stays manual.
Is this only for CBSE Class 10?
Currently yes. We're expanding to ICSE, State Boards, and Class 12 soon!
