What a Class 10 sample paper is

A sample paper is a full practice paper that follows the board paper: the same sections, the same kinds of questions and the same marks for each. CBSE publishes its own official sample papers and marking schemes on its website, and you should look at those too. The papers below are separate practice papers in the same pattern, made so you can sit more than one before the exam.

The CBSE 2026-27 pattern for Class 10 sets about 50% of the paper as competency-based questions, about 20% as MCQs and about 30% as short and long answers. These papers are drawn to that mix, and the table further down shows the competency-based marks of each one.

The pattern of every paper: 80 marks, Sections A to E

Every paper below is an 80-mark theory paper with the same shape. The Maths papers follow the Standard (041) pattern, and the Science papers use the same five sections.

How to take a sample paper online

  1. Pick a paper above and start it. Sit it in one go, without notes.
  2. Write your answers on paper, the way you would in the exam, with every step shown.
  3. Section A questions are marked on the answer alone, as in the real exam. For the written sections, Sections B–D (B–E in full-length tests), photograph your pages and upload them: your answers are marked step by step like a CBSE examiner, and each lost mark is sorted into a silly slip, a calculation error, a concept gap or a presentation miss.
  4. Fix the concept gaps first, then retake that chapter’s questions before the next paper.

Marking your written answers needs the Board Pass or the Test Series Pack. Common questions

Where can I find CBSE Class 10 sample papers for 2026-27?

CBSE publishes its own official sample papers and marking schemes on its website. LazyTopper adds 8 separate board-pattern practice papers for Maths and 8 for Science, each with the same sections and marks as the board paper.

What is the pattern of the Class 10 Maths sample paper?

Each Maths paper is 80 marks in five sections: Section A has 20 questions of 1 mark, Section B has 5 of 2 marks, Section C has 6 of 3 marks, Section D has 4 of 5 marks and Section E has 3 case-based questions of 4 marks. The Maths papers follow the Standard (041) pattern, and about half of each paper is competency-based.

Is the Science sample paper built the same way?

Yes. Each Science paper is also 80 marks across Sections A to E with the same number of questions and marks in each section, and about half of the paper is competency-based.

How is my sample paper marked?

Board answers are marked in steps, so the formula, the working, the final answer and the unit can each earn marks. Photograph your written answers and the paper is marked step by step like a CBSE examiner, with the lost marks shown. Section A questions score full marks or zero on the answer alone.

Can I print the sample papers?

Yes. The Mock Pack is a printable PDF of 8 board-pattern papers for one subject, each with a marking guide. The Maths + Science Bundle adds every chapter question bank and both subjects' papers.

Is this the real 2027 board paper?

No. A sample paper is practice in the board pattern, not the actual paper. LazyTopper is an independent platform and is not affiliated with CBSE.
