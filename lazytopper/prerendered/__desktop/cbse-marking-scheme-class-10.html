CBSE Class 10 Marking Scheme 2027 — how step marks are given

Class 10 answers are marked in steps, not only on the last line. Here is what each section carries and where step marks are earned and lost.

The paper pattern and marks by section

The CBSE 2026-27 Class 10 paper pattern sets about 50% of the paper as competency-based questions, about 20% as MCQs and about 30% as short and long answers. The questions fall into five sections, and each section carries a fixed number of marks per question.

How step marking works

A Section A question is a 1-mark item: the answer is right or it is not, and an MCQ scores full marks or zero on the answer alone, with no step marks. Beyond Section A, the marking scheme breaks an answer into steps, and each step carries part of the marks:

  • Section B, 2 marks: the formula or approach, then the substitution or working, then the answer with its unit.
  • Section C, 3 marks: the given data and formula, the substitution, the intermediate working and the final answer with its unit.
  • Section D, 5 marks: set the problem up, formulate it, simplify, solve and conclude with units.
  • Section E, 4 marks: a case study with parts. The first two parts earn a mark each, and the last part earns one mark for the working and one for the final answer.

Half-mark steps are real. Writing the correct formula alone can earn half a mark even when the rest of the answer goes wrong.

Error carried forward

A mistake is charged once. If a step has a calculation error but the steps after it follow the correct method from that wrong value, those later steps still earn their marks. So a slip early in a long answer does not wipe out the whole answer, provided your working is on the page for the examiner to follow.

Units, keywords, equations and diagrams

  • Units. A final numerical answer without its SI unit loses half a mark.
  • Science keywords. Examiners look for the exact CBSE technical term: oesophagus, not “food pipe”.
  • Chemical equations. A balanced equation earns half a mark, and the state symbols or the catalyst earn another half mark.
  • Diagrams. The accuracy of the drawing and its labels are marked separately, so a correct diagram with missing labels still loses marks.

Writing an answer that earns every step

  1. Start with the formula or the method, because that step can earn marks on its own.
  2. Show the substitution and the working line by line, so each step is on the page to be credited.
  3. Write the final answer with its SI unit.
  4. In Science, use the exact NCERT term, balance every equation and label every diagram.
  5. If you spot a slip, carry on with the right method from the value you have; later steps can still earn marks.

Examiners can only credit what is written, so a correct answer given as a bare final line leaves the step marks with nothing to attach to.

Why you lost the marks: four kinds of mistake

Knowing how marks are given also tells you what to fix. A lost mark usually has one of four causes:

  • a silly slip, such as copying a number wrongly from your own earlier line;
  • a calculation error, such as wrong arithmetic or a split that does not add up;
  • a concept you have not understood, where the method itself is wrong;
  • presentation only, such as a missing unit, label or state symbol.

If your later steps show that you know the method, the mistake is not a concept gap. Sorting your lost marks this way tells you whether to revise the chapter or just slow down and write the unit.

Common questions

How are CBSE Class 10 answers marked?

Every answer is marked against value points. A 1-mark MCQ is right or wrong on the answer alone, while a 2, 3 or 5-mark answer earns marks step by step: the formula, the working and the final answer are credited separately.

What is error carried forward in CBSE marking?

If a step contains a calculation error but the later steps follow the correct method from that wrong value, the later steps still earn their marks. A mistake is charged once.

Do I lose marks for a missing unit?

Yes. A final numerical answer without its SI unit loses half a mark.

Are diagrams and chemical equations marked separately?

Yes. In Science a diagram's accuracy and its labels are marked separately, and a balanced chemical equation and its state symbols or catalyst can each earn half a mark.
