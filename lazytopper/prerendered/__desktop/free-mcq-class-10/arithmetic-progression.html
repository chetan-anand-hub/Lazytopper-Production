Arithmetic Progressions MCQs

  1. Q1

    The 8th term of an AP is 17 and its 14th term is 29. The common difference of the AP is:
    • (a)3
    • (b)2
    • (c)5
    • (d)-2
    Show answer

    Answer: (b) 2

    a+7d=17 and a+13d=29. Subtracting, 6d=12, so d=2.

  2. Q2

    Choose the correct answer from the given four options: If the 2nd term of an AP is 13 and the 5th term is 25, what is its 7th term?
    • (a)30
    • (b)33
    • (c)37
    • (d)38
    Show answer

    Answer: (b) 33

    a+d=13 and a+4d=25 give d=4, a=9. Then a7​=9+6×4=33.

  3. Q3

    Mark the correct alternative in the following: If the sum of first n even natural numbers is equal to k times the sum of first n odd natural numbers, then k =
    • (a)n1​
    • (b)nn−1​
    • (c)2nn+1​
    • (d)nn+1​
    Show answer

    Answer: (d) nn+1​

    Sum of first n even numbers =n(n+1); sum of first n odd numbers =n2. So k=n2n(n+1)​=nn+1​.

  4. Q4

    Mark the correct alternative in the following: If Sr​ denotes the sum of the first r terms of an A.P. Then, S3n​:(S2n​−Sn​) is:
    • (a)n
    • (b)3n
    • (c)3
    • (d)None of these.
    Show answer

    Answer: (c) 3

    Using Sn​=2n​[2a+(n−1)d], we get S2n​−Sn​=2n​[2a+(3n−1)d] and S3n​=23n​[2a+(3n−1)d]. The ratio is 3.

  5. Q5

    Mark the correct alternative in the following: Two A.P.'s have the same common difference. The first term of one of these is 8 and that of the other is 3. The difference between their 30th term is:
    • (a)11
    • (b)3
    • (c)8
    • (d)5
    Show answer

    Answer: (d) 5

    With the same d, A30​−a30​=(8+29d)−(3+29d)=5.

  6. Q6

    Choose the correct answer from the given four options: The 21st term of the AP whose first two terms are –3 and 4 is:
    • (a)17
    • (b)137
    • (c)143
    • (d)-143
    Show answer

    Answer: (b) 137

    d=4−(−3)=7. So a21​=−3+20×7=137.

  7. Q7

    The 7th term of an AP is -1 its 16th term is 17. The nth term of the AP is:
    • (a)(3n + 8)
    • (b)(4n - 7)
    • (c)(15 - 2n)
    • (d)(2n - 15)
    Show answer

    Answer: (d) (2n - 15)

    a+6d=−1 and a+15d=17 give d=2, a=−13. So an​=−13+(n−1)2=2n−15.

  8. Q8

    If the common difference of an A.P. is 3, then a20​−a15​ is:
    • (a)5
    • (b)3
    • (c)15
    • (d)20
    Show answer

    Answer: (c) 15

    a20​−a15​=5d=5×3=15.
