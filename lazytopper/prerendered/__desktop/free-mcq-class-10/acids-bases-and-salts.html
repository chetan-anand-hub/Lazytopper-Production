Acids, Bases and Salts MCQs

  1. Q1

    If you take some distilled water in a test-tube, add an equal amount of acetic acid to it, shake the test-tube well and leave it undisturbed on the test-tube stand, then after about 5 minutes, what would you observe?
    • (a)There is a layer of water over the layer of acetic acid.
    • (b)A precipitate is settling at the bottom of the test-tube.
    • (c)Bubbles of colourless gas are coming out of the test-tube.
    • (d)There is a clear, colourless transparent solution in the test-tube.
    Show answer

    Answer: (d) There is a clear, colourless transparent solution in the test-tube.

    Acetic acid is miscible with water, so it dissolves readily and forms a homogeneous, clear, colourless solution.

  2. Q2

    Match the chemical substances given in Column (A) with their appropriate application given in Column (B):
    Column (A)Column (B)
    A. Bleaching powder1. Preparation of glass
    B. Baking soda2. Production of H2​ and Cl2​
    C. Washing soda3. Decolourisation
    D. Sodium chloride4. Antacid
    • (a)A—2, B—1, C—4, D—3
    • (b)A—3, B—2, C—4, D—1
    • (c)A—3, B—4, C—1, D—2
    • (d)A—2, B—4, C—1, D—3
    Show answer

    Answer: (c) A—3, B—4, C—1, D—2

    Bleaching powder is used for decolourisation; baking soda as an antacid; washing soda in preparing glass; sodium chloride for producing H2​ and Cl2​.

  3. Q3

    Calcium phosphate is present in tooth enamel. Its nature is:
    • (a)Basic.
    • (b)Acidic.
    • (c)Neutral.
    • (d)Amphoteric.
    Show answer

    Answer: (a) Basic.

    Calcium phosphate Ca3​(PO4​)2​ is formed from calcium hydroxide (strong base) and phosphoric acid (weak acid), so it is basic.

  4. Q4

    You are having five solutions A, B, C, D and E with pH values as follows: A = 1.8, B = 7, C = 8.5, D = 8 and E = 5 Which solution would be most likely to liberate hydrogen with magnesium powder?
    • (a)Solution A and B.
    • (b)Solution A.
    • (c)Solution C.
    • (d)All of the above.
    Show answer

    Answer: (b) Solution A.

  5. Q5

    A solution turns phenolphthalein indicator pink. The most likely pH of this solution will be:
    • (a)6
    • (b)4
    • (c)9
    • (d)7
    Show answer

    Answer: (c) 9

    A solution with pH greater than 7 is basic, and basic solutions turn phenolphthalein pink. So the pH is most likely 9.
