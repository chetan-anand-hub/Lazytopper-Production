  1. Q1

    The length of the tangent from an external point P to a circle of radius 5cm is 10cm. The distance of the point from the centre of the circle is:
    • (a)8cm
    • (b)104​cm
    • (c)12cm
    • (d)125​cm
    Show answer

    Answer: (d) 125​cm

    The tangent is perpendicular to the radius, so OP2=PT2+OT2=100+25=125 and OP=125​ cm.

  2. Q2

    From a point P which is at a distance of 13cm from the centre O of a circle of radius 5cm, the pair of tangents PQ and PR to the circle are drawn. Then the area of the quadrilateral PQOR is:
    • (a)60cm2
    • (b)65cm2
    • (c)30cm2
    • (d)32.5cm2
    Show answer

    Answer: (a) 60cm2

    PQ=132−52​=12 cm. The quadrilateral is two right triangles: 2×21​×12×5=60 cm2.

  3. Q8

    In a right triangle ABC, right-angled at B, BC = 12cm and AB = 5cm. The radius of the circle inscribed in the triangle (in cm) is:
    • (a)4
    • (b)3
    • (c)2
    • (d)1
    Show answer

    Answer: (c) 2

    AC=13 cm. For a right triangle, r=2AB+BC−AC​=25+12−13​=2 cm.
