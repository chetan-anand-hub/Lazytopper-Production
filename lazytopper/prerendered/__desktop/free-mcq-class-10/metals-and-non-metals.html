Metals and Non-metals MCQs

  1. Q1

    Alloys are homogeneous mixtures of a metal with a metal or non-metal. Which among the following alloys contain non-metal as one of its constituents?
    • (a)Brass.
    • (b)Bronze.
    • (c)Amalgam.
    • (d)Steel.
    Show answer

    Answer: (d) Steel.

    Steel contains iron (a metal) and carbon (a non-metal). Brass is copper and zinc, bronze is copper and tin, and amalgam is mercury with another metal.

  2. Q2

    The least reactive metal among the following is:
    • (a)Sodium.
    • (b)Silver.
    • (c)Copper.
    • (d)Lead.
    Show answer

    Answer: (b) Silver.

    Silver lies below the other metals in the reactivity series, so it is the least reactive.

  3. Q3

    A student puts one big iron nail each in four test tubes containing solutions of zinc sulphate, aluminium sulphate, copper sulphate and iron sulphate. A reddish brown coating was observed only on the surface of iron nail which was put in the solution of:
    • (a)Zinc sulphate.
    • (b)Iron sulphate.
    • (c)Copper sulphate.
    • (d)Aluminium sulphate.
    Show answer

    Answer: (c) Copper sulphate.

    Iron is more reactive than copper, so it displaces copper from copper sulphate solution. The reddish brown copper deposits on the nail.

  4. Q4

    An element A is soft and can be cut with a knife. This is very reactive to air and cannot be kept open in air. It reacts vigorously with water. Identify the element from the following.
    • (a)Mg
    • (b)Na
    • (c)P
    • (d)Ca
    Show answer

    Answer: (b) Na

    Sodium is a soft metal that can be cut with a knife and is highly reactive with air and water.

  5. Q5

    The molecules having triple bond in them are:
    • (a)Oxygen and ethyne.
    • (b)Carbon dioxide and ammonia.
    • (c)Methane and ethene.
    • (d)Nitrogen and ethyne.
    Show answer

    Answer: (d) Nitrogen and ethyne.

    In a nitrogen molecule the two nitrogen atoms share 3 electrons each to form a triple bond. In ethyne the two carbon atoms are also joined by a triple bond.

  6. Q6

    The elements whose oxides can turn phenolphthalein solution pink are:
    • (a)Na and K.
    • (b)K and C.
    • (c)Na and S.
    • (d)K and P.
    Show answer

    Answer: (a) Na and K.

    Na and K are metals whose oxides are basic, and basic oxides turn phenolphthalein pink.
