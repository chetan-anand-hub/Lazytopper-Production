Polynomials MCQs

  1. Q1

    The sum and product of the zeros of a quadratic polynomial are 3 and -10 respectively. The quadratic polynomial is:
    • (a)x2−3x+10
    • (b)x2+3x−10
    • (c)x2−3x−10
    • (d)x2+3x+10
    Show answer

    Answer: (c) x2−3x−10

    A quadratic with zeros α,β is x2−(α+β)x+αβ=x2−3x−10.

  2. Q2

    If α,β are the zeros of the polynomial x2+6x+2, then (α1​+β1​)=?
    • (a)3
    • (b)-3
    • (c)12
    • (d)-12
    Show answer

    Answer: (b) -3

    α+β=−6 and αβ=2. So α1​+β1​=αβα+β​=2−6​=−3.

  3. Q3

    If one zero of 3x2+8x+k be the reciprocal of the other, then k=?
    • (a)3
    • (b)−3
    • (c)31​
    • (d)3−1​
    Show answer

    Answer: (a) 3

    Zeros α and α1​ have product 3k​. Since the product is 1, k=3.

  4. Q4

    If α,β are the zeros of the polynomial p(x)=4x2+3x+7, then α1​+β1​ is equal to:
    • (a)37​
    • (b)3−7​
    • (c)73​
    • (d)7−3​
    Show answer

    Answer: (d) 7−3​

    α+β=−43​ and αβ=47​. So α1​+β1​=αβα+β​=−73​.

  5. Q5

    A quadratic polynomial, the sum of whose zeroes is 0 and one zero is 3, is:
    • (a)x2−9
    • (b)x2+9
    • (c)x2+3
    • (d)x2−3
    Show answer

    Answer: (a) x2−9

    The other zero is −3, so sum =0 and product =−9. The polynomial is x2−0⋅x−9=x2−9.
