Chemical Reactions and Equations MCQs

  1. Q1

    Which of the following gases can be used for storage of fresh sample of an oil for a long time?
    • (a)Carbon dioxide or oxygen.
    • (b)Nitrogen or oxygen.
    • (c)Carbon dioxide or helium.
    • (d)Helium or nitrogen.
    Show answer

    Answer: (d) Helium or nitrogen.

    Inert gases like helium and nitrogen prevent oxidation (rancidity), so they can be used to store food items such as oil.

  2. Q2

    In a double displacement reaction such as the reaction between sodium sulphate solution and barium chloride solution: 1. Exchange of atoms takes place. 2. Exchange of ions takes place. 3. A precipitate is produced. 4. An insoluble salt is produced. The correct option is:
    • (a)(B) and (D).
    • (b)(A) and (C).
    • (c)Only (B).
    • (d)(B), (C) and (D).
    Show answer

    Answer: (d) (B), (C) and (D).

    Sodium sulphate and barium chloride exchange ions to form insoluble barium sulphate (a precipitate) and sodium chloride: Na2​SO4​(aq)+BaCl2​(aq)→BaSO4​(s)↓+2NaCl(aq).

  3. Q4

    One of the following is an exothermic reaction. This is:
    • (a)Electrolysis of water.
    • (b)Conversion of limestone into quicklime.
    • (c)Process of respiration.
    • (d)Process of photosynthesis.
    Show answer

    Answer: (c) Process of respiration.

    Respiration releases heat energy that maintains body temperature, so it is exothermic.

  4. Q5

    Which among the following is (are) double displacement reaction(s)? 1. Pb+CuCl2​→PbCl2​+Cu 2. Na2​SO4​+BaCl2​→BaSO4​+2NaCl 3. C+O2​→CO2​ 4. CH4​+2O2​→CO2​+2H2​O
    • (a)1 and 4
    • (b)2 only
    • (c)1 and 2
    • (d)3 and 4
    Show answer

    Answer: (b) 2 only

    In reaction 2, sodium and barium displace each other from their salts (exchange of ions), so it is a double displacement reaction.

  5. Q6

    The following reaction is an example of a: 4NH3​(g)+5O2​(g)→4NO(g)+6H2​O(g) 1. Displacement reaction. 2. Combination reaction. 3. Redox reaction. 4. Neutralisation reaction.
    • (a)1 and 4
    • (b)2 and 3
    • (c)1 and 3
    • (d)3 and 4
    Show answer

    Answer: (c) 1 and 3

    A redox reaction involves oxidation and reduction together. The source also treats it as a displacement reaction, as H atoms of NH3​ are displaced by oxygen.

  6. Q7

    Which of the following statements about the given reaction are correct? 3Fe(s)+4H2​O(g)→Fe3​O4​(s)+4H2​(g) 1. Iron metal is getting oxidised. 2. Water is getting reduced. 3. Water is acting as reducing agent. 4. Water is acting as oxidising agent.
    • (a)1, 2 and 3
    • (b)3 and 4
    • (c)1, 2 and 4
    • (d)2 and 4
    Show answer

    Answer: (c) 1, 2 and 4

    Oxygen is added to iron, so iron is oxidised. Oxygen is removed from water, so water is reduced and acts as the oxidising agent.

  7. Q8

    Which one of the following involves a chemical reaction?
    • (a)Evaporation of water.
    • (b)Storing of nitrogen gas under pressure.
    • (c)Keeping petrol in a China dish in open.
    • (d)Heating magnesium wire in the presence of air at high temperature.
    Show answer

    Answer: (d) Heating magnesium wire in the presence of air at high temperature.
