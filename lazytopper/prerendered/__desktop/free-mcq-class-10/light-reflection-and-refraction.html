Light: Reflection and Refraction MCQs

  1. Q1

    One of the following does not apply to a concave mirror. This is:
    • (a)Focal length is negative.
    • (b)Image distance can be positive or negative.
    • (c)Image distance is always positive.
    • (d)Height of image can be positive or negative.
    Show answer

    Answer: (c) Image distance is always positive.

    Image distance is positive only in one case: when the object is between the pole and the focus. In other cases the image forms in front of the mirror, so it is not always positive.

    • (a)-4cm
    • (b)-400mm
    • (c)-4m

  3. Q3

    Where should an object be placed in front of a convex lens to get a real image of the size of the object?
    • (a)At the principal focus of the lens.
    • (b)At twice the focal length.
    • (c)At infinity.
    • (d)Between the optical centre of the lens and its principal focus.
    Show answer

    Answer: (b) At twice the focal length.

  4. Q4

    If the focal length of a spherical mirror is 12.5cm, its radius of curvature will be:
    • (a)25cm
    • (b)15cm
    • (c)20cm
    • (d)35cm
    Show answer

    Answer: (a) 25cm

    Radius of curvature R=2f=2×12.5=25 cm.

  5. Q5

    Magnification produced by a plane mirror is:
    • (a)Less than one.
    • (b)Greater than one.
    • (c)Zero.
    • (d)Equal to one.
    Show answer

    Answer: (d) Equal to one.

    The image formed by a plane mirror is the same size as the object, so the magnification is equal to one.

  6. Q6

    The image formed by a concave mirror is real, inverted and highly diminished (much smaller than the object). The object must be:
    • (a)Between pole and focus.
    • (b)At focus.
    • (c)At the centre of curvature.
    • (d)At infinity.
    Show answer

    Answer: (d) At infinity.

    A real, inverted and highly diminished image is formed by a concave mirror only when the object is at infinity.
