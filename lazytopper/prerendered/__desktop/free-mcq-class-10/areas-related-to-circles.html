Areas Related to Circles MCQs

  1. Q1

    The area of a sector whose perimeter is four times its radius r units, is:
    • (a)4r2​ sq. units
    • (b)2r2 sq. units
    • (c)r2 sq. units
    • (d)2r2​ sq. units
    Show answer

    Answer: (c) r2 sq. units

    Perimeter =2r+l=4r, so arc length l=2r. Area =21​lr=21​(2r)(r)=r2.

  2. Q3

    If the area of a sector of a circle bounded by an arc of length 5πcm is equal to 20πcm2, then its radius is:
    • (a)12cm
    • (b)16cm
    • (c)8cm
    • (d)10cm
    Show answer

    Answer: (c) 8cm

    Area of sector =21​lr: 20π=21​(5π)r, so r=8 cm.

  3. Q4

    A chord of a circle of radius 10cm subtends a right angle at the centre. The area of the minor segments (given, π=3.14) is:
    • (a)32.5cm2
    • (b)34.5cm2
    • (c)28.5cm2
    • (d)30.5cm2
    Show answer

    Answer: (c) 28.5cm2

    Minor segment = sector − triangle =41​(3.14)(100)−21​(10)(10)=78.5−50=28.5 cm2.

  4. Q5

    If a chord of a circle of radius 28cm makes an angle of 90° at the centre, then the area of the major segment is:
    • (a)392cm2
    • (b)1456cm2
    • (c)1848cm2
    • (d)2240cm2
    Show answer

    Answer: (d) 2240cm2

    Major segment = circle − minor segment =2464−(616−392)=2240 cm2 (using π=722​).

  5. Q6

    If the area of a sector of a circle is 185​ of the area of the circle, then the sector angle is equal to:
    • (a)60º
    • (b)90º
    • (c)100º
    • (d)120º
    Show answer

    Answer: (c) 100º

    360∘θ​=185​, so θ=100∘.

  6. Q8

    If the perimeter of a sector of a circle of radius 6.5cm is 29cm, then its area is:
    • (a)58cm2
    • (b)52cm2
    • (c)25cm2
    • (d)56cm2
    Show answer

    Answer: (b) 52cm2

    Arc length =29−2(6.5)=16 cm. Area =21​lr=21​(16)(6.5)=52 cm2.
