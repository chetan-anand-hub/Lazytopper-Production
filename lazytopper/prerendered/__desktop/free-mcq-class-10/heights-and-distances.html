  1. Q1

    The length of shadow of a tower on the plane ground is 3​ times the height of the tower. The angle of elevation of sun is:
    • (a)45°
    • (b)30°
    • (c)60°
    • (d)90°
    Show answer

    Answer: (b) 30°

    tanθ=shadowheight​=3​1​, so θ=30∘.

    • (d)75°

    The shadow of a 5-m-long stick is 2m long. At the same time the length of the shadow of a 12.5-m-high tree is:
    • (a)3m
    • (b)3.5m

    The angle of depression of a car parked on the road from the top of a 150m high tower is 30°. The distance of the car from the tower (in metres) is:
    • (a)503​

  5. Q5

    If a 1.5m tall girl stands at a distance of 3m from a lamp-post and casts a shadow of length 4.5m on the ground, then the height of the lamp-post is:
    • (a)1.5m
    • (b)2m
    • (c)2.5m
    • (d)2.8m
    Show answer

    Answer: (c) 2.5m

    By similar triangles, 3+4.5H​=4.51.5​, so H=2.5 m.

  6. Q6

    If the angles of elevation of a tower from two points distant a and b (a > b) from its foot and in the same straight line from it are 30° and 60°, then the height of the tower is:
    • (a)a+b​
    • (b)ab​
    • (c)a−b​
    • (d)ba​​
    Show answer

    Answer: (b) ab​

    h=atan30∘ and h=btan60∘. Multiplying, h2=abtan30∘tan60∘=ab, so h=ab​.

  7. Q7

    If the angles of elevation of the top of a tower from two points at distances a and b from the base and in the same straight line with it are complementary, then the height of the tower is:
    • (a)ba​​
    • (b)ab​
    • (c)a+b​
    • (d)a−b​
    Show answer

    Answer: (b) ab​

    With complementary angles θ and 90∘−θ: tanθ=ah​ and cotθ=bh​. Multiplying, h2=ab, so h=ab​.
