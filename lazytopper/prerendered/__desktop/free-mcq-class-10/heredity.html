Heredity MCQs

  1. Q1

    Which statement is true for a dominant allele?
    • (a)It cannot undergo mutation.
    • (b)It gives a greater chance of survival than a recessive allele.
    • (c)It gives the same phenotype in heterozygotes and homozygotes.
    • (d)It is only responsible for male characteristics.
    Show answer

    Answer: (c) It gives the same phenotype in heterozygotes and homozygotes.

    A dominant allele expresses itself in the heterozygous condition too. For example TT and Tt both show the tall phenotype.

  2. Q2

    A recessive homozygote is crossed with a heterozygote of the same gene. What will be the phenotype of the F1​ - generation?
    • (a)All dominant.
    • (b)75% dominant, 25% recessive.
    • (c)50% dominant, 50% recessive.
    • (d)25% dominant, 50% heterozygous, 25% recessive.
    Show answer

    Answer: (c) 50% dominant, 50% recessive.

    The cross is Tt×tt, giving Tt and tt in the ratio 1 : 1, so 50% dominant and 50% recessive.

  3. Q3

    A zygote which has inherited an X chromosome from the father will develop into:
    • (a)Baby boy.
    • (b)Baby girl.
    • (c)Adult.
    • (d)Either boy or girl.
    Show answer

    Answer: (b) Baby girl.

    The mother always gives an X chromosome. A zygote receiving an X from the father is XX and develops into a baby girl.

  4. Q4

    A cross between two individuals results in a ratio of 9 : 3 : 3 : 1 for four possible phenotypes of progeny. This is an example of a:
    • (a)Dihybrid cross.
    • (b)Monohybrid cross.
    • (c)Test cross.
    • (d)None of these.
    Show answer

    Answer: (a) Dihybrid cross.

    A dihybrid cross gives progeny in the ratio 9 : 3 : 3 : 1.
