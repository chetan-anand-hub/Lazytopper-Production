Magnetic Effects of Electric Current MCQs

  1. Q1

    The force experienced by a current-carrying conductor placed in a magnetic field is the largest when the angle between the conductor and the magnetic field is:
    • (a)45°
    • (b)60°
    • (c)90°
    • (d)180°
    Show answer

    Answer: (c) 90°

    The force on a current-carrying conductor is largest when the conductor is at 90° to the magnetic field.

  2. Q2

    An AC source has a frequency of 50Hz. The number of times, its direction changes in one second is:
    • (a)25
    • (b)50
    • (c)75
    • (d)100
    Show answer

    Answer: (d) 100

    A frequency of 50 Hz means 50 cycles per second. The current reverses direction twice in each cycle, so 100 times per second.

  3. Q5

    If the direction of electric current in a solenoid when viewed from a particular end is anticlockwise, then this end of solenoid will be:
    • (a)West pole.
    • (b)South pole.
    • (c)North pole.
    • (d)East pole.
    Show answer

    Answer: (c) North pole.

    A solenoid end where the current appears anticlockwise behaves as a North pole.

  4. Q6

    Circuit Breaker Device which can be used in place of fuse in domestic electric wiring is called:
    • (a)CBD.
    • (b)DCB.
    • (c)MCD.
    • (d)MCB.
    Show answer

    Answer: (d) MCB.

    Miniature circuit breakers (MCB) are used in domestic wiring in place of fuses.
