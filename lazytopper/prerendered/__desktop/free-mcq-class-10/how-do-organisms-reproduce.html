How do Organisms Reproduce MCQs

  1. Q1

    A student identified the various parts of an embryo of a gram seed and listed them as given below: 1. Testa. 2. Plumule. 3. Radicle. 4. Cotyledon. 5. Tegman. Out of these the actual parts of the embryo are:
    • (a)I, II, III.
    • (b)II, III, IV.
    • (c)III, IV, V.
    • (d)II, IV, V.
    Show answer

    Answer: (b) II, III, IV.

    The embryo of a gram seed consists of plumule, radicle and cotyledon. Testa and tegmen are parts of the seed coat.

  2. Q2

    A student was asked to observe and identify the various parts of an embryo of a red kidney bean seed. He identified the parts and listed them as under: 1. Tegmen. 2. Testa. 3. Cotyledon. 4. Radicle. 5. Plumule. The correctly identified parts among these are:
    • (a)I, II and III.
    • (b)II, III and IV.
    • (c)III, IV and V.
    • (d)I, III, IV and V.
    Show answer

    Answer: (c) III, IV and V.

    The cotyledon, radicle and plumule are the parts of the embryo. Tegmen and testa are parts of the seed coat.

  3. Q3

    During adolescence, several changes occur in the human body. Mark one change from the following associated with sexual maturation in boys:
    • (a)Loss of milk teeth.
    • (b)Increase in height.
    • (c)Cracking of voice.
    • (d)Weight gain.
    Show answer

    Answer: (c) Cracking of voice.

    Cracking of voice in males is brought about by the male hormone testosterone, produced after sexual maturation.

  4. Q4

    The number of chromosomes in parents and offsprings of a particular species remains constant due to:
    • (a)Doubling of chromosomes after zygote formation.
    • (b)Halving of chromosomes during gamete formation.
    • (c)Doubling of chromosomes after gamete formation.
    • (d)Halving of chromosomes after gamete formation.
    Show answer

    Answer: (b) Halving of chromosomes during gamete formation.

    During gamete formation the chromosome number is halved, so the zygote has the same number as the parent's body cells. This keeps the number constant in a species.

  5. Q5

    The disease kala-azar is caused by a micro-organism known as:
    • (a)Planaria.
    • (b)Leech.
    • (c)Leishmania.
    • (d)Plasmodium.
    Show answer

    Answer: (c) Leishmania.

    Leishmania is a protozoan that causes kala-azar.

  6. Q6

    Vegetative propagation refers to formation of new plants from:
    • (a)Stem, roots and flowers.
    • (b)Stem, roots and leaves.
    • (c)Stem, flowers and fruits.
    • (d)Stem, leaves and flowers.
    Show answer

    Answer: (b) Stem, roots and leaves.

    Vegetative propagation is the formation of new plants from stems, roots and leaves.

  7. Q7

    An organism which may be considered to be a kind of plant and reproduces by budding is:
    • (a)Paramecium.
    • (b)Bread mould.
    • (c)Hydra.
    • (d)Yeast.
    Show answer

    Answer: (d) Yeast.

    Yeast is a fungus, considered a non-green plant, and it reproduces by budding.
