Surface Areas and Volumes MCQs

  1. Q1

    The curved surface area of a right circular cone of height 15cm and base diameter 16cm is:
    • (a)60πcm2
    • (b)68πcm2
    • (c)120πcm2
    • (d)136πcm2
    Show answer

    Answer: (d) 136πcm2

    r=8 cm, l=82+152​=17 cm. CSA =πrl=π(8)(17)=136π cm2.

  2. Q2

    The length of the diagonal of a cube is 63​cm. Its total surface area is:
    • (a)144 cm2
    • (b)216 cm2
    • (c)180 cm2
    • (d)108 cm2
    Show answer

    Answer: (b) 216 cm2

    Diagonal =3​x=63​, so edge x=6 cm. TSA =6x2=216 cm2.

  3. Q3

    How many bags of grain can be stored in a cuboidal granary (8m × 6m × 3m), if each bag occupies a space of 0.64m3?
    • (a)8256
    • (b)90
    • (c)212
    • (d)225
    Show answer

    Answer: (d) 225

    Volume =8×6×3=144 m3. Number of bags =0.64144​=225.

  4. Q4

    A right triangle with sides 3cm, 4cm and 5cm is rotated about the side of 3cm to form a cone. The volume of the cone so formed is:
    • (a)12πcm3
    • (b)15πcm3
    • (c)16πcm3
    • (d)20πcm3
    Show answer

    Answer: (c) 16πcm3

    Rotating about the 3 cm side gives height 3 cm and radius 4 cm. V=31​πr2h=31​π(16)(3)=16π cm3.

  5. Q5

    A sphere of diameter 18cm is dropped into a cylindrical vessel of diameter 36cm, partly filled with water. If the sphere is completely submerged, then the water level rises by:
    • (a)3cm
    • (b)4cm
    • (c)5cm
    • (d)6cm
    Show answer

    Answer: (a) 3cm

    Volume of sphere =34​π(9)3=972π. This equals π(18)2h, so h=324972​=3 cm.

  6. Q6

    A solid is hemispherical at the bottom and conical (of same radius) above it. If the surface areas of the two parts are equal, then the ratio of its radius and the slant height of the conical part is:
    • (a)1 : 2
    • (b)2 : 1
    • (c)1 : 4
    • (d)4 : 1
    Show answer

    Answer: (a) 1 : 2

    Hemisphere curved area 2πr2 equals cone curved area πrl, so l=2r and r:l=1:2.

  7. Q7

    The surface area of a sphere is 154 cm2. The volume of the sphere is:
    • (a)17932​cm3
    • (b)35931​cm3
    • (c)143731​cm3
    • (d)None of these
    Show answer

    Answer: (a) 17932​cm3

    4πr2=154 gives r=3.5 cm. V=34​⋅722​⋅(3.5)3=17932​ cm3.

  8. Q8

    Choose the correct answer from the given four options: A right circular cylinder of radius r cm and height h cm (h > 2r) just encloses a sphere of diameter:
    • (a)r cm
    • (b)2r cm
    • (c)h cm
    • (d)2h cm
    Show answer

    Answer: (b) 2r cm

    A sphere just enclosed by the cylinder touches its curved surface, so its diameter equals the cylinder's diameter, 2r cm (since h>2r).
