Life Processes MCQs

  1. Q1

    The internal energy (cellular energy) reserve in autotrophs is:
    • (a)Proteins.
    • (b)Fatty acids.
    • (c)Glycogen.
    • (d)Starch.
    Show answer

    Answer: (d) Starch.

    Autotrophs such as plants store energy in the form of starch.

  2. Q2

    Which of the following statements about the autotrophs is incorrect?
    • (a)They synthesise carbohydrates from carbon dioxide and water in the presence of sunlight and chlorophyll.
    • (b)They store carbohydrates in the form of starch.
    • (c)They convert carbon dioxide and water into carbohydrates in the absence of sunlight.
    • (d)They constitute the first trophic level in food chains.
    Show answer

    Answer: (c) They convert carbon dioxide and water into carbohydrates in the absence of sunlight.

    Autotrophs combine carbon dioxide and water to form carbohydrates using sunlight, trapped by chlorophyll. They cannot do this in the absence of sunlight, so statement 3 is incorrect.

  4. Q4

    The value of normal systolic and diastolic blood pressure in human is:
    • (a)120mm Hg and 80mm Hg, respectively.
    • (b)80mm Hg and 120mm Hg, respectively.
    • (c)100mm Hg and 90mm Hg, respectively.
    • (d)130mm Hg and 85mm Hg, respectively.
    Show answer

    Answer: (a) 120mm Hg and 80mm Hg, respectively.

    Normal blood pressure is about 120 mm Hg (systolic, the top number) over 80 mm Hg (diastolic, the bottom number).

  5. Q5

    Which of the following component of our food is digested by an enzyme which is present in saliva as well as in pancreatic juice?
    • (a)Proteins.
    • (b)Fat.
    • (c)Minerals.
    • (d)Carbohydrate.
    Show answer

    Answer: (d) Carbohydrate.

    Carbohydrates are digested by amylase, which is present in both saliva and pancreatic juice.

  6. Q6

    Which of the following is the only conducting tissue in non - flowering plants?
    • (a)Xylem vessels.
    • (b)Sieve tubes.
    • (c)Companion cells.
    • (d)Tracheids.
    Show answer

    Answer: (d) Tracheids.

    Tracheids are the only conducting tissue in non-flowering plants.

  7. Q7

    The instrument for measuring blood pressure is called:
    • (a)Manometer.
    • (b)Sphygmomanometer.
    • (c)Barometer.
    • (d)Potentiometer.
    Show answer

    Answer: (b) Sphygmomanometer.

    The device used for measuring blood pressure is called a sphygmomanometer.
