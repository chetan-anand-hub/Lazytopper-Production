Probability MCQs

  1. Q1

    A box contains cards numbered 6 to 50. A card is drawn at random from the box. The probability that the drawn card has a number which is a perfect square is:
    • (a)451​
    • (b)152​
    • (c)454​
    • (d)91​
    Show answer

    Answer: (d) 91​

    Cards 6 to 50 number 45. Perfect squares are 9, 16, 25, 36, 49: five of them. Probability =455​=91​.

  2. Q2

    A card is drawn at random from a pack of 52 cards. The probability that the drawn card is not an ace is:
    • (a)131​
    • (b)139​
    • (c)134​
    • (d)1312​
    Show answer

    Answer: (d) 1312​

    There are 4 aces, so P(not ace)=5248​=1312​.

  3. Q3

    A box contains 3 blue, 2 white and 4 red marbles. If a marble is drawn at random from the box, what is the probability that it will not be a white marble?
    • (a)31​
    • (b)94​
    • (c)97​
    • (d)92​
    Show answer

    Answer: (c) 97​

    There are 9 marbles, 2 white. P(not white)=97​.

  4. Q4

    In a lottery, there are 8 prizes and 16 blanks. What is the probability of getting a prize?
    • (a)21​
    • (b)31​
    • (c)32​
    • (d)None of these
    Show answer

    Answer: (b) 31​

    Total tickets =24. P(prize)=248​=31​.

  5. Q5

    The probability of a certain event is:
    • (a)0
    • (b)1
    • (c)21​
    • (d)No existent.
    Show answer

    Answer: (b) 1

    A sure (certain) event always happens, so its probability is 1.

  6. Q6

    Cards bearing numbers 2, 3, 4, ...., 11 are kept in a bag. A card is drawn at random from the bag. The probability of getting a card with a prime number is:
    • (a)21​
    • (b)52​
    • (c)103​
    • (d)95​
    Show answer

    Answer: (a) 21​

    There are 10 cards. Primes: 2, 3, 5, 7, 11, so 5 of them. Probability =105​=21​.

  7. Q7

    A bag contains 8 red, 2 black and 5 white balls. One ball is drawn at random. What is the probability that the ball drawn is not black?
    • (a)158​
    • (b)152​
    • (c)1513​
    • (d)31​
    Show answer

    Answer: (c) 1513​

    There are 15 balls, 2 black. P(not black)=1513​.
