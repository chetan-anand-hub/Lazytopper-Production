Common questions

What is the Class 10 Crash Course 2027?

A revision set for CBSE Class 10 Maths and Science: a 1-page revision sheet for every chapter, NCERT diagrams, the most-asked board questions with marking schemes, and a 75-day plan to the boards. It is made by an IITian from IIT Kanpur.

Can I buy the Crash Course on its own?

No. It is a bonus included free with the Board Pass, the Pre-Boards Test Series, the Maths + Science Bundle, a Subject Pack, a CBQ Pack or a Chapter Pack. A Chapter Pack includes that chapter's sheet.

Which Crash Course files come with which purchase?

The Board Pass, the Test Series Pack and the Maths + Science Bundle include both subject books and every chapter sheet. A Subject Pack includes that subject's book and its chapter sheets. A CBQ Pack includes the book for its subject. A Chapter Pack includes that chapter's sheet.

Does the Crash Course cover the 2026-27 syllabus?

Yes. It follows the CBSE 2026-27 Class 10 Maths and Science syllabus. Topics removed from that syllabus are not in it.
