Questions

What’s inside a pack?

For each chapter: its questions grouped by CBSE section (A to E) with a matching answer key with worked solutions, two chapter tests and one worksheet (each with a key). Mock packs are eight full 80-mark papers per subject with keys. A few questions that depend on a diagram we can’t print cleanly are left out, so every page can be solved on paper.

Can I download again later?

Yes. Download again any time until 31 May 2027: sign in, open My packs at the top of this page and download any file as often as you like. Packs are for your own use; each PDF carries your name and email on every page, so please don’t share the files.

Can I get my answers marked?

Chapter tests, worksheets and mock papers print a code (like PK-M-FULL-M1). After you solve one, upload your answers from the “papers waiting for your answers” row on Home and LazyTopper marks it. What marking you get there depends on your LazyTopper plan.

How does the Board Pass credit work?

Packs are one-time and never renew. If you take the Board Pass within 7 days of buying a pack, the pack price comes off the Board Pass. The Board Pass adds what a PDF can’t: your answers marked step by step, Mistake Intelligence, the tutor for doubts and online timed tests. Compare with the Board Pass.

What if a file doesn’t open?

Download it again from My packs; PDFs open in any PDF reader or in your browser. Still stuck? Contact us.
