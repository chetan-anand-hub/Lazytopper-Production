What’s inside a pack?

For each chapter: its questions grouped by CBSE section (A to E) with matching step-by-step worked solutions, two chapter tests and one worksheet (each with a marking guide). Mock packs are eight full 80-mark papers per subject with marking guides. A few questions that depend on a diagram we can’t print cleanly are left out, so every page can be solved on paper.

Can I download again later?

Yes. Download again any time until 31 March 2027: sign in, open My packs at the top of this page and download any file as often as you like. Packs are for your own use; each PDF carries your name, or your email or masked phone number, on every page, so please don’t share the files.

Can I get my answers marked?

Chapter tests, worksheets and mock papers print a code (like PK-M-FULL-M1). After you solve one, upload your answers from the “papers waiting for your answers” row on Home and LazyTopper marks it. What marking you get there depends on your LazyTopper plan.

How is a pack different from the Board Pass?

Packs are one-time and never renew. The Board Pass adds what a PDF can’t: your answers marked step by step, Mistake Intelligence, the tutor for doubts and online timed tests. Compare with the Board Pass.

What are CBQ Packs?

Half your board paper is competency-based. Every CBQ type, chapter-wise, with step-wise worked solutions. Competency-based questions (case study, assertion-reason, MCQ, short and long answer), 1,400+ CBQs per subject, chapter-wise with step-wise worked solutions. They are sold on their own page, ₹199 + GST per subject: see the CBQ Packs.
