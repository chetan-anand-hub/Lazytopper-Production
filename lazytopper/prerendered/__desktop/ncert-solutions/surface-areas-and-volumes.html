Exercise 12.1

NCERT Ex 12.1 Q11 mark
Two cubes each of volume 64 cm³ are joined end to end. The surface area of the resulting cuboid is
Step 1 Side of each cube = ∛64 = 4 cm.

Step-by-step solution

  1. Side of each cube = ∛64 = 4 cm.
  2. When joined end to end, the resulting cuboid has dimensions l × b × h = 8 × 4 × 4 cm.
  3. Surface area = 2(lb + bh + hl) = 2(8·4 + 4·4 + 4·8) cm² = 2(32 + 16 + 32) cm² = 2 × 80 cm² = 160 cm².

Answer: 160 cm² — option (B).

NCERT Ex 12.1 Q42 marks
A cubical block of side 7 cm is surmounted by a hemisphere. What is the greatest diameter the hemisphere can have? Find the surface area of the solid. (Take π = 22/7.)
Step 1 Greatest possible diameter = side of the cube = 7 cm, so radius r = 3.5 cm.

Step-by-step solution

  1. Greatest possible diameter = side of the cube = 7 cm, so radius r = 3.5 cm.
  2. SA of solid = TSA of cube − base area of hemisphere + CSA of hemisphere = 6a² − πr² + 2πr² = 6a² + πr².
  3. = 6(7)² + (22/7)(3.5)² cm² = 294 + (22/7)(12.25) cm² = 294 + 38.5 cm² = 332.5 cm².

Answer: Greatest diameter = 7 cm; surface area = 332.5 cm².

NCERT Ex 12.1 Q62 marks
A medicine capsule is in the shape of a cylinder with two hemispheres stuck to each of its ends. The length of the entire capsule is 14 mm and the diameter of the capsule is 5 mm. Find its surface area. (Take π = 22/7.)
Step 1 Radius r = 5/2 = 2.5 mm. Cylindrical length = 14 − 2(2.5) = 9 mm.

Step-by-step solution

  1. Radius r = 5/2 = 2.5 mm. Cylindrical length = 14 − 2(2.5) = 9 mm.
  2. SA = CSA of cylinder + CSA of two hemispheres = 2πrh + 4πr² = 2πr(h + 2r).
  3. = 2 × (22/7) × 2.5 × (9 + 5) mm² = 2 × (22/7) × 2.5 × 14 mm² = 220 mm².

Answer: Surface area of capsule = 220 mm².

NCERT Ex 12.1 Q23 marks
A vessel is in the form of a hollow hemisphere mounted by a hollow cylinder. The diameter of the hemisphere is 14 cm and the total height of the vessel is 13 cm. Find the inner surface area of the vessel. (Take π = 22/7.)
Step 1 Radius r = 14/2 = 7 cm. Height of cylindrical part h = 13 − 7 = 6 cm.

Step-by-step solution

  1. Radius r = 14/2 = 7 cm. Height of cylindrical part h = 13 − 7 = 6 cm.
  2. Inner SA = CSA of hemisphere + CSA of cylinder = 2πr² + 2πrh = 2πr(r + h).
  3. = 2 × (22/7) × 7 × (7 + 6) cm² = 2 × 22 × 13 cm² = 572 cm².

Answer: Inner surface area = 572 cm².

NCERT Ex 12.1 Q33 marks
A toy is in the form of a cone of radius 3.5 cm mounted on a hemisphere of the same radius. The total height of the toy is 15.5 cm. Find the total surface area of the toy. (Take π = 22/7.)
Step 1 Radius r = 3.5 cm. Height of cone h = 15.5 − 3.5 = 12 cm.

Step-by-step solution

  1. Radius r = 3.5 cm. Height of cone h = 15.5 − 3.5 = 12 cm.
  2. Slant height l = √(r² + h²) = √(12.25 + 144) = √156.25 = 12.5 cm.
  3. TSA = CSA of cone + CSA of hemisphere = πrl + 2πr² = πr(l + 2r).
  4. = (22/7) × 3.5 × (12.5 + 7) cm² = (22/7) × 3.5 × 19.5 cm² = 11 × 19.5 cm² = 214.5 cm².

Answer: Total surface area of toy = 214.5 cm².

Exercise 12.2

NCERT Ex 12.2 Q23 marks
Rachel, an engineering student, was asked to make a model shaped like a cylinder with two cones attached at its two ends by using a thin aluminium sheet. The diameter of the model is 3 cm and its length is 12 cm. If each cone has a height of 2 cm, find the volume of air contained in the model. (Take π = 22/7.)
Step 1 Radius r = 3/2 = 1.5 cm. Length of cylinder h = 12 − 2(2) = 8 cm. Cone height hc​ = 2 cm.

Step-by-step solution

  1. Radius r = 3/2 = 1.5 cm. Length of cylinder h = 12 − 2(2) = 8 cm. Cone height hc​ = 2 cm.
  2. Volume = πr²h + 2 × (1/3)πr²hc​ = πr²[h + (2/3)hc​].
  3. = (22/7)(1.5)²[8 + 4/3] cm³ = (22/7)(2.25)(28/3) cm³.
  4. = (22 × 2.25 × 28)/(7 × 3) cm³ = 1386/21 cm³ = 66 cm³.

Answer: Volume of air in model = 66 cm³.

NCERT Ex 12.2 Q35 marks
A gulab jamun contains sugar syrup up to about 30% of its volume. Find approximately how much syrup would be found in 45 gulab jamuns, each shaped like a cylinder with two hemispherical ends, with length 5 cm and diameter 2.8 cm. (Take π = 22/7.)
Step 1 Radius r = 2.8/2 = 1.4 cm. Cylinder length = 5 − 2(1.4) = 2.2 cm.

Step-by-step solution

  1. Radius r = 2.8/2 = 1.4 cm. Cylinder length = 5 − 2(1.4) = 2.2 cm.
  2. Volume of one gulab jamun = πr²h + (4/3)πr³ = πr²[h + (4/3)r] = (22/7)(1.4)²[2.2 + (4/3)(1.4)] cm³.
  3. = (22/7)(1.96)[2.2 + 1.8667] cm³ ≈ (22/7)(1.96)(4.0667) cm³ ≈ 25.0507 cm³.
  4. Volume of 45 gulab jamuns ≈ 45 × 25.0507 cm³ ≈ 1127.28 cm³.
  5. Syrup (30%) = 0.30 × 1127.28 ≈ 338 cm³.

Answer: Approximate syrup in 45 gulab jamuns ≈ 338 cm³.

Examples and more questions

NCERT Example 13 marks
Rasheed got a playing top (lattu) which is shaped like a cone surmounted by a hemisphere. The entire top is 5 cm in height and the diameter of the top is 3.5 cm. Find the area he has to colour. (Take π = 22/7.)
Step 1 Radius r = 3.5/2 = 1.75 cm. Height of cone h = 5 − 1.75 = 3.25 cm.

Step-by-step solution

  1. Radius r = 3.5/2 = 1.75 cm. Height of cone h = 5 − 1.75 = 3.25 cm.
  2. Slant height l = √(r² + h²) = √(1.75² + 3.25²) = √(3.0625 + 10.5625) = √13.625 ≈ 3.7 cm.
  3. Area to colour = CSA of hemisphere + CSA of cone = 2πr² + πrl = πr(2r + l).
  4. = (22/7) × 1.75 × (3.5 + 3.7) cm² = (22/7) × 1.75 × 7.2 cm² ≈ 39.6 cm².

Answer: Area to be coloured ≈ 39.6 cm².

CBSE board pattern · Case-based4 marks
A tent is in the shape of a cylinder surmounted by a conical top. The height and diameter of the cylindrical part are 2.1 m and 4 m respectively, and the slant height of the conical top is 2.8 m. (i) Find the area of the canvas required for making the tent (the base of the tent is NOT covered with canvas). (ii) Find the cost of the canvas at ₹500 per m². (Take π = 22/7.)
Step 1 Radius r = 4/2 = 2 m. Cylinder height h = 2.1 m. Cone slant l = 2.8 m.

Step-by-step solution

  1. Radius r = 4/2 = 2 m. Cylinder height h = 2.1 m. Cone slant l = 2.8 m.
  2. Canvas area = CSA of cylinder + CSA of cone = 2πrh + πrl = πr(2h + l).
  3. = (22/7)(2)(2 × 2.1 + 2.8) m² = (22/7)(2)(4.2 + 2.8) m² = (22/7)(2)(7) m² = 44 m².
  4. Cost = 44 × ₹500 = ₹22,000.

Answer: Canvas area = 44 m²; total cost = ₹22,000.
