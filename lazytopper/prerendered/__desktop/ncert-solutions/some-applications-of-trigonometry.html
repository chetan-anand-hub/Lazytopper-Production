Exercise 9.1

NCERT Ex 9.1 Q12 marks
A circus artist is climbing a 20 m long rope, which is tightly stretched and tied from the top of a vertical pole to the ground. Find the height of the pole if the rope makes an angle of 30° with the ground.
Step 1 Right triangle: hypotenuse = rope = 20 m; angle with ground = 30°; opposite side = height h.

Step-by-step solution

  1. Right triangle: hypotenuse = rope = 20 m; angle with ground = 30°; opposite side = height h.
  2. sin 30° = opposite/hypotenuse = h/20.
  3. 1/2 = h/20 ⇒ h = 10.

Answer: Height of the pole = 10 m.

NCERT Ex 9.1 Q23 marks
A tree breaks due to a storm and the broken part bends so that the top of the tree touches the ground making an angle of 30° with it. The distance between the foot of the tree and the point where the top touches the ground is 8 m. Find the height of the tree.
Step 1 Let the unbroken part standing upright be BC = h (vertical), and the broken/leaning part CA = ℓ. The broken top touches the ground at A, 8 m from B (foot).

Step-by-step solution

  1. Let the unbroken part standing upright be BC = h (vertical), and the broken/leaning part CA = ℓ. The broken top touches the ground at A, 8 m from B (foot).
  2. Angle at A = 30°; right angle is at B.
  3. tan 30° = BC/BA ⇒ 1/√3 = h/8 ⇒ h = 8/√3 = 8√3/3 m.
  4. cos 30° = BA/CA ⇒ √3/2 = 8/ℓ ⇒ ℓ = 16/√3 = 16√3/3 m.
  5. Total height of tree = h + ℓ = 8√3/3 + 16√3/3 = 24√3/3 = 8√3 m.

Answer: Height of the tree = 8√3 m ≈ 13.86 m.

NCERT Ex 9.1 Q42 marks
The angle of elevation of the top of a tower from a point on the ground, which is 30 m from the foot of the tower, is 30°. Find the height of the tower.
Step 1 tan 30° = height/distance = h/30.

Step-by-step solution

  1. tan 30° = height/distance = h/30.
  2. 1/√3 = h/30 ⇒ h = 30/√3 = 10√3 m.

Answer: Height of the tower = 10√3 m ≈ 17.32 m.

NCERT Ex 9.1 Q52 marks
A kite is flying at a height of 60 m above the ground. The string attached to the kite is temporarily tied to a point on the ground; the inclination of the string with the ground is 60°. Find the length of the string assuming no slack.
Step 1 Let string length = ℓ. Height of kite = 60 m. Angle of inclination with ground = 60°.

Step-by-step solution

  1. Let string length = ℓ. Height of kite = 60 m. Angle of inclination with ground = 60°.
  2. sin 60° = opposite/hypotenuse = 60/ℓ.
  3. √3/2 = 60/ℓ ⇒ ℓ = 120/√3 = 40√3 m.

Answer: Length of string = 40√3 m ≈ 69.28 m.

NCERT Ex 9.1 Q65 marks
A 1.5 m tall boy is standing at some distance from a 30 m tall building. The angle of elevation from his eyes to the top of the building increases from 30° to 60° as he walks towards the building. Find the distance he walked towards the building.
Step 1 Height available above eye-level = 30 − 1.5 = 28.5 m.

Step-by-step solution

  1. Height available above eye-level = 30 − 1.5 = 28.5 m.
  2. Let the initial horizontal distance from eye to top of building be x and final be y. Walking forward gives 30° → 60°.
  3. At 30°: tan 30° = 28.5/x ⇒ x = 28.5 √3.
  4. At 60°: tan 60° = 28.5/y ⇒ y = 28.5/√3 = 28.5√3/3 = 9.5√3.
  5. Distance walked = x − y = 28.5√3 − 9.5√3 = 19√3 m.
  6. Numerically ≈ 19 × 1.732 ≈ 32.9 m.

Answer: Distance walked = 19√3 m ≈ 32.9 m.

NCERT Ex 9.1 Q75 marks
From a point on the ground, the angles of elevation of the bottom and the top of a transmission tower fixed at the top of a 20 m high building are 45° and 60° respectively. Find the height of the tower.
Step 1 Let the building be AB = 20 m and the tower be BC = h on top of it. Let P be the point on the ground at distance d from A. ∠APB = 45° (to top of building) and ∠APC = 60° (to top of tower).

Step-by-step solution

  1. Let the building be AB = 20 m and the tower be BC = h on top of it. Let P be the point on the ground at distance d from A. ∠APB = 45° (to top of building) and ∠APC = 60° (to top of tower).
  2. In ∆PAB: tan 45° = 20/d ⇒ 1 = 20/d ⇒ d = 20 m.
  3. In ∆PAC: tan 60° = (20 + h)/d = (20 + h)/20.
  4. √3 = (20 + h)/20 ⇒ 20 + h = 20√3 ⇒ h = 20√3 − 20 = 20(√3 − 1).
  5. Numerically ≈ 20(0.732) ≈ 14.64 m.

Answer: Height of the tower = 20(√3 − 1) m ≈ 14.64 m.

NCERT Ex 9.1 Q95 marks
The angle of elevation of the top of a building from the foot of the tower is 30°, and the angle of elevation of the top of the tower from the foot of the building is 60°. If the tower is 50 m high, find the height of the building.
Step 1 Let the building be PQ (height = h) and the tower be RS (height = 50 m). The distance between their feet is d.

Step-by-step solution

  1. Let the building be PQ (height = h) and the tower be RS (height = 50 m). The distance between their feet is d.
  2. From foot of tower (R), angle of elevation of top of building (P) = 30°: tan 30° = h/d ⇒ 1/√3 = h/d ⇒ d = h√3. …(1)
  3. From foot of building (Q), angle of elevation of top of tower (S) = 60°: tan 60° = 50/d ⇒ √3 = 50/d ⇒ d = 50/√3. …(2)
  4. Equate (1) and (2): h√3 = 50/√3 ⇒ 3h = 50 ⇒ h = 50/3.
  5. Numerically ≈ 16.67 m.

Answer: Height of the building = 50/3 m ≈ 16.67 m.

NCERT Ex 9.1 Q145 marks
A 1.2 m tall girl spots a balloon moving with the wind in a horizontal line at a height of 88.2 m from the ground. The angle of elevation of the balloon from her eyes at any instant is 60°. After some time, the angle of elevation reduces to 30°. Find the distance travelled by the balloon during the interval.
Step 1 Effective vertical height above eye level = 88.2 − 1.2 = 87 m (balloon moves along this horizontal line).

Step-by-step solution

  1. Effective vertical height above eye level = 88.2 − 1.2 = 87 m (balloon moves along this horizontal line).
  2. Let x₁ = horizontal distance from girl's eyes to balloon when angle = 60°; x₂ = horizontal distance when angle = 30°.
  3. At 60°: tan 60° = 87/x₁ ⇒ x₁ = 87/√3 = 29√3.
  4. At 30°: tan 30° = 87/x₂ ⇒ x₂ = 87√3.
  5. Distance travelled = x₂ − x₁ = 87√3 − 29√3 = 58√3 m.
  6. Numerically ≈ 58 × 1.732 ≈ 100.46 m.

Answer: Distance travelled = 58√3 m ≈ 100.46 m.

NCERT Example 1 (Ch9)2 marks
A tower stands vertically on the ground. From a point on the ground, 15 m from the foot of the tower, the angle of elevation of the top of the tower is 60°. Find the height of the tower.
Step 1 Picture: right triangle with horizontal leg = 15 m (distance from point to tower foot), angle of elevation 60° at the observer's foot, vertical leg = height of tower h.

Step-by-step solution

  1. Picture: right triangle with horizontal leg = 15 m (distance from point to tower foot), angle of elevation 60° at the observer's foot, vertical leg = height of tower h.
  2. tan 60° = opposite/adjacent = h / 15.
  3. tan 60° = √3, so √3 = h / 15.
  4. h = 15√3 m.

Answer: Height of the tower = 15√3 m ≈ 25.98 m.

NCERT Example 4 (Ch9)5 marks
From a point P on the ground, the angle of elevation of the top of a 10 m tall building is 30°. A flag is hoisted at the top of the building and the angle of elevation of the top of the flagstaff from P is 45°. Find the length of the flagstaff and the distance of the building from the point P. (Take √3 = 1.732.)
Step 1 Let A be the foot of the building, B the top of the building, D the top of the flagstaff; P is on the ground. AB = 10 m (building), BD = x (flagstaff). PA = distance from P to building foot.

Step-by-step solution

  1. Let A be the foot of the building, B the top of the building, D the top of the flagstaff; P is on the ground. AB = 10 m (building), BD = x (flagstaff). PA = distance from P to building foot.
  2. In right ∆PAB: tan 30° = AB/PA ⇒ 1/√3 = 10/PA ⇒ PA = 10√3 m = 10(1.732) = 17.32 m.
  3. In right ∆PAD: tan 45° = AD/PA = (10 + x)/PA. Since tan 45° = 1, AD = PA = 10√3.
  4. 10 + x = 10√3 ⇒ x = 10√3 − 10 = 10(√3 − 1) = 10(1.732 − 1) = 10(0.732) = 7.32 m.
  5. So the flagstaff is 7.32 m long and the building is 17.32 m from P.

Answer: Length of flagstaff = 7.32 m; distance of building from P = 17.32 m.

NCERT Example 5 (Ch9)5 marks
The shadow of a tower standing on level ground is 40 m longer when the Sun's altitude is 30° than when it is 60°. Find the height of the tower.
Step 1 Let the tower be AB with height h, B the foot. Let BC = x (shadow at 60° altitude), BD = x + 40 (shadow at 30° altitude).

Step-by-step solution

  1. Let the tower be AB with height h, B the foot. Let BC = x (shadow at 60° altitude), BD = x + 40 (shadow at 30° altitude).
  2. In ∆ABC (right-angled at B), tan 60° = h/x ⇒ √3 = h/x ⇒ h = x√3. …(1)
  3. In ∆ABD (right-angled at B), tan 30° = h/(x + 40) ⇒ 1/√3 = h/(x + 40) ⇒ h = (x + 40)/√3. …(2)
  4. Equate (1) and (2): x√3 = (x + 40)/√3 ⇒ 3x = x + 40 ⇒ 2x = 40 ⇒ x = 20.
  5. Substitute back: h = 20√3 m.

Answer: Height of the tower = 20√3 m ≈ 34.64 m.
