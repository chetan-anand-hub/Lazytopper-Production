Exemplar Q11 mark
In the list of organisms given below, those that reproduce by the asexual method are: (i) banana (ii) dog (iii) yeast (iv) Amoeba

Step-by-step solution

  1. Banana: vegetative propagation (asexual).
  2. Dog: sexual reproduction only.
  3. Yeast: budding (asexual).
  4. Amoeba: binary fission (asexual). Answer: (B)

Answer: (b) (i), (iii) and (iv).

Exemplar Q31 mark
Which of the following is the correct sequence of events of sexual reproduction in a flower?

Step-by-step solution

  1. Step 1: Pollination (pollen → stigma).
  2. Step 2: Fertilisation (male + female gamete fuse).
  3. Step 3: Zygote develops into embryo within ovule.
  4. Step 4: Seed germinates → seedling. Answer: (C)

Answer: (c) Pollination → fertilisation → embryo → seedling.

Exemplar Q41 mark
Offspring formed by asexual method of reproduction have greater similarity among themselves because: (i) asexual reproduction involves only one parent; (ii) asexual reproduction does not involve gametes; (iii) asexual reproduction occurs before sexual reproduction; (iv) asexual reproduction occurs after sexual reproduction

Step-by-step solution

  1. Asexual involves only ONE parent → all offspring inherit only its DNA → identical.
  2. No gametes → no shuffling/recombination of genetic material → no variation.
  3. Statements (iii) and (iv) are unrelated to similarity. Answer: (A)

Answer: (a) (i) and (ii).

Exemplar Q161 mark
Which of the following statements are true for flowers? (i) Flowers are always bisexual; (ii) They are the sexual reproductive organs; (iii) They are produced in all groups of plants; (iv) After fertilisation they give rise to fruits

Step-by-step solution

  1. (i) WRONG — flowers may be unisexual (papaya) or bisexual (Hibiscus).
  2. (ii) Correct — flowers are sexual reproductive organs of angiosperms.
  3. (iii) WRONG — flowers only in angiosperms, not in all plants.
  4. (iv) Correct — after fertilisation, ovary develops into fruit. Answer: (D)

Answer: (d) (ii) and (iv).

Exemplar Q171 mark
Which among the following statements are true for unisexual flowers? (i) They possess both stamen and pistil; (ii) They possess either stamen or pistil; (iii) They exhibit cross pollination; (iv) Unisexual flowers possessing only stamens cannot produce fruits

Step-by-step solution

  1. (i) WRONG — unisexual flowers have EITHER stamen OR pistil, not both.
  2. (ii) Correct definition of unisexual.
  3. (iii) Correct — they must cross-pollinate (need other flower for opposite gamete).
  4. (iv) Correct — male flowers (only stamens) cannot produce fruits (need ovary). Answer: (B)

Answer: (b) (ii), (iii) and (iv).

Exemplar Q201 mark
Offspring formed as a result of sexual reproduction exhibit more variations because

Step-by-step solution

  1. Sexual reproduction combines DNA from TWO parents of the SAME species.
  2. Combination of two different sets of genes → variation.
  3. Cross-species mating doesn't normally produce viable offspring. Answer: (B)

Answer: (b) Genetic material comes from two parents of the same species.

Exemplar Q251 mark
Which among the following is not the function of testes at puberty? (i) formation of germ cells (ii) secretion of testosterone (iii) development of placenta (iv) secretion of estrogen

Exemplar Q261 mark
The correct sequence of organs in the male reproductive system for transport of sperms is

Step-by-step solution

  1. Sperm is produced in testis.
  2. Carried via vas deferens.
  3. Vas deferens joins with tube from urinary bladder; sperm enters urethra.
  4. Urethra carries sperm out through penis.
  5. (ureter carries urine from kidney to bladder — not involved in sperm transport). Answer: (A)

Answer: (a) Testis → vas deferens → urethra.

Short answer questions

Exemplar Q292 marks
Can you consider cell division as a type of reproduction in unicellular organism? Give one reason.

Step-by-step solution

  1. Unicellular organism = single cell = entire individual.
  2. Cell division produces two cells → two new individuals.
  3. Hence cell division IS reproduction in unicellular organisms.

Answer: Yes — in unicellular organisms, cell division creates new individuals (since the cell IS the organism).

Exemplar Q382 marks
Why cannot fertilisation take place in flowers if pollination does not occur?

Step-by-step solution

  1. Pollen contains the male gamete.
  2. Pollen must land on stigma (pollination) so that the pollen tube can grow into the ovary.
  3. Pollen tube delivers male gamete to the egg (ovule) for fertilisation.
  4. Without pollination → no pollen on stigma → no pollen tube → no gamete fusion → no fertilisation.

Answer: Without pollination, pollen (and hence male gamete) never reaches the ovule → no fertilisation.
