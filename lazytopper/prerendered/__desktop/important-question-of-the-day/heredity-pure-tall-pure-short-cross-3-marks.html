The question

Short answer3 marks
Show the cross and observations made by Mendel when he crossed pure tall pea plant with a pure short pea plant to get F1 progeny.
Show the step-marking answer
  1. [1 mark] Cross: Parents - pure tall (TT) × pure short (tt); gametes - T (from tall) and t (from short).
  2. [1 mark] F1 progeny: T × t → Tt; all F1 plants are Tt, i.e. all tall.
  3. [1 mark] Observations (any two): all F1 plants were tall; there were no short plants and no plants of medium (intermediate) height; only one of the two parental traits (tallness) was expressed - shortness was not seen in F1.

Answer: TT × tt → F1 all Tt (tall); no short or intermediate plants - tallness is dominant, shortness not expressed in F1.
