Frequently Asked Questions

Is Basic really free?
Yes. Basic is free forever: study, practise and open papers without paying. Checked answers, the AI tutor and Mistake Intelligence come with Premium.
Should I pay monthly or till my boards?
Till boards is one payment that covers every month until your first board paper, at 20% less than paying monthly. The price shrinks each month as the boards get closer.
What happens when the founding offer ends?
When it ends, new members join at the regular price — ₹999 for a month, or 20% off when you pay once till your boards, which is what we publish today alongside the founding rate. Your rate is locked. Once you buy a pass as a founding member, every pass you buy after that stays at the founding price. We never raise the price of a pass you have already bought.
Are there any usage limits?
Basic has daily limits on opening tests and mocks. Premium has generous fair use: your usage bars show what is left and when it resets. The 7-day trial has daily and weekly limits, listed above.
What happens after the trial ends?
You keep using the free plan. Nothing is charged automatically; you choose when to upgrade.
Can I pay here?
Yes. Tap Pay, finish in Razorpay's secure checkout (UPI, card or netbanking), and Premium turns on straight away.
Is this only for CBSE Class 10?
