Section D5 marks
Draw a labelled ray diagram to show refraction of light when it passes from a denser medium (glass) to a rarer medium (air). Mark the angle of incidence, angle of refraction and normal. State Snell’s law.
Section E4 marksCase-based
A student placed an object 30 cm in front of a convex lens of focal length 20 cm. (a) Find the position of the image using the lens formula. (b) What is the magnification? (c) State the nature of the image (real/virtual, inverted/erect, magnified/diminished). (d) If the object is moved to a position 15 cm from the lens, how does the image change?
Section A1 markAssertion–Reason
Assertion–Reason: refer to assertion and reason below.
Assertion: When light travels from a denser to a rarer medium, it bends away from the normal.
Reason: The speed of light increases when it passes from a denser to a rarer medium.
Section A1 markAssertion–Reason
Assertion–Reason: refer to assertion and reason below.
Assertion: A concave mirror always forms a real and inverted image.
Reason: When the object is placed between the pole and focus of a concave mirror, the image formed is virtual and erect.
