How your tests are marked

  • Section A (the MCQs) is marked the moment you submit, free on every account.
  • Sections B to D (and E in full-length tests): write on paper and upload a photo. The Test Series checks every written answer step by step, 2 checks per paper, like a CBSE examiner, and shows where each mark was lost.
  • Downloading and printing these papers comes with the Test Series and the Board Pass.
