About these books

These are the NCERT textbooks for the 2026-27 session, the edition the CBSE Class 10 board exam in 2027 follows. Chapters removed from the syllabus are not listed. Mathematics Chapters 8 and 9 (trigonometry and its applications) share one set of LazyTopper notes. The official sample papers and marking schemes are on our CBSE Class 10 page.
