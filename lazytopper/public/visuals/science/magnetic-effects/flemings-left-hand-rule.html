📝 Worked Example (CBSE Exam Type)

Q: A horizontal conductor carries current from West to East. The magnetic field points vertically downward. Find the direction of force on the conductor.

Step 1 — Identify I direction:
Current (I) flows West → East. Point your left hand's middle finger toward East (→).
Step 2 — Identify B direction:
Magnetic field (B) is vertically downward (↓). Point your left hand's forefinger downward.
Keep middle finger → East and forefinger ↓ Down simultaneously.
✅ Answer — Force Direction:
Using F = I(L × B)  |  East × Down = South (toward South)
∴ Your thumb points South.
The conductor will be pushed toward the South direction! 🎯
