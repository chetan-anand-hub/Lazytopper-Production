⚙️ Motor Running — Converting Electrical → Mechanical Energy

🔩 Armature (आर्मेचर)

Rectangular coil that rotates in the magnetic field. Carries current from battery.

🔄 Commutator (कम्यूटेटर)

Reverses current direction every half rotation. Ensures continuous rotation in DC motors.

🖊️ Brushes (ब्रश)

Carbon brushes maintain electrical contact with rotating commutator rings.

🧲 Magnetic Field (चुंबकीय क्षेत्र)

Created by permanent magnets (N→S). Force on conductor: F = BIL. Fleming's Left Hand Rule applies.

Faraday's Law — Induced EMF (प्रेरित विद्युत वाहक बल)
ε = −N × dΦ/dt
N=Turns  |  Φ=Magnetic Flux  |  t=Time
जनित्र — यांत्रिक ऊर्जा → विद्युत ऊर्जा (विद्युत चुंबकीय प्रेरण)
RPM: 5
⚡ Generating AC — Electromagnetic Induction

🔄 AC Generator (प्रत्यावर्ती धारा जनित्र)

Uses slip rings (वलय). Current reverses every half cycle. Used in power stations.

⚡ DC Generator (दिष्ट धारा जनित्र)

Uses commutator (कम्यूटेटर). Current flows in one direction only. Used in cars, bicycles.

🧲 Fleming's Right Hand Rule

फ्लेमिंग के दाहिने हाथ का नियम — Thumb=Motion, Index=Field, Middle=Current direction

📐 Lenz's Law (लेंज का नियम)

Induced current opposes the change in magnetic flux that causes it. Energy is conserved.
