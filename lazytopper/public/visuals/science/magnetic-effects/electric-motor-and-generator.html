⚡ Electric Generator — विद्युत जनरेटर

Converts Mechanical Energy → Electrical Energy

EMF = B × L × v × sin θ
Faraday's Law: EMF induced due to changing magnetic flux (विद्युत चुम्बकीय प्रेरण)

⚖️ Motor vs Generator — तुलना

⚙️ Electric Motor

  • Electrical → Mechanical
  • Uses: Commutator
  • Left Hand Rule
  • Needs power supply
  • Used in: fans, pumps
  • Current is INPUT

⚡ Generator

  • Mechanical → Electrical
  • AC: Slip rings
  • Right Hand Rule
  • Produces electricity
  • Used in: power plants
  • Current is OUTPUT
