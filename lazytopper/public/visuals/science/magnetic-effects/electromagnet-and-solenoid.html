⚡ Electromagnet & Solenoid | विद्युत चुम्बक

CBSE Class 10 Science • Magnetic Effects of Electric Current

🔑 Core Concept विद्युत चुम्बक

Magnetic Field Strength of Solenoid
B = μ₀ × n × I
B = Magnetic Field  |  μ₀ = 4π×10⁻⁷ T·m/A  |  n = turns/meter  |  I = Current (A)
A Solenoid (परिनालिका) is a long coil of wire with many circular turns. When electric current flows through it, it acts like a bar magnet. Inserting a soft iron core makes it an Electromagnet!

📋 Step-by-Step Walkthrough क्रमशः

Step 1 of 6
Take a long insulated wire and wind it tightly into many circular loops around a cylindrical shape. This coil is called a Solenoid (परिनालिका).
Step 2 of 6
Connect the solenoid ends to a battery (बैटरी). An electric current (विद्युत धारा) begins to flow through every turn of the coil.
Step 3 of 6
Each circular turn acts as a tiny circular magnet. All turns together create a combined magnetic field — like a bar magnet (दण्ड चुम्बक)!
Step 4 of 6
The end where current flows anticlockwise becomes the North Pole (N). Where current flows clockwise → South Pole (S). (ANTI = N rule)
Step 5 of 6
Inserting a soft iron core (मृदु लोहे की क्रोड) inside the solenoid greatly increases magnetic field strength — this is now an Electromagnet!
Step 6 of 6 ✅
The field can be increased by: more turns (n↑), more current (I↑), using soft iron core. It can be reversed by reversing current direction. ∴ It's controllable!

📐 Worked Example हल उदाहरण

Question: A solenoid has 500 turns wound over a length of 50 cm. A current of 2 A passes through it. Find the magnetic field inside (μ₀ = 4π×10⁻⁷ T·m/A).
Given:
N = 500 turns, L = 50 cm = 0.5 m, I = 2 A
Step 1: Find n (turns per meter)
n = N ÷ L = 500 ÷ 0.5 = 1000 turns/m
Step 2: Apply Formula
B = μ₀ × n × I = 4π×10⁻⁷ × 1000 × 2
Step 3: Calculate
B = 4π×10⁻⁷ × 2000 = 8π×10⁻⁴ ≈ 2.51×10⁻³ T
∴ Answer:
The magnetic field B ≈ 2.51 × 10⁻³ Tesla (or 2.51 mT)

⚖️ Comparison तुलना

🧲 Permanent Magnet (स्थायी चुम्बक)

• Always magnetic
• Cannot be turned off
• Fixed strength
• Steel used
• Cannot change poles

⚡ Electromagnet (विद्युत चुम्बक)

• Magnetic when current flows
• Can be switched OFF ✓
• Strength adjustable ✓
• Soft iron used
• Poles reversible ✓

🏭 Applications (उपयोग): Electric bells • Cranes (lifting scrap) • MRI machines • Speakers • Electric motors • Magnetic locks

📌 Key Points to Remember
