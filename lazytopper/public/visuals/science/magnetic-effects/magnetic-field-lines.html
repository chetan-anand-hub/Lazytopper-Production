What are Magnetic Field Lines? (चुंबकीय क्षेत्र रेखाएं)

Imaginary lines that represent the direction and strength of a magnetic field. They show the path along which a free north pole would move in the magnetic field.

⚡ Key Rule (मुख्य नियम): Field lines emerge from North (N) pole and enter the South (S) pole outside the magnet. Inside the magnet, they go from S → N.

📋 Properties of Magnetic Field Lines (गुण)
🔄

1. Closed Loops

Field lines form continuous closed loops - they never start or end (unlike electric field lines).

🚫

2. Never Intersect

Two magnetic field lines never cross each other. If they did, compass would point in 2 directions - impossible!

↗️

3. N→S Outside

Outside magnet: N pole → S pole. Inside magnet: S pole → N pole. Always continuous.

💪

4. Density = Strength

Closely spaced lines = Strong field (near poles). Widely spaced = Weak field (far from magnet).

↔️

5. Tangent = Direction

The tangent drawn at any point on field line gives the direction of magnetic field at that point.

😤

6. Repel Laterally

Field lines tend to repel each other sideways (lateral repulsion) while contracting longitudinally.

👣 Step-by-Step: How to Draw Field Lines of a Bar Magnet
Step 1: Place the Bar Magnet
Draw a rectangle. Label left half N (North) and right half S (South).
एक आयत बनाएं। बाईं तरफ N और दाईं तरफ S लिखें।
Step 2: Identify Pole Regions
Field lines are densest near poles. Mark points around N and S poles where lines will emerge/enter.
ध्रुवों के पास रेखाएं सबसे घनी होती हैं।
Step 3: Draw Lines from N Pole
Lines curve outward from N pole, going around the magnet. They curve towards the S pole.
∴ Direction: N → S (outside)
N ध्रुव से बाहर की ओर वक्र रेखाएं खींचें।
Step 4: Lines Enter S Pole
The same lines that came from N pole now enter the S pole. Add arrows to show direction (N→S outside).
वही रेखाएं S ध्रुव में प्रवेश करती हैं।
Step 5: Inside the Magnet
Inside the magnet, field lines go from S → N (completing the closed loop). Draw parallel lines inside magnet with arrows S→N.
चुंबक के अंदर रेखाएं S से N की ओर जाती हैं।
Step 6: Verify Properties ✓
✅ Lines are closed loops
✅ Lines never intersect
✅ Dense near poles = Strong field
✅ Sparse far away = Weak field
∴ चुंबकीय बल रेखाओं का चित्र पूर्ण हुआ!
