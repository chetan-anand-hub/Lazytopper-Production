✏️ Worked Example – Step by Step

Question: An object is placed 20 cm in front of a convex lens of focal length 10 cm. Find image distance and magnification.

वस्तु (object) 20 cm दूर, f = +10 cm (convex)

Given: u = −20 cm (object is on left, so negative)
f = +10 cm (convex lens, so positive)
Find: v = ?, m = ?

Apply the Lens Formula:

1/v − 1/u = 1/f

Substituting values:

1/v − 1/(−20) = 1/10

Simplify:

1/v + 1/20 = 1/10
1/v = 1/10 − 1/20 = 2/20 − 1/20 = 1/20

∴ v = +20 cm (positive → real image, right side)

Calculate Magnification:

m = v/u = 20/(−20) = −1

∵ m = −1 → image is same size, inverted (real & inverted)

Object is at 2F → image forms at 2F on other side ✓

∴ Conclusion:
• Image distance v = +20 cm
• Magnification m = −1
• Image is Real, Inverted, Same Size
• Image forms at 2F on other side of lens
