Refraction (अपवर्तन)
Light bends when passing between media of different densities due to change in speed.
प्रकाश माध्यम बदलने पर मुड़ता है
📐
Normal (अभिलंब)
A perpendicular line at the point of incidence. Angles are measured from the normal.
अपवर्तन सतह पर लंबवत रेखा
🏎️
Speed Change (चाल)
Light slows down in denser media. n = c/v. Higher n → lower speed → more bending.
सघन माध्यम में चाल कम होती है
🔺
Prism (प्रिज्म)
Prism splits white light into spectrum (VIBGYOR) due to dispersion. Each color bends differently.
श्वेत प्रकाश सात रंगों में बँटता है
