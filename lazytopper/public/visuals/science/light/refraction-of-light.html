📐 स्नेल का नियम | Snell's Law KEY FORMULA
n₁ sin θ₁ = n₂ sin θ₂
n₁ = Refractive index of medium 1  |  θ₁ = Angle of incidence (आपतन कोण)
n₂ = Refractive index of medium 2  |  θ₂ = Angle of refraction (अपवर्तन कोण)
n = c / v = sin i / sin r
n = अपवर्तनांक (Refractive Index)  |  c = Speed of light in vacuum (3×10⁸ m/s)
v = Speed of light in medium  |  i = angle of incidence  |  r = angle of refraction

🔢 Worked Example हल किया उदाहरण
Question: A ray of light passes from air into glass. The angle of incidence is 30°. The refractive index of glass is 1.5. Find the angle of refraction.
(वायु से काँच में किरण जाती है। आपतन कोण = 30°, n_glass = 1.5. अपवर्तन कोण ज्ञात करो।)
💡 Key Rules to Remember मुख्य नियम
Rule 1: Denser Medium → Bends Towards Normal
When light goes from rarer (air) → denser (glass/water): ∠r < ∠i  |  Speed decreases, wavelength decreases
Rule 2: Rarer Medium → Bends Away from Normal
When light goes from denser (glass) → rarer (air): ∠r > ∠i  |  Speed increases, wavelength increases
Rule 3: Normal Incidence → No Bending
If ∠i = 0°, then ∠r = 0°. Light passes straight through the boundary. (अभिलंब पर कोई मोड़ नहीं)
Rule 4: Total Internal Reflection (पूर्ण आंतरिक परावर्तन)
When dense → rare AND ∠i > Critical Angle → No refraction, complete reflection. Used in optical fibers!
