⚖️ Law of Reflection (परावर्तन के नियम)

1
The angle of incidence (∠i) is always equal to the angle of reflection (∠r).
आपतन कोण = परावर्तन कोण
2
The incident ray, the normal at the point of incidence, and the reflected ray — all lie in the same plane.
∠i = ∠r
Angle of Incidence = Angle of Reflection

📐 Mirror Formula (दर्पण सूत्र)

1/f = 1/v + 1/u
f = focal length | v = image distance | u = object distance
m = -v/u = h'/h
m = magnification | h' = image height | h = object height
−
Distances in direction opposite to incident light (u is always −ve)
+
Distances in direction of incident light (real images, concave f)
∠ Normal
All angles measured from normal, not the surface
Convex f
Focal length of convex mirror is always +ve

✏️ Worked Example (हल किया उदाहरण)

Question: An object is placed 30 cm in front of a concave mirror of focal length 15 cm. Find the image distance and magnification.
STEP 1 – Write Given Data
Object distance: u = −30 cm (always negative, sign convention)
Focal length: f = −15 cm (concave mirror, negative)
Image distance: v = ? (to find)
STEP 2 – Apply Mirror Formula
1/f = 1/v + 1/u
1/(−15) = 1/v + 1/(−30)
−1/15 = 1/v − 1/30
1/v = −1/15 + 1/30
1/v = −2/30 + 1/30 = −1/30
STEP 3 – Solve for v
1/v = −1/30
∴ v = −30 cm
Negative sign means the image is formed on the SAME side as the object (Real image)
✅ ANSWER – Magnification
m = −v/u = −(−30)/(−30) = −1
m = −1 → Image is real, inverted, same size as object
Image distance v = −30 cm
∴ प्रतिबिम्ब वास्तविक, उल्टा और समान आकार का है।
