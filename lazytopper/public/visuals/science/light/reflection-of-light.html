📚 Step-by-Step Concepts

Click each step to reveal

STEP 1 – What is Reflection?
When light hits a smooth surface and bounces back, it's called Reflection of Light.
जब प्रकाश एक चिकनी सतह से टकराकर वापस लौटता है, उसे परावर्तन कहते हैं।
STEP 2 – Key Terms
• Incident Ray – Ray falling on mirror (आपतित किरण)
• Reflected Ray – Ray bouncing back (परावर्तित किरण)
• Normal – Perpendicular to mirror at point of incidence (अभिलंब)
• Point of Incidence – Where ray meets mirror
STEP 3 – Mirror Formula
For curved mirrors: 1/v + 1/u = 1/f
Where: v = image distance, u = object distance, f = focal length
Magnification: m = -v/u = h'/h
दर्पण सूत्र: 1/v + 1/u = 1/f
STEP 4 – Sign Convention
New Cartesian Sign Convention (नई कार्तेसियन चिह्न परिपाटी):
• All distances from Pole (P) of mirror
• Distance in direction of incident light = Positive (+)
• Distance opposite to incident light = Negative (−)
• Heights above principal axis = Positive

✏️ Worked Example

📘 Problem (CBSE Type)
An object is placed 15 cm in front of a concave mirror of focal length 10 cm. Find the image distance and magnification.

Given: u = −15 cm, f = −10 cm (concave, sign convention)
Formula: 1/v + 1/u = 1/f
➜ 1/v = 1/f − 1/u = 1/(−10) − 1/(−15)
➜ 1/v = −1/10 + 1/15 = (−3+2)/30 = −1/30
➜ v = −30 cm (Real, Inverted image, same side as object)
Magnification: m = −v/u = −(−30)/(−15) = −2
∴ Image is Real, Inverted, Magnified (×2)

Plane Mirror (समतल दर्पण)
• Image: Virtual, Erect, Same size (m = +1)
• Lateral inversion (पार्श्व उलटाव)
• Image distance = Object distance
• Use: Looking glass, periscope
Concave Mirror (अवतल दर्पण)
• Converging mirror (अभिसारी दर्पण)
• Can form Real & Virtual images
• Object at C → Real, Inverted, Same size
• Object at F → Image at infinity
• Use: Torches, shaving mirrors, headlights
Convex Mirror (उत्तल दर्पण)
• Diverging mirror (अपसारी दर्पण)
• Always: Virtual, Erect, Diminished (m < 1)
• Wide field of view
• Use: Rear-view mirrors, security mirrors
