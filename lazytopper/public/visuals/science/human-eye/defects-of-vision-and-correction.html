🔴 Myopia (निकट दृष्टि दोष) Short-Sightedness

A person with myopia can see near objects clearly but distant objects appear blurry. The far point shifts closer.

📌 Causes (कारण)

• Elongation of eyeball (most common)
• Excessive curvature of eye lens
• Loss of flexibility of lens

P = −ve (Concave Lens)
Correction: Concave lens of suitable focal length | अवतल लेंस
Power is NEGATIVE for concave lens

🟡 Hypermetropia (दूर दृष्टि दोष) Long-Sightedness

A person with hypermetropia can see distant objects clearly but near objects appear blurry. Near point shifts away from 25 cm.

📌 Causes (कारण)

• Shortening of eyeball
• Decreased curvature of eye lens
• Focal length of lens becomes too long

P = +ve (Convex Lens)
Correction: Convex lens of suitable focal length | उत्तल लेंस
Power is POSITIVE for convex lens

🟢 Presbyopia (जरा दूरदर्शिता) Age-Related

A natural age-related change in vision. Usually appears after age 40-45 years. The ciliary muscles weaken and the elasticity of eye lens decreases.
