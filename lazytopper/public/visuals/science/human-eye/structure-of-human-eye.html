📚 Step-by-Step: How We See — दृष्टि कैसे होती है

1Light enters through Cornea (कॉर्निया)
Light from an object first hits the Cornea. The cornea is transparent and acts as the primary refracting surface. It provides about 2/3rd of the eye's total focusing power. The cornea refracts (bends) the incoming light rays.
2Pupil controls light amount (पुतली)
After cornea, light passes through the Pupil — the dark circular opening in the centre of the Iris. The Iris (coloured part) controls the size of the pupil. In bright light → pupil contracts. In dim light → pupil dilates. This is called the Iris Reflex.
3Lens focuses light (क्रिस्टलीय लेंस)
The crystalline lens (biconvex lens) focuses the refracted light precisely onto the retina. Its curvature changes due to ciliary muscles — this is called Accommodation (समंजन). For near objects: lens becomes thicker (more curved). For far objects: lens becomes thinner (less curved).
4Image forms on Retina (दृष्टिपटल)
The lens forms a real, inverted, diminished image on the Retina. The retina contains photoreceptors: Rods (for dim light, black & white) and Cones (for bright light, colour vision). The fovea (yellow spot) at the centre gives sharpest vision.
5Signal to Brain (मस्तिष्क को संकेत)
Photoreceptors convert light into electrical signals. These signals travel through the Optic Nerve (दृष्टि तंत्रिका) to the brain. The brain interprets these signals and makes us "see" the object as upright and colourful. ✅ This completes the process of vision!
