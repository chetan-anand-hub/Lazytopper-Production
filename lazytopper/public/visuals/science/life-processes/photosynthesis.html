6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂
↑ Chlorophyll + Sunlight required ↑
6CO₂ = Carbon Dioxide 6H₂O = Water C₆H₁₂O₆ = Glucose 6O₂ = Oxygen released

Sunlight
सूर्य का प्रकाश
Energy source. Chlorophyll absorbs mainly red & blue wavelengths of light. Without it, photosynthesis stops!
💨
Carbon Dioxide
कार्बन डाइऑक्साइड
Enters through stomata (रंध्र). From air (0.04%). Used to build glucose molecules.
💧
Water (H₂O)
जल
Absorbed by roots → transported via xylem. Water molecules are split to release O₂ (photolysis).
🟢
Chlorophyll
पर्णहरित
Green pigment in chloroplasts. Traps sunlight. Gives leaves green color. Found in mesophyll cells.
📋 Step-by-Step Process | चरण दर चरण

👆 Click each step to reveal details

1 Light Absorption | प्रकाश अवशोषण
Chlorophyll (पर्णहरित) in chloroplasts traps sunlight energy. This happens in the thylakoid membranes during the Light Reaction (प्रकाश अभिक्रिया). Water is split: 2H₂O → 4H⁺ + 4e⁻ + O₂ ↑
2 CO₂ Absorption | CO₂ अवशोषण
Carbon dioxide from air enters through stomata (रंध्र). Guard cells (द्वार कोशिकाएँ) control stomata opening. CO₂ dissolved in water reaches mesophyll cells.
3 Dark Reaction | अंधेरे अभिक्रिया (Calvin Cycle)
In the stroma of chloroplast, CO₂ combines with a 5-carbon compound (RuBP). Using ATP energy from light reaction, glucose (C₆H₁₂O₆) is synthesized. Called Calvin Cycle.
4 Products Released | उत्पाद
Glucose (ग्लूकोज) is stored or used for energy. Oxygen (ऑक्सीजन) is released through stomata as byproduct. 6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂
