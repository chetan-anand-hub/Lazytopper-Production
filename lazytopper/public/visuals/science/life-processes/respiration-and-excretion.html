🔬 What is Respiration? (श्वसन)

Respiration is the biochemical process of breaking down food (glucose) to release energy in the form of ATP.

C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ → 6CO₂ + 6H₂O + Energy (ATP)
ग्लूकोज + ऑक्सीजन → कार्बन डाइऑक्साइड + जल + ऊर्जा

Aerobic → 36–38 ATP molecules per glucose

🪜 Step-by-Step: How Breathing Works

Step 1
Air enters through nose/mouth → cleaned, warmed, moistened
Step 2
Air passes through pharynx → larynx → trachea
Step 3
Trachea splits into two bronchi leading to each lung
Step 4
Bronchi branch into bronchioles ending in tiny alveoli (वायुकोश)
Step 5
Gas Exchange: O₂ from alveoli → blood; CO₂ from blood → alveoli
Step 6
Diaphragm contracts/relaxes to create breathing movements (Inhalation/Exhalation)

⚡ Aerobic vs Anaerobic Respiration

✅ Aerobic (वायवीय)

  • Requires O₂
  • Complete oxidation
  • 36–38 ATP produced
  • Products: CO₂ + H₂O
  • Location: Mitochondria
  • E.g., Human cells

⚡ Anaerobic (अवायवीय)

  • No O₂ needed
  • Incomplete oxidation
  • Only 2 ATP produced
  • Products: Lactic acid / Ethanol+CO₂
  • Location: Cytoplasm
  • E.g., Yeast, muscles

🧪 Anaerobic in Yeast (खमीर) vs Muscles

🍺 In Yeast (Fermentation)
Glucose → Ethanol + CO₂ + 2ATP
Used in: Bread, Beer, Wine making
💪 In Muscle Cells
Glucose → Lactic Acid + 2ATP
Causes: Muscle cramps during exercise!

🔄 Stages of Aerobic Respiration

🍬 Glucose (C₆H₁₂O₆)
↓ Glycolysis (in Cytoplasm)
2 Pyruvate + 2 ATP + 2 NADH
↓ Krebs Cycle (in Mitochondria)
CO₂ + NADH + FADH₂ + 2 ATP
↓ Electron Transport Chain
⚡ ~34 ATP + H₂O

Total ≈ 36–38 ATP per glucose molecule
