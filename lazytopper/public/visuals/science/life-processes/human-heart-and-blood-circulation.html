💡 Double Circulation (द्विपरिसंचरण)

Blood passes through the heart TWICE in one complete circuit.
Right side → Lungs → Left side → Body → Right side

🔵 Right Atrium (दायां आलिंद)

दायां अलिंद

Receives deoxygenated blood from body via Superior & Inferior Vena Cava. Pumps to Right Ventricle.

🔴 Left Atrium (बायां आलिंद)

बायां अलिंद

Receives oxygenated blood from lungs via Pulmonary Veins. Pumps to Left Ventricle.

🔵 Right Ventricle (दायां निलय)

दायां निलय

Pumps deoxygenated blood to lungs via Pulmonary Artery for oxygenation.

🔴 Left Ventricle (बायां निलय)

बायां निलय

Pumps oxygenated blood to entire body via Aorta. Has thickest walls!

दैहिक परिसंचरण
Heart ↔ Body
1

Body → Right Atrium (RA)

Deoxygenated blood from body enters Right Atrium through Superior & Inferior Vena Cava (महाशिरा)

2

RA → Right Ventricle (RV)

Blood flows from RA to RV through the Tricuspid valve (त्रिवलन वाल्व)

3

RV → Lungs (Pulmonary Circulation)

RV pumps blood to lungs via Pulmonary Artery (फुफ्फुसीय धमनी). CO₂ released, O₂ absorbed.

4

Lungs → Left Atrium (LA)

Oxygenated blood returns to LA through Pulmonary Veins (फुफ्फुसीय शिरा)

5

LA → Left Ventricle (LV)

Blood flows from LA to LV through Bicuspid/Mitral valve (द्विवलन वाल्व)

6

LV → Entire Body (Systemic Circulation)

LV pumps oxygenated blood to body via Aorta (महाधमनी). Cycle completes
