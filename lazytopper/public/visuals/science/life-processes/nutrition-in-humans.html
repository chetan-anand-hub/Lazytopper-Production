📌 Core Concept

Humans are heterotrophs — they cannot make their own food. Food (complex molecules) must be broken down into simpler, absorbable molecules through digestion via the alimentary canal (आहार नाल).

📐 Surface Area Formula

Villi increase surface area ≈ 200 m² (size of a tennis court!) for efficient absorption. This is why small intestine is 6–7 metres long.
