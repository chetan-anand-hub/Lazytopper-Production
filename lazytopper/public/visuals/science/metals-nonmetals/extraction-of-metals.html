📋 Key Concepts Summary CBSE Exam

🪨 Ore vs Mineral

Mineral = naturally found metal compound. Ore = mineral from which metal can be profitably extracted. अयस्क vs खनिज

🧹 Concentration of Ore

Removal of gangue (impurities) from ore. Methods: Hydraulic washing, Froth flotation, Magnetic separation. अयस्क का सांद्रण

🔥 Calcination vs Roasting

Calcination: limited air, carbonates. Roasting: excess air, sulphides. Both give metal oxide.

⚡ Refining

Electrolytic refining purifies impure metals. Impure metal = anode, pure metal = cathode, salt solution = electrolyte. शोधन
