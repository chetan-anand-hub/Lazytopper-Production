What is Ionic Bond? (आयनिक बंध)

A chemical bond formed by the complete transfer of one or more electrons from a metal atom to a non-metal atom, creating oppositely charged ions that attract each other.

Metal → loses e⁻ → Cation (धनायन)  |  Non-Metal → gains e⁻ → Anion (ऋणायन)
Na (Metal) + Cl (Non-Metal) → Na⁺ + Cl⁻ → NaCl (Salt)

🔢 Step-by-Step | चरण-दर-चरण
1
Identify Atoms: Na has 2,8,1 configuration. It has only 1 valence electron (संयोजकता इलेक्ट्रॉन). Cl has 2,8,7 — needs just 1 more to complete its octet.
2
Octet Rule (अष्टक नियम): Every atom wants 8 electrons in its outermost shell (noble gas configuration). Na wants to lose 1e⁻, Cl wants to gain 1e⁻.
3
Electron Transfer: Na transfers its 1 valence electron to Cl. Now: Na has 10e⁻, 11p⁺ → Net charge = +1 (becomes Na⁺ cation, धनायन)
4
Anion Formation: Cl now has 18e⁻, 17p⁺ → Net charge = -1 (becomes Cl⁻ anion, ऋणायन). Configuration: 2,8,8 ✓ Octet complete!
5
Bond Formation: Na⁺ and Cl⁻ are attracted by electrostatic force (स्थिरविद्युत बल). This attraction IS the ionic bond! Result: NaCl (Sodium Chloride = Common Salt)
