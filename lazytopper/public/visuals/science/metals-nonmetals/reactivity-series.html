मुख्य अवधारणा — Core Concept

The Reactivity Series arranges metals in decreasing order of their reactivity (tendency to lose electrons / form positive ions).

More Reactive Metal → Displaces Less Reactive Metal from its salt solution

ऊपर का धातु → नीचे के धातु को विस्थापित करता है ✓

📌 Rule: Metal A + Salt of B → Salt of A + Metal B  |  Only if A is MORE reactive than B

📝 Worked Example — हल किया उदाहरण
❓ Question: Will Iron (Fe) displace Copper (Cu) from Copper Sulphate (CuSO₄) solution?
क्या लोहा, कॉपर सल्फेट से तांबे को विस्थापित करेगा?
1
Find positions in Reactivity Series
अभिक्रियाशीलता श्रेणी में स्थान देखें
Fe → Position 7 (from top)
Cu → Position 9 (from top)
∴ Fe is ABOVE Cu in the series
2
Apply the Displacement Rule
विस्थापन नियम लगाएं
If Metal A is above Metal B → A displaces B
Fe is above Cu → Fe CAN displace Cu ✓
3
Write the Chemical Equation
रासायनिक समीकरण लिखें
Fe + CuSO₄ → FeSO₄ + Cu
Iron + Copper Sulphate → Iron Sulphate + Copper
4
Observe the Changes
परिवर्तन देखें
• Blue CuSO₄ solution turns GREEN (FeSO₄)
• Reddish-brown Cu deposits on Fe nail
• Fe nail loses mass slightly
5
✅ Conclusion — निष्कर्ष
YES! Fe displaces Cu from CuSO₄.
∵ Fe is more reactive than Cu.
∴ Fe + CuSO₄ → FeSO₄ + Cu ✓
