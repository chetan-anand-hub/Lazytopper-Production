❓ Question:

A solution has [H⁺] = 0.001 mol/L. Find the pH and state whether it is acidic, basic, or neutral.

⚡ Quick Facts to Remember
  • 🔴 Strong acids (HCl, H₂SO₄) → pH near 0–2
  • 🔵 Strong bases (NaOH, KOH) → pH near 12–14
  • 🟢 Pure water → pH = 7 (neutral)
  • 🌿 Litmus: Red in acid, Blue in base (लिटमस)
  • 🌸 Phenolphthalein: Colourless in acid, Pink in base
  • ⚠️ pH scale range: 0 to 14 (logarithmic / लघुगणकीय)
