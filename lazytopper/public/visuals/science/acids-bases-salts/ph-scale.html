📐 Key Concept pH क्या है?
pH = −log₁₀[H⁺]
pH is the negative logarithm of hydrogen ion concentration
⚡ Higher [H⁺] → Lower pH → More Acidic
🔴Acidic (अम्लीय): pH < 7 → More H⁺ ions in solution
🟢Neutral (उदासीन): pH = 7 → Equal H⁺ and OH⁻ ions (e.g., pure water)
🔵Basic/Alkaline (क्षारीय): pH > 7 → More OH⁻ ions in solution

📖 Worked Example Step-by-step
Question: A solution turns blue litmus red. Its pH is likely to be:
(A) 3   (B) 7   (C) 9   (D) 11
1 Understand the clue: Blue litmus turns red → the solution is acidic (अम्लीय)
2 Apply pH rule: Acidic solutions have pH < 7
3 Check options: A = 3 ✓ (acidic), B = 7 (neutral), C = 9 (basic), D = 11 (basic)
✓ Answer: (A) pH = 3 — It is acidic. pH < 7 means acidic, so blue litmus turns red. ∴ Option A is correct!
