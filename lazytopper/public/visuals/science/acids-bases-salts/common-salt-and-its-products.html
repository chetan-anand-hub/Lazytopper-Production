🟢 Sodium Hydroxide — NaOH (Caustic Soda / कास्टिक सोडा)

2NaCl(aq) + 2H₂O(l) → 2NaOH(aq) + Cl₂(g) + H₂(g)

Nature: Strong base (क्षार) | Highly corrosive

Uses (उपयोग):

🧴 Soap making📰 Paper making🧵 Textile🧪 De-greasing metals

⚠️ When Cl₂ and NaOH react → Bleaching Liquid (NaOCl) is formed

🟡 Chlorine Gas — Cl₂ (क्लोरीन गैस)

Produced at ANODE during electrolysis of brine

Nature: Greenish-yellow gas | Toxic | Strong oxidising agent

Uses (उपयोग):

🏊 Water purification🧴 Bleaching powder💊 PVC plastics🧪 Disinfectants
Cl₂ + Ca(OH)₂ → CaOCl₂ (Bleaching Powder / विरंजक चूर्ण)

🟠 Baking Soda — NaHCO₃ (बेकिंग सोडा)

NaCl + H₂O + CO₂ + NH₃ → NH₄Cl + NaHCO₃ (Solvay Process)

Full name: Sodium Hydrogen Carbonate (सोडियम हाइड्रोजन कार्बोनेट)

On heating:

2NaHCO₃ → Na₂CO₃ + H₂O + CO₂↑

Uses:

🍞 Baking (CO₂ makes bread fluffy)💊 Antacid🧯 Fire extinguisher

🟣 Washing Soda — Na₂CO₃·10H₂O (धुलाई सोडा)

NaHCO₃ → (heat) → Na₂CO₃ → (dissolved + recrystallised) → Na₂CO₃·10H₂O

Full name: Sodium Carbonate Decahydrate (सोडियम कार्बोनेट डेकाहाइड्रेट)

Nature: Basic salt | Alkaline solution

Uses:

🧺 Laundry (removes hardness)🏭 Glass making🧪 Borax manufacture
