📋 Worked Example — Step by Step Click Next!
Problem: When HCl reacts with NaOH, write the balanced equation and identify products.
Step 1
Identify the Reactants
HCl = Hydrochloric Acid (अम्ल) → Gives H⁺ ions in water
NaOH = Sodium Hydroxide (क्षार) → Gives OH⁻ ions in water
∴ This is an Acid + Base reaction!
Step 2
Write the Unbalanced Equation
HCl + NaOH → NaCl + H₂O
Count atoms: H:2, Cl:1, Na:1, O:1 on each side ✓
∵ Already balanced! (1:1 ratio)
Step 3
Identify the Products
NaCl = Sodium Chloride = Common Table Salt (लवण / नमक)
H₂O = Water (जल)
Heat is also released → Exothermic (ऊष्माक्षेपी)
Step 4
Check pH of Products
NaCl solution → pH = 7 (Neutral salt)
Strong acid + Strong base → Neutral salt
∴ Neutralisation is complete! उदासीनीकरण हुआ ✓
Step 5
Final Balanced Equation ✅
HCl(aq) + NaOH(aq) → NaCl(aq) + H₂O(l) + Heat
Acid + Base → Salt + Water | उदासीनीकरण अभिक्रिया
📚 More Neutralisation Examples
H₂SO₄ + 2KOH → K₂SO₄ + 2H₂O
Sulphuric Acid + Potassium Hydroxide → Potassium Sulphate (salt) + Water. Salt type: Normal salt (pH = 7)
HNO₃ + NaOH → NaNO₃ + H₂O
Nitric Acid + Sodium Hydroxide → Sodium Nitrate + Water. Used in fertilisers!
HCl + NH₄OH → NH₄Cl + H₂O
Hydrochloric Acid + Ammonium Hydroxide → Ammonium Chloride + Water. NH₄Cl is used in dry cells!
