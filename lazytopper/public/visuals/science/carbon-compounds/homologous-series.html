🔑 मुख्य अवधारणा (Key Concept) Core
Each member differs by

Molecular mass difference = 14 u  |  Formula: CₙH₂ₙ₊₂ (alkanes)

A Homologous Series is a group of organic compounds that have the same general formula, similar chemical properties, and differ from each other by —CH₂— (methylene group) with a mass difference of 14 u.

📐 Structural Formula (संरचनात्मक सूत्र)

Methane → Ethane → Propane — each step adds one —CH₂—

∴ Each member = previous member + CH₂ (14 u)

🪜 Worked Example (हल किया उदाहरण)
Q: What is the molecular formula of the 5th member of the alkane series? What is its molecular mass?
Step 1: Use General Formula
Alkanes follow: CₙH₂ₙ₊₂
For 5th member, n = 5
Step 2: Calculate H atoms
H = 2n + 2 = 2(5) + 2 = 12
∴ Formula = C₅H₁₂ (Pentane)
Step 3: Calculate Molecular Mass
Mass of C = 5 × 12 = 60 u
Mass of H = 12 × 1 = 12 u
Total = 60 + 12 = 72 u
✅ Verification
Butane (C₄H₁₀, 58 u) + CH₂ (14 u) = Pentane (72 u) ✓
∴ Each consecutive member differs by 14 u (CH₂)
