🧮 Worked Example Step-by-step

How does CH₄ satisfy octet rule?
Given: Carbon (2,4) + 4 × Hydrogen (1)
∵ C needs 4 more e⁻ to complete octet
∵ Each H needs 1 more e⁻ to complete duplet
∴ C shares 1 e⁻ with each of 4 H atoms
∴ C gets: 4 + 4 = 8 electrons ✓ (octet)
∴ Each H gets: 1 + 1 = 2 electrons ✓ (duplet)
CH₄ formula: C + 4H → CH₄
4 shared pairs = 4 single covalent bonds (एकल सहसंयोजक बंध)
