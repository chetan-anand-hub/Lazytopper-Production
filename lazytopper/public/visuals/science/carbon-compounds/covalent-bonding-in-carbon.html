How Covalent Bond Forms | बंध कैसे बनता है

Each carbon has 4 valence electrons — needs 4 more to complete octet (अष्टक पूर्ण करना)
Carbon shares electrons with other atoms — neither gains nor loses (इलेक्ट्रॉन साझा करता है)
Each shared pair = one covalent bond shown as a line (—) (सहसंयोजक बंध)
Carbon can bond with C, H, O, N, Cl forming millions of compounds (अनगिनत यौगिक)
