⚛️ Carbon Chain Structures

कार्बन श्रृंखला संरचनाएँ | Isomers & Carbon Compounds

🔑 Key Concept

Carbon (C) can form straight, branched, and cyclic chains. Same molecular formula → different structures = Isomers (समावयवी)

CₙH₂ₙ₊₂ Alkanes   CₙH₂ₙ Alkenes   CₙH₂ₙ₋₂ Alkynes

📏 Straight Chain (सीधी श्रृंखला) — Normal Chain

💡 Remember: In straight chain, each carbon is connected to the next in a single continuous line. Also called n-alkane (n = normal/सामान्य).

🌿 Branched Chain (शाखित श्रृंखला)

Carbon chain has one or more side branches. Click on a molecule to explore!

💡 Key Point: Branched chains have lower boiling points than straight-chain isomers because they are more compact (less surface area).

🔄 Cyclic Chain (चक्रीय श्रृंखला)

🔥 Benzene is special! It has alternating single & double bonds (aromatic / सुगंधित). Formula: C₆H₆. It's the parent of aromatic compounds.

🔀 Structural Isomers (संरचनात्मक समावयवी)

Same molecular formula, different structural formula = Structural Isomers

∴ Different physical & chemical properties

Step 1: Take molecular formula C₄H₁₀ (Butane / ब्यूटेन)
Formula check: CₙH₂ₙ₊₂ → n=4 → 4×2+2 = 10 H ✓
Step 2: Structure 1 — n-Butane (Straight Chain / सीधी श्रृंखला)
Step 3: Structure 2 — Isobutane (Branched Chain / शाखित श्रृंखला)
Step 4: Both have formula C₄H₁₀ but different structures!
∴ They are Structural Isomers (संरचनात्मक समावयवी)
n-Butane bp: -0.5°C  |  Isobutane bp: -11.7°C (different properties!)
