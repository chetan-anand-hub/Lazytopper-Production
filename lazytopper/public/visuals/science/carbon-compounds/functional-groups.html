📖 What is a Functional Group? क्रियाशील समूह

A functional group is a specific atom or group of atoms in an organic compound that gives it its characteristic chemical properties. All compounds having the same functional group undergo similar chemical reactions.

💡 Think of it like: Functional groups are like "personality traits" of organic molecules — they decide how the molecule behaves!

📝 Worked Example Step-by-Step

Question: Identify the functional group in CH₃COOH and name the compound.

Step 1 — पहचान करें
🔍 Write out the full structure: CH₃ — C(=O) — OH
We see two parts: a C=O group AND an OH group connected to the SAME carbon.
Step 2 — समूह खोजें
⚗️ When C=O and —OH are on the SAME carbon, they together form: —COOH (Carboxyl Group | कार्बोक्सिल समूह)
Step 3 — नाम दें
📛 The compound has 2 carbons (eth-) + carboxylic acid suffix (-oic acid)
∴ Name = Ethanoic Acid (एथेनोइक अम्ल)
Step 4 — गुण जानें
🧴 Ethanoic acid is commonly called Acetic Acid. Its dilute solution is Vinegar (सिरका) used in cooking. It turns blue litmus → Red (Acidic)
✅ Final Answer
CH₃COOH → Functional Group = —COOH (Carboxyl)
Name = Ethanoic Acid | Acetic Acid
∴ It belongs to the Carboxylic Acid family! 🎉

🧠 Memory Tips याद रखें
🍺 Alcohol: Think "O-H stands for Oh Hello!" — The OH group greets water molecules (water soluble!)
🔚 Aldehyde: "Al-de-HIDE at the END" — Aldehyde always hides at the end of the chain!
🎯 Ketone: "Ketone is in the Middle Zone" — C=O always in the CENTER of chain
🍋 Carboxylic Acid: "COOH = CO + OH together = SOUR!" — Vinegar, lemon juice!
