🔬 What is a Chemical Reaction? (रासायनिक अभिक्रिया)

A chemical reaction involves the transformation of reactants (अभिकारक) into products (उत्पाद). Atoms are rearranged but never created or destroyed.

General Form: Reactants → Products

अभिकारक → उत्पाद
