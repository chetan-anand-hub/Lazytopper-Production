What is Corrosion?

When metals are exposed to moisture, oxygen, or chemicals, their surface is slowly eaten away. This is called corrosion. Iron rusting is the most common example.

4Fe + 3O₂ + 6H₂O → 4Fe(OH)₃ (Rust / जंग)

Rust = Hydrated Iron(III) Oxide = Fe₂O₃·xH₂O

⚡ Galvanisation (जस्तीकरण) — Step by Step

Click to reveal each step:

1Iron object is cleaned thoroughly to remove dirt & rust.
2Iron is dipped in molten Zinc (Zn) at ~450°C
3Zinc forms a thin protective layer on iron surface
4Zinc itself oxidizes: 2Zn + O₂ → 2ZnO, protecting iron below
5Result: Galvanised Iron — used in buckets, pipes, roofing!

विकृतगंधिता | Rancidity

What is Rancidity?

When fats and oils in food are oxidised by oxygen, they produce unpleasant smell and taste. This is called rancidity (विकृतगंधिता).

Fats/Oils + O₂ → Rancid Products
(Aldehydes, ketones — bad smell)
