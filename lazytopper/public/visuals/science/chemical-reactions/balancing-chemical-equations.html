⚖️ Law of Conservation of Mass (द्रव्यमान संरक्षण का नियम)

In a chemical reaction, mass is neither created nor destroyed.
∴ Atoms on Reactant side = Atoms on Product side

🔬 Why Balance Equations?

Unbalanced equations violate the law of conservation of mass!

📌 Golden Rules for Balancing (याद करें!)

Rule 1: Change only coefficients (numbers before formulas), NOT subscripts inside formulas.
Rule 2: Count atoms of each element on both sides. Use a table!
Rule 3: Start balancing with the element that appears in the fewest compounds.
Rule 4: Balance H and O last (they appear in most compounds).
Reactants (अभिकारक) ⇌ Products (उत्पाद)
Atoms balanced ∴ Law of Conservation of Mass satisfied ✓
