🔬 What is Balancing?

A chemical equation is balanced when the number of atoms of each element is equal on both sides — Reactants (अभिकारक) and Products (उत्पाद). We use coefficients (गुणांक) to balance equations.

⚖️ Law of Conservation of Mass | द्रव्यमान संरक्षण का नियम

Matter is neither created nor destroyed in a chemical reaction.
∴ Atoms on LEFT side = Atoms on RIGHT side

💡 Key Rule: We can only change coefficients (numbers in front of molecules), NEVER the subscripts (small numbers inside the formula).

📝 Quick Tips (परीक्षा के लिए)
Step 1: Write the unbalanced equation first
Step 2: Count atoms of each element on both sides
Step 3: Use Hit & Trial method — adjust coefficients only!
Step 4: Verify: count all atoms again on both sides
Remember: NEVER change subscripts — only change coefficients!
