🌸 Flower Structure & Pollination | पुष्प संरचना और परागण

CBSE Class 10 Science | Chapter: How do Organisms Reproduce?

🔑 Core Concept: A flower is the reproductive structure of a plant. It contains male parts (stamen/पुंकेसर) that produce pollen and female parts (pistil/स्त्रीकेसर) that receive pollen. Transfer of pollen from anther to stigma is called pollination (परागण).

🐝 Pollination Types | परागण के प्रकार
Pollination (परागण) = Transfer of pollen grains from anther (परागकोश) to stigma (वर्तिकाग्र)
Self-Pollination (स्व-परागण): same flower or same plant  |  Cross-Pollination (पर-परागण): different plant
