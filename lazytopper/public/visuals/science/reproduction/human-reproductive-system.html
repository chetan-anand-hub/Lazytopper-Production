CBSE Class 10 Science • Chapter: How do Organisms Reproduce?

🔑 Key Concept | मुख्य अवधारणा

Sexual Reproduction in humans involves fusion of male gamete (sperm / शुक्राणु) and female gamete (ovum / अंडाणु) to form a zygote (युग्मनज) which develops into a new individual.

🧩 Why Reproduce Sexually?

✅ Advantages
Genetic variation → better adaptability
Two parents → stronger offspring
📌 Key Fact
Humans are viviparous — young develop inside mother's body

♂ Male Reproductive System | पुरुष जनन तंत्र

Primary function: produce sperms (शुक्राणु) and deliver them for fertilization. Testes (वृषण) are the primary sex organs.
