🔑 Key Concept अवधारणा

Asexual Reproduction (अलैंगिक जनन):
A single parent produces offspring identical to itself — no fusion of gametes. Offspring are genetically identical (clones).

⚡ Binary Fission द्विखंडन द्विखंडन

Definition: The parent cell divides into two equal daughter cells. Each daughter cell grows into a new organism.
Example organisms: Amoeba, Paramecium, Bacteria (Leishmania)

📍 Leishmania has a whip-like flagellum — division starts from the flagellum end (binary fission along a definite plane)

📋 Step-by-Step Walkthrough

👆 Click each step to reveal details

Step 1: DNA Replication ›
The DNA of the parent cell duplicates (replicates). This ensures each new cell gets a complete copy of genetic material.
🔑 No spindle fibres in prokaryotes (like bacteria)
Step 2: Elongation of Cell ›
The cell grows longer. The two copies of DNA move to opposite ends of the elongated cell — this separates them.
Step 3: Cytokinesis — Cell Splits ›
A constriction forms in the middle. The cell membrane pinches inward, dividing the cytoplasm. Result: 2 equal daughter cells — each genetically identical to parent.
⚠️ Special Note: Amoeba vs Leishmania ›
Amoeba: Can divide in any plane (irregular shape).
Leishmania: Has a fixed flagellum — division occurs at a specific plane relative to the flagellum. This is a CBSE exam favourite!

🌱 Budding मुकुलन मुकुलन

Definition: A small outgrowth (bud) develops on the parent body, grows, and eventually detaches to form a new organism.
Examples: Hydra, Yeast (Saccharomyces cerevisiae)
