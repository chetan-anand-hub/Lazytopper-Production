📚 How CFCs Destroy Ozone - Step by Step Click to Reveal
CFC ओज़ोन को कैसे नष्ट करते हैं? (CBSE Important!)
Step 1: CFC Released 🏭
Chlorofluorocarbons (CFCs) are released from refrigerators, air conditioners, and aerosol sprays. These rise into the stratosphere.
फ्रिज, AC से CFC गैसें वायुमंडल में जाती हैं।
Step 2: UV Breaks CFC ☢️
UV radiation in stratosphere breaks CFC molecules, releasing highly reactive Chlorine (Cl•) free radicals.
CFC → Cl• (क्लोरीन मुक्त मूलक)
Step 3: Chlorine Attacks Ozone ⚡
One Cl• radical destroys thousands of O₃ molecules in a chain reaction:
Cl• + O₃ → ClO + O₂
एक क्लोरीन अणु हजारों ओज़ोन अणुओं को नष्ट कर सकता है!
Step 4: Ozone Hole Forms 🕳️
The ozone layer thins, creating an "Ozone Hole" especially over Antarctica. UV-B rays now reach Earth directly.
