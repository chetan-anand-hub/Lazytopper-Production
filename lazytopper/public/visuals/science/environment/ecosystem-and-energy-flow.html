Ecosystem Components – पारितंत्र के घटक

☀️

Producer

उत्पादक

Plants, Algae

Green plants make food via photosynthesis. They form the base of every food chain.

Energy stored: 100%
🐛

Primary Consumer

प्राथमिक उपभोक्ता

Herbivores

Eat producers directly. E.g., grasshopper, rabbit, deer.

Energy: 10%
🦎

Secondary Consumer

द्वितीयक उपभोक्ता

Omnivores/Carnivores

Eat primary consumers. E.g., frog, small fish, fox.

Energy: 1%
🦅

Tertiary Consumer

तृतीयक उपभोक्ता

Top Predators

Eat secondary consumers. E.g., hawk, eagle, snake.

Energy: 0.1%
🍄

Decomposer

अपघटक

Bacteria, Fungi

Break down dead matter, return nutrients to soil. Essential for nutrient cycling.

Also called Saprotrophs (मृतजीवी)
