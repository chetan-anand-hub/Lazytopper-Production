🔑 Core Concept | मूल अवधारणा

A Food Chain shows the linear transfer of energy from one organism to another through feeding. Multiple interlinked food chains form a Food Web.

⚡ 10% Law: Only 10% of energy transfers to next trophic level
Energy at Trophic Level n = Energy at (n−1) × 0.10

खाद्य श्रृंखला में केवल 10% ऊर्जा अगले पोषण स्तर तक पहुँचती है

⚡ 10% Law | ऊर्जा का 10% नियम

Given by Lindeman (1942). Only 10% of energy at each trophic level is transferred to the next. The rest (90%) is lost as heat.

📌 Key Points for Exam | परीक्षा के लिए मुख्य बिंदु
  • Food chain always starts with green plants (Producers/Autotrophs) | हमेशा हरे पौधों से शुरू
  • Energy flows unidirectionally (one way only) | ऊर्जा एकदिशीय प्रवाहित होती है
  • 10% Law by Lindeman — only 10% energy transferred | केवल 10% ऊर्जा स्थानांतरित
  • 90% energy is lost as heat at each trophic level | 90% ऊष्मा के रूप में खर्च
  • Max 3-4 trophic levels possible due to energy loss | अधिकतम 3-4 पोषण स्तर
  • Food Web is more realistic & stable than food chain | खाद्य जाल अधिक स्थिर
  • Decomposers (Bacteria, Fungi) recycle nutrients | अपघटक पोषक तत्
