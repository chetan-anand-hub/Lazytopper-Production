💡 Key Concept पादप हार्मोन

What are Plant Hormones?

Plant hormones (पादप हार्मोन) are chemical messengers produced in small quantities that control and coordinate growth, development, and responses to the environment. Unlike animals, plants do NOT have a nervous system — hormones do all the signaling!

⚡ Golden Rule: Stimulus → Hormone Released → Differential Growth → Movement/Response

Auxin (ऑक्सिन)

वृद्धि हार्मोन
• Promotes cell elongation
• Causes phototropism & geotropism
• Produced at shoot tip (apical meristem)
• Moves from tip downward

Gibberellin (जिबरेलिन)

बौनापन-रोधी हार्मोन
• Promotes stem elongation
• Helps in seed germination
• Breaks dormancy in seeds
• Makes dwarf plants grow tall

Cytokinin (साइटोकाइनिन)

कोशिका विभाजन हार्मोन
• Promotes cell division
• Delays aging (senescence)
• Works WITH auxin
• Promotes lateral bud growth

Abscisic Acid (एब्सिसिक एसिड)

तनाव हार्मोन
• Inhibits growth (stress hormone)
• Causes stomata to CLOSE in drought
• Promotes seed dormancy
• Causes leaf fall (abscission)

Ethylene (एथिलीन) — Only Gas Hormone!

फल पकाने वाला हार्मोन
• Promotes fruit ripening 🍎
• Causes flowers to wither
• Promotes horizontal growth
• Commercially used to ripen bananas & mangoes

🎯 Quick Quiz परीक्षा अभ्यास

📊 Tropism vs Nastic Movement तुलना / Comparison

Tropism (अनुवर्तन)

• Directional response
• Depends on direction of stimulus
• Permanent movement
• Due to differential growth
• Example: Phototropism, Geotropism

Nastic Movement (नैस्टी)

• Non-directional response
• Stimulus direction doesn't matter
• Reversible movement
• Due to turgor pressure change
• Example: Touch-me-not (Mimosa)

⚠️ CBSE Tip: Mimosa pudica (छुईमुई / लाजवंती) shows NASTIC movement — NOT tropism. The leaf folds due to loss of turgor pressure, not growth!
