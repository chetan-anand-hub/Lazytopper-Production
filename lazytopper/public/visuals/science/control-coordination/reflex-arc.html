⚡ Reflex Arc — प्रतिवर्त चाप

CBSE Class 10 Science | Control and Coordination | Chapter 7

KEY CONCEPT
Stimulus → Receptor → Sensory Nerve → Spinal Cord → Motor Nerve → Effector → Response
उद्दीपन → ग्राही → संवेदी तंत्रिका → मेरुरज्जु → प्रेरक तंत्रिका → प्रभावक → अनुक्रिया

A reflex action is a rapid, automatic, involuntary response to a stimulus — it does NOT require brain processing. The signal travels through a pathway called the reflex arc.

📚 Step-by-Step Walkthrough Click each step
1
🔥 Stimulus Detected
उद्दीपन का पता चलना
A hot object touches the skin. Thermoreceptors (heat receptors) in the skin detect the extreme temperature. This is the stimulus — the starting point of the reflex arc.
2
⚡ Sensory Neuron Fires
संवेदी न्यूरॉन सक्रिय
The receptor generates a nerve impulse (electrical signal). This travels along the afferent / sensory neuron toward the spinal cord. Signal speed: ~1–100 m/s!
3
🧠 Spinal Cord Processes
मेरुरज्जु में प्रसंस्करण
The signal reaches the spinal cord — NOT the brain! A relay neuron (interneuron) inside the spinal cord processes it and instantly sends a command back. Brain is informed later.
4
📡 Motor Neuron Carries Command
प्रेरक न्यूरॉन आदेश लेता है
The spinal cord sends a command via the efferent / motor neuron to the muscle. This is the return journey of the signal — from spinal cord to the effector organ.
5
💪 Effector Responds
प्रभावक अनुक्रिया करता है
The effector (muscle) receives the motor signal and contracts — the hand is pulled away instantly! This happens BEFORE you consciously feel pain. That's the power of reflex!
6
🧠 Brain Gets Message (Later!)
मस्तिष्क को बाद में पता चलता है
AFTER the hand is already withdrawn, the signal reaches the brain → you feel pain now! The brain also helps you remember the hot object and avoid it in future.

⚖️ Reflex vs Voluntary Action
⚡ Reflex Action (प्रतिवर्त)
✓ Involuntary (अनैच्छिक)
✓ Very fast (~50ms)
✓ No brain involvement initially
✓ Controlled by Spinal Cord
✓ Examples: Knee jerk, blinking, sneezing, withdrawing from heat
🧠 Voluntary Action (ऐच्छिक)
✓ Voluntary (ऐच्छिक)
✓ Slower (~150ms+)
✓ Brain fully involved
✓ Controlled by Cerebrum
✓ Examples: Writing, walking, talking, eating
