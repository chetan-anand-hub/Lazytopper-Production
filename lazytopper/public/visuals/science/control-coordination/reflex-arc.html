Reflex Arc — Control & Coordination

Class 10 CBSE Science Ch.7
Stimulus → Receptor → Sensory Neuron → Spinal Cord → Motor Neuron → Effector → Response
उत्तेजना → ग्राही → संवेदी न्यूरॉन → मेरुरज्जु → गति न्यूरॉन → प्रभावकारक → अनुक्रिया

🔥
Receptor
ग्राही
Detects stimulus. Found in skin, eyes, ears. Converts signal to nerve impulse.
⚡
Sensory Neuron
संवेदी न्यूरॉन
Carries impulse FROM receptor TO spinal cord (afferent pathway).
🔵
Spinal Cord
मेरुरज्जु
Integration center. Relay neuron processes signal. Brain not needed!
💚
Motor Neuron
गति न्यूरॉन
Carries impulse FROM spinal cord TO effector (efferent pathway).
💪
Effector
प्रभावकारक
Muscle or gland that produces the response. Withdraws hand!

⚡ Reflex Action (प्रतिवर्त क्रिया)

  • Involuntary (अनैच्छिक)
  • Very fast response
  • Controlled by Spinal Cord
  • Brain NOT involved
  • Example: Knee jerk, blinking
  • Example: Withdrawing hand from hot object

🤔 Voluntary Action (ऐच्छिक क्रिया)

  • Voluntary (ऐच्छिक)
  • Slower response
  • Controlled by Brain
  • Brain IS involved
  • Example: Writing, walking
  • Example: Picking up a book
