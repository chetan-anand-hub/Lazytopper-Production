🔬 न्यूरॉन (Neuron) — The Nerve Cell
Key Concept
Receptor → Afferent Nerve → CNS → Efferent Nerve → Effector
Neuron = Basic structural & functional unit of Nervous System
👆 Click on any part of the neuron below to learn about it!
Types of Neurons (न्यूरॉन के प्रकार)
🟢 Sensory Neuron
(संवेदी न्यूरॉन)
Carries signals FROM receptors TO CNS. Also called Afferent neuron.
🔵 Motor Neuron
(चालक न्यूरॉन)
Carries signals FROM CNS TO effectors (muscles/glands). Also called Efferent neuron.
🟡 Relay/Inter Neuron
(अंतर-न्यूरॉन)
Connects sensory and motor neurons. Found in CNS (Brain & Spinal Cord).
Nerve Impulse Speed
~100 m/s
in myelinated fibres

Brain Functions Summary (मस्तिष्क के कार्य)
🔴 Cerebrum (प्रमस्तिष्क)
Thinking, memory, intelligence, voluntary actions, speech, hearing, vision
🔵 Cerebellum (अनुमस्तिष्क)
Balance, posture, coordination of muscular movements
🟢 Medulla Oblongata (मज्जा)
Breathing, heartbeat, blood pressure, swallowing — Vital functions
🟡 Hypothalamus
Temperature regulation, hunger, thirst, sleep, endocrine link
🛡️ Protection: Brain is protected by Cranium (skull) + 3 Meninges (Dura mater, Arachnoid, Pia mater) + CSF (Cerebrospinal Fluid)
🦴 Spinal Cord (मेरुरज्जु): Protected by Vertebral column (backbone). Controls reflex actions and connects brain to body.
⚡ Reflex Arc — अनैच्छिक क्रिया
Stimulus → Receptor → Sensory Nerve → Spinal Cord → Motor Nerve → Effector → Response
Reflex actions are automatic — Brain is NOT involved initially!
