💡 Key Points to Remember परीक्षा टिप्स
⚡ SI unit of Power = Watt (W) = Joule/second | 1kW = 1000W
🔋 SI unit of Energy = Joule (J) | Commercial unit = kWh (kilowatt-hour)
🏭 1 kWh = 1 Unit = 3.6 × 10⁶ J = 3,600,000 Joules
📊 P = VI = I²R = V²/R (all three forms for exam!)
⚠️ Higher wattage = More electricity consumed per hour
