📜 Ohm's Law Statement

"The current (I) through a conductor between two points is directly proportional to the voltage (V) across those two points — provided temperature and physical conditions remain constant."

ओम का नियम: किसी चालक में बहने वाली विद्युत धारा उस पर लगाए गए विभवांतर के समानुपाती होती है।

∴ V ∝ I → V = IR
∴ I = V/R
∴ R = V/I

✅ Key Points to Remember
  • ⚡ V = IR is Ohm's Law (ओम का नियम)
  • 📊 V-I graph is a straight line through origin → shows direct proportionality
  • 🌡️ Valid only when temperature is constant (तापमान स्थिर)
  • ⚖️ Slope of V-I graph = Resistance (R)
  • 🔬 SI unit: Voltage → Volt (V), Current → Ampere (A), Resistance → Ohm (Ω)
  • 🔗 Ohmic conductors obey this law; Diodes/LEDs do NOT (non-ohmic)
