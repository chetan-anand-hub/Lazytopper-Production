🧮 Worked Example — हल किया उदाहरण

Question: A resistor has resistance 5 Ω. If a voltage of 15 V is applied across it, find the current flowing through it. Also find the voltage if current is 4 A.
Step 0 of 4

Click "Next Step" to start solving →

Step 1 — Given (दिया गया)
Identify what is given and what is to find:
R = 5 Ω, V = 15 V, I = ?
Step 2 — Formula (सूत्र)
Apply Ohm's Law formula:
V = I × R ∴ I = V ÷ R
Step 3 — Calculation (गणना)
Substituting values:
I = 15 ÷ 5 = 3 A
Step 4 — Part 2: Find V when I = 4A
Now use V = I × R:
V = 4 × 5 = 20 V
✅ Answer: I = 3 A, and V = 20 V when I = 4 A

💡 Key Points — मुख्य बिंदु

∝ Proportionality: V ∝ I means if Voltage doubles, Current doubles (R constant / R स्थिर)
📉 Inverse: I ∝ 1/R — If Resistance increases, Current decreases (V constant)
📐 Graph: V-I graph is a straight line through origin. Slope = R (प्रतिरोध = ढाल)
⚠ Ohmic Conductor: Conductors obeying Ohm's law at constant temperature are called Ohmic conductors (ओमीय चालक)
