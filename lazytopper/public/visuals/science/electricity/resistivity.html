📖 Worked Example — Step by Step

Q: A copper wire (ρ = 1.7×10⁻⁸ Ω·m) is 5 m long with cross-sectional area 0.5 mm² (= 0.5×10⁻⁶ m²). Find its resistance.

Step 1 — Formula
Write the formula:
R = ρ × L / A
Step 2 — Substitute
Put in values:
R = (1.7×10⁻⁸) × 5 / (0.5×10⁻⁶)
Step 3 — Simplify numerator
1.7×10⁻⁸ × 5 = 8.5×10⁻⁸
R = 8.5×10⁻⁸ / 0.5×10⁻⁶
Step 4 — Final Answer
Divide:
R = 8.5/0.5 × 10⁻⁸⁺⁶ = 17 × 10⁻² = 0.17 Ω
