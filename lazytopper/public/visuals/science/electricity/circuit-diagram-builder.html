📝 Worked Example — हल किया हुआ उदाहरण

A circuit has a battery of 6V, two resistors R₁=2Ω and R₂=4Ω in series. Click to reveal each step:

Step 1: Find Total Resistance → Rtotal = R₁ + R₂ = 2 + 4 = 6 Ω
Step 2: Find Current (I) → I = V ÷ R = 6 ÷ 6 = 1 Ampere (V = 6V, R = 6Ω)
Step 3: Voltage across R₁ → V₁ = I × R₁ = 1 × 2 = 2 Volts
Step 4: Voltage across R₂ → V₂ = I × R₂ = 1 × 4 = 4 Volts
✅ Check: V₁ + V₂ = 2 + 4 = 6V = Battery Voltage ✓   ∴ Circuit is consistent!

📖 Step-by-Step Guide — कदम दर कदम

Step 1: Power Source — शक्ति स्रोत

Start with a Battery (बैटरी). The longer line is +ve terminal, shorter is -ve. EMF is measured in Volts (V).

∵ बिना बैटरी के परिपथ में कोई धारा नहीं बहती।

Step 2: Add a Switch — स्विच लगाएं

Switch (स्विच) controls current flow. When closed (बंद), current flows. When open (खुला), circuit breaks.

∴ स्विच खुला होने पर परिपथ अपूर्ण होता है।

Step 3: Connect Resistor — प्रतिरोध जोड़ें

Resistor (प्रतिरोध) opposes current. Unit: Ohm (Ω). Series: RT = R₁+R₂. Parallel: 1/RT = 1/R₁+1/R₂

Step 4: Ammeter in Series — एमीटर श्रेणी में

Ammeter MUST be connected in series. It has very low resistance. Never connect in parallel! Measures current in Amperes (A).

⚠ एमीटर हमेशा श्रेणी क्रम में जोड़ें।

Step 5: Voltmeter in Parallel — वोल्टमीटर समांतर में

Voltmeter MUST be connected in parallel across component. It has very high resistance. Measures potential difference in Volts (V).

⚠ वोल्टमीटर हमेशा समांतर क्रम में जोड़ें।

Step 6: Complete the Circuit — परिपथ पूर्ण करें

Use Load Preset buttons to see Series/Parallel circuits. Toggle switch to see current flow animation! V = I × R (ओम का नियम)
