📐 Key Formulas (मुख्य सूत्र)
🔵 Series (श्रेणी)
R = R₁ + R₂ + R₃
∴ R_total सबसे बड़ा होता है
Same Current I | Different Voltage
🟢 Parallel (समानांतर)
1/R = 1/R₁ + 1/R₂ + 1/R₃
∴ R_total सबसे छोटा होता है
Same Voltage V | Different Current

🧠 Quick Check Test Yourself
Three resistors 2Ω, 3Ω, 6Ω are in parallel. What is R_total?
✅ Correct! 1/R = 1/2 + 1/3 + 1/6 = 3/6 + 2/6 + 1/6 = 6/6 = 1 → R = 1 Ω
