⚡ What is a Circuit? परिपथ

A closed path through which electric current flows. Toggle the switch to complete the circuit!

🔴 Switch ON — Current Flowing!
V = I × R  (Ohm's Law / ओम का नियम)
Voltage = Current × Resistance  |  V in Volts, I in Amperes, R in Ohms (Ω)

Two ways to connect resistors: Series (श्रेणी) and Parallel (समानांतर)

📝 Worked Example हल उदाहरण

Question: Three resistors R₁=2Ω, R₂=3Ω, R₃=5Ω are connected in series across a 10V battery. Find: (a) R_total (b) Current (c) Voltage across each resistor.
Parallel Example: Same 3 resistors in parallel across 6V.
1/R_total = 1/2 + 1/3 + 1/5 = 15/30 + 10/30 + 6/30 = 31/30 R_total ≈ 0.97 Ω
I_total = 6 ÷ 0.97 ≈ 6.19 A  |  Each branch has same voltage = 6V

🔗 Series (श्रेणी)

  • R = R₁+R₂+...
  • Same I everywhere
  • V divides (V=V₁+V₂)
  • R_total > any R

⚡ Parallel (समानांतर)

  • 1/R = 1/R₁+1/R₂+...
  • Same V everywhere
  • I divides (I=I₁+I₂)
  • R_total < any R
