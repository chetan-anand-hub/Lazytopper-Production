🧑‍🔬
Gregor Johann Mendel Father of Genetics
Mendel (1822–1884) conducted experiments on pea plants (Pisum sativum) for 8 years. He studied 7 contrasting traits and discovered the fundamental laws of heredity — how traits are passed from parents to offspring (संतान).
🔑 वंशागति के नियम | Laws of Inheritance
📚 Key Terms — Click to Explore!
Dominant
प्रभावी लक्षण
Trait that shows up in F1 generation. Represented by CAPITAL letter (T, R, Y). Always masks the recessive trait when present.
Recessive
अप्रभावी लक्षण
Trait that is masked by dominant. Shown by small letter (t, r, y). Appears only in homozygous condition (tt).
Gene / Allele
जीन / युग्म विकल्पी
Genes are segments of DNA. Alleles are different forms of same gene. Each organism has 2 alleles per trait (one from each parent).
Genotype/Phenotype
जीनप्ररूप / लक्षणप्ररूप
Genotype = genetic makeup (TT, Tt, tt). Phenotype = observable trait (Tall, Tall, Dwarf). Same phenotype can have different genotypes!

⚖️ Mendel's Laws of Inheritance
🔬 Law 1: Law of Dominance
When two pure breeding parents are crossed, only the dominant trait appears in F1 generation. The recessive trait is hidden but not lost.
प्रभाविता का नियम — F1 में केवल प्रभावी लक्षण दिखता है
🔀 Law 2: Law of Segregation
The two alleles for each trait separate (segregate) during gamete formation. Each gamete gets only ONE allele for each trait.
पृथक्करण का नियम — युग्मक बनते समय एलील अलग होते हैं

🚶 Step-by-Step Walkthrough Click to Reveal
1
Choose Parent Plants (P Generation): Select pure breeding Tall (TT) and Dwarf (tt) pea plants. These are homozygous — both alleles are same.
2
Gamete Formation (युग्मक निर्माण): TT plant produces only T gametes. tt plant produces only t gametes. ∵ Law of Segregation — alleles separate during meiosis.
3
F1 Cross (P × P): T gamete × t gamete → All offspring are Tt. Phenotype = ALL TALL (T is dominant). This proves the Law of Dominance. 100% Tall, 0% Dwarf.
4
F1 × F1 Self-pollination: Tt × Tt. Each parent gives T or t with equal probability (50% each). Draw Punnett Square with T and t on axes.
5
F2 Results: TT (Tall) : Tt (Tall) : tt (Dwarf) = 1:2:1 genotypic ratio. ∴ Phenotypic ratio = 3 Tall : 1 Dwarf. The recessive trait REAPPEARS in F2! This proves traits don't blend — they are inherited as discrete units.
6
Key Conclusion (निष्कर्ष): Traits are controlled by factors (genes) that come in pairs. One from father, one from mother. They separate during reproduction and recombine in offspring. Dominant masks recessive but never destroys it! 🧬
