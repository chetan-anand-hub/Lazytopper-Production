📐 प्रमेय (Theorem) CBSE Important
Theorem 10.1: The tangent at any point of a circle is perpendicular to the radius through the point of contact.

Theorem 10.2: The lengths of two tangents drawn from an external point to a circle are equal.

✏️ Worked Example (हल किया उदाहरण)
Problem: A tangent PQ at point P of a circle of radius 5 cm meets a line through the centre O at a point Q so that OQ = 13 cm. Find PQ.
1 Given: Radius OP = 5 cm, OQ = 13 cm, PQ is tangent at P
2 ∵ Tangent ⊥ Radius → ∠OPQ = 90°
3 Applying Pythagoras: OQ² = OP² + PQ²
4 13² = 5² + PQ² → 169 = 25 + PQ²
5 PQ² = 144 → PQ = √144 = 12 cm ✅
