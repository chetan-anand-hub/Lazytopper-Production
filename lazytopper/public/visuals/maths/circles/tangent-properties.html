प्रमेय (Theorem) 1

"The tangent at any point of a circle is perpendicular to the radius through the point of contact."

"वृत्त के किसी बिंदु पर स्पर्शरेखा, स्पर्श बिंदु से खींची गई त्रिज्या पर लंबवत होती है।"

∠OPT = 90°  (OT ⊥ tangent)
प्रमेय (Theorem) 2 — External Point

"The lengths of tangents drawn from an external point to a circle are equal."

"बाह्य बिंदु से वृत्त पर खींची गई स्पर्शरेखाओं की लंबाइयाँ बराबर होती हैं।"

PA = PB (External tangents from P)
Worked Step-by-Step Example

📝 Problem

A tangent PQ is drawn from external point P to a circle with centre O and radius 5 cm. If OP = 13 cm, find the length of tangent PQ.

Click "Next Step" to reveal the solution step by step
Step 1 — Identify the right angle (लंबकोण पहचानें)

∵ The tangent at any point is ⊥ to the radius at point of contact.
∴ ∠OQP = 90°
So △OQP is a right-angled triangle at Q.

Step 2 — Apply Pythagoras Theorem (पाइथागोरस प्रमेय)

In △OQP:   OP² = OQ² + PQ²
13² = 5² + PQ²
169 = 25 + PQ²

Step 3 — Solve for PQ

PQ² = 169 − 25 = 144
PQ = √144 = 12 cm ✓

∴ Answer

PQ = 12 cm
The key was: Tangent ⊥ Radius → Right triangle → Pythagoras!

PA = PB  (Equal tangents from external pt.)
PQ² = OP² − r²  (Tangent length formula)
