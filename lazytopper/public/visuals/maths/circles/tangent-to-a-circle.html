Q: A point P is 13 cm from the centre O of a circle of radius 5 cm. Find the length of the tangent from P to the circle.
Given: OP = 13 cm, OT = 5 cm (radius)
Since OT ⊥ PT → ∠OTP = 90°

By Pythagoras Theorem:
OP² = OT² + PT²
13² = 5² + PT²
169 = 25 + PT²
PT² = 144
PT = 12 cm ✓
📌 Theorem 10.1: The tangent at any point of a circle is perpendicular to the radius through the point of contact.

📌 Theorem 10.2: The lengths of tangents drawn from an external point to a circle are equal.
  → PA = PB (two tangents from P)

📌 Key Relation (Pythagoras):
  OP² = OT² + PT²

📌 Number of Tangents:
  • From outside circle → 2 tangents
  • From on circle → 1 tangent
  • From inside circle → 0 tangents

📌 Remember: ∠OTP = 90° always!
