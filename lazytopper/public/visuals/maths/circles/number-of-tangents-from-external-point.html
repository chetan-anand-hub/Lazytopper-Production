⭕ Circles — Number of Tangents from External Point

CBSE Class 10 Mathematics | Chapter 10 | इकाई: वृत्त

📐 प्रमेय (Theorem 10.2)
From an external point, exactly 2 tangents can be drawn to a circle, and they are equal in length.
बाहरी बिंदु से एक वृत्त पर दो स्पर्श रेखाएँ खींची जा सकती हैं जो लंबाई में बराबर होती हैं।
PA = PB  |  ∠OPA = ∠OPB  |  △OPA ≅ △OPB

Proof using congruent triangles (RHS):
In △OAP and △OBP:
• OA = OB (radii of same circle) ∵ same circle
• OP = OP (common side)
• ∠OAP = ∠OBP = 90° (radius ⊥ tangent)
∴ △OAP ≅ △OBP (by RHS congruence)
∴ PA = PB (by CPCT)  ✓

📖 Step-by-Step Proof Walkthrough
1

Given (दिया हुआ)

A circle with centre O. P is an external point. PA and PB are tangents drawn from P to the circle touching at A and B.

Circle: centre O, radius r  |  External point P
2

To Prove (सिद्ध करना है)

The lengths of the two tangents are equal.

PA = PB
3

Construction (रचना)

Join OA, OB, and OP to form two triangles: △OAP and △OBP.

Draw: OA, OB, OP
4

Key Fact: Radius ⊥ Tangent

By Theorem 10.1: The tangent at any point of a circle is perpendicular to the radius at that point.

∠OAP = 90°  &  ∠OBP = 90°
5

Congruence (सर्वांगसमता)

In △OAP and △OBP:
• OA = OB (equal radii)
• ∠OAP = ∠OBP = 90°
• OP = OP (common hypotenuse)

∴ △OAP ≅ △OBP  (by RHS)
6

✅ Conclusion (निष्कर्ष)

By CPCT (Corresponding Parts of Congruent Triangles):

∴ PA = PB  (Proved ✓)
💡 Worked Example (हल किया उदाहरण)

📝 Example 1

From a point P, the length of tangent to a circle is 12 cm. If the radius of the circle is 5 cm, find the distance OP (from external point to centre).

Given: PA = 12 cm (tangent length), OA = 5 cm (radius)

Since OA ⊥ PA (radius ⊥ tangent):

In right △OAP:

OP² = OA² + PA²

OP² = 5² + 12² = 25 + 144 = 169

∴ OP = √169 = 13 cm ✓

Also: PB = PA = 12 cm (tangents from external point)

📝 Example 2

Two tangents PA and PB are drawn to a circle with centre O. If ∠APB = 60°, find ∠AOB.

Since △OAP ≅ △OBP:

∠OPA = ∠OPB = 60°÷2 = 30°

In △OAP: ∠OAP + ∠OPA + ∠AOP = 180°

90° + 30° + ∠AOP = 180°

∠AOP = 60°

Similarly ∠BOP = 60°

∴ ∠AOB = ∠AOP + ∠BOP = 120° ✓

Short formula: ∠AOB + ∠APB = 180° → ∠AOB = 180° - 60° = 120°
