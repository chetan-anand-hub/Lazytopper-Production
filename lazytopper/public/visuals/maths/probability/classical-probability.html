📐 मुख्य सूत्र (Key Formula)
P(E) = n(E) ÷ n(S)
P(घटना) = अनुकूल परिणाम ÷ कुल परिणाम
P(E) = Probability of event E n(E) = Favorable outcomes n(S) = Total outcomes (Sample Space)
🎯 Sample Space (नमूना समष्टि) Set of ALL possible outcomes of an experiment.
✅ Favorable Outcomes (अनुकूल परिणाम) Outcomes where the desired event occurs.
⚖️ Equally Likely (समसम्भावित) Each outcome has equal chance — key assumption!
📏 Range of P(E) Always: 0 ≤ P(E) ≤ 1
P(impossible)=0, P(sure)=1

📝 Worked Example — Step by Step
Question: A bag contains 3 red balls, 5 blue balls, and 2 green balls. A ball is drawn at random. Find the probability of drawing a blue ball.
1
Identify the Total Outcomes — n(S)
▶

The bag has: 3 red + 5 blue + 2 green balls.

Total balls = 3 + 5 + 2 = 10

∴ n(S) = 10 (All outcomes are equally likely since one ball drawn at random)

2
Identify Favorable Outcomes — n(E)
▶

Event E = "Drawing a blue ball"

Number of blue balls = 5

∴ n(E) = 5 (any of the 5 blue balls is a favorable outcome)

3
Apply the Formula
▶

P(E) = n(E) ÷ n(S)

P(blue ball) = 5 ÷ 10

P(blue ball) = 5/10 = 1/2 = 0.5

4
Find Complementary Probability — P(E̅)
▶

P(not blue) = P(E̅) = 1 − P(E)

P(E̅) = 1 − 1/2 = 1/2

∵ P(E) + P(E̅) = 1 always! (Complementary Events Property)

✅ Check: 5/10 + 5/10 = 10/10 = 1 ✔

💡 Key Properties to Remember
🔹 0 ≤ P(E) ≤ 1 — Probability is always between 0 and 1
🔹 P(impossible event) = 0 — e.g., getting 7 on a dice
🔹 P(sure/certain event) = 1 — e.g., getting a number ≤ 6 on dice
🔹 P(E) + P(E̅) = 1 — Complementary events (पूरक घटनाएँ)
🔹 Outcomes must be equally likely — equally probable
🔹 Sum of all probabilities in sample space = 1
