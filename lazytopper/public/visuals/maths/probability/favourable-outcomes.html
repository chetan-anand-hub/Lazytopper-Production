📐 Key Definitions (मुख्य परिभाषाएँ)
P(E) = n(E) ÷ n(S)
P(घटना) = अनुकूल परिणाम ÷ कुल परिणाम (नमूना समष्टि)
n(E) = Favourable outcomes for event E n(S) = Total outcomes in sample space
🎯 Sample Space S (नमूना समष्टि) The complete set of ALL possible outcomes of a random experiment.
✅ Favourable Outcome (अनुकूल परिणाम) An outcome that satisfies the condition of the event E we are interested in.
⚖️ Equally Likely (समसम्भावित) Each outcome has the same chance — a key requirement before applying the formula.
🚫 Unfavourable Outcome An outcome that does NOT satisfy event E. These are counted in P(not E).

💡 What Makes an Outcome "Favourable"?
🔹 Favourable outcomes are those that satisfy the event condition exactly as stated in the question.
🔹 Read the event carefully — "getting an even number" and "getting a number ≥ 4" are different events.
🔹 Outcomes are always counted from the sample space, not guessed.
🔹 If events are mutually exclusive, add their favourable outcomes: n(A or B) = n(A) + n(B).
🔹 P(E) + P(not E) = 1  →  Favourable + Unfavourable = Total n(S).
📝 Exam Tip: Write the full sample space on your rough sheet first, then circle/mark the favourable outcomes before counting. This avoids missing any outcome.
