🎲 Probability — Complementary Events

पूरक घटनाएँ (Pūrak Ghaṭanāẽ) · CBSE Class 10 Mathematics

मुख्य सूत्र (Main Formula)
P(not E) = 1 − P(E)
∵ P(E) + P(not E) = 1  |  0 ≤ P(E) ≤ 1
P(E) = Probability of event E occurring  |  P(not E) = P(Ē) = Probability it does NOT occur

📝 Worked Example (हल किया गया उदाहरण)
Question:

A bag contains 5 red balls and 3 blue balls. One ball is drawn at random.
If P(red ball) = 5/8, find P(not a red ball).

    ⚡ Special Cases (विशेष स्थितियाँ)
    P(E) = 0
    Impossible Event
    अशक्य घटना
    ∴ P(not E) = 1
    P(E) = 1
    Certain Event
    निश्चित घटना
    ∴ P(not E) = 0
    💡 Key Insight: The more likely E is, the less likely "not E" is — they are complementary (पूरक)!
