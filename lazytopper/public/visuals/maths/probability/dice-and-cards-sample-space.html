📝 Worked Example 1 — Two Dice

Question: Two dice are thrown simultaneously. Find the probability that the sum of numbers on both dice is 8.

STEP 1 — Sample Space | नमूना समष्टि
When 2 dice are thrown, total outcomes = 6 × 6 = 36
∴ n(S) = 36
STEP 2 — Favorable Outcomes | अनुकूल परिणाम
Pairs where sum = 8:
(2,6), (3,5), (4,4), (5,3), (6,2)
∴ n(E) = 5
STEP 3 — Apply Formula
P(sum = 8) = n(E) ÷ n(S)
P(sum = 8) = 5/36 ≈ 0.139

📝 Worked Example 2 — Cards

Question: A card is drawn from a well-shuffled deck of 52 cards. Find P(King of Hearts).

STEP 1 — Sample Space
Total cards in a standard deck = 52
∴ n(S) = 52
STEP 2 — Favorable Outcomes
King of Hearts — there is only 1 such card
∴ n(E) = 1
STEP 3 — Apply Formula
P(King of Hearts) = n(E) ÷ n(S) = 1 ÷ 52
P(King of Hearts) = 1/52 ≈ 0.019

📝 Worked Example 3 — Single Die

Question: A die is thrown once. Find P(prime number | अभाज्य संख्या).

STEP 1 — Sample Space
S = {1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6}
∴ n(S) = 6
STEP 2 — Favorable Outcomes
Prime numbers in S: {2, 3, 5}
∴ n(E) = 3
STEP 3 — Apply Formula
P(prime) = 3 ÷ 6 = 1/2
P(prime number) = 1/2 = 0.5

⚡ Key Facts to Remember | याद रखें

• 0 ≤ P(E) ≤ 1 (Probability is always between 0 and 1)
• P(E) + P(Ē) = 1 (Complementary events)
• P(certain event) = 1 | P(impossible event) = 0
• Deck: 4 suits × 13 cards = 52 | Face cards = J+Q+K = 12
• Two dice: n(S) = 36 | One die: n(S) = 6
