Polynomials: Relationship between Zeroes & Coefficients | Class 10

🎯 मुख्य अवधारणा (Core Concept)

For a polynomial with zeroes α (alpha) and β (beta), there are beautiful relationships with its coefficients!

Quadratic (द्विघात)
Cubic (त्रिघात)
General Quadratic Polynomial
p(x) = ax² + bx + c
with zeroes α and β
∑ Sum of Zeroes (योग)
α + β = −b/a
= −(coefficient of x) ÷ (coefficient of x²)
∏ Product of Zeroes (गुणनफल)
α × β = c/a
= constant term ÷ coefficient of x²
General Cubic Polynomial
p(x) = ax³ + bx² + cx + d
with zeroes α, β and γ (gamma)
α + β + γ (Sum)
= −b/a
αβ + βγ + γα (Sum of products of pairs)
= c/a
α × β × γ (Product)
= −d/a

Type Sum (α+β) Product (αβ)

∴ Polynomial Form x² − (α+β)x + αβ

🔑 Key Trick (महत्वपूर्ण युक्ति)

To form a quadratic from roots: x² − (Sum of roots)x + (Product of roots) = 0
Example: roots 2 & 3 → x² − 5x + 6 = 0 ✓
