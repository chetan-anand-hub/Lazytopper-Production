🔑 मुख्य अवधारणा (Key Concept)

p(x) = 0 के हल = Graph का x-axis से मिलन बिंदु = Zeroes (शून्यक)

The zeroes of a polynomial p(x) are the x-coordinates of the points where the graph of y = p(x) intersects the x-axis.

📏 Linear Polynomial: ax + b

A straight line — always cuts x-axis at exactly 1 point → 1 zero

Zero of 2x - 4 is x = 2 where graph crosses x-axis ✓

Formula: Zero = -b/a

📈 Quadratic Polynomial: ax² + bx + c

A parabola — can cut x-axis at 0, 1 or 2 points

✌️ 2 Zeroes

Parabola cuts x-axis twice

☝️ 1 Zero

Parabola touches x-axis

🚫 0 Zeroes

Parabola doesn't touch x-axis
