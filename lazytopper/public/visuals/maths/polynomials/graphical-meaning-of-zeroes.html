🎯 Core Concept: शून्यक (Zero/Root) = Point where graph crosses X-axis

p(x) = 0 ⟹ x-intercept on graph
The x-coordinates where the polynomial graph touches or crosses the X-axis are called the zeroes (शून्यक) of the polynomial

⛰️ Quadratic
