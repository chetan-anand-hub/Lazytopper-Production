📐 Division Algorithm for Polynomials

बहुपदों का विभाजन एल्गोरिदम • CBSE Class 10 Mathematics

🔑 The Key Formula प्रमेय (Theorem)
Dividend = Divisor × Quotient + Remainder
p(x) = g(x) × q(x) + r(x)
Where deg[r(x)] < deg[g(x)] OR r(x) = 0
Dividend (भाज्य)
p(x)
The polynomial being divided
Always has the highest degree
Divisor (भाजक)
g(x)
The polynomial we divide by
deg[g(x)] ≤ deg[p(x)]
Quotient (भागफल)
q(x)
Result of the division
deg[q(x)] = deg[p(x)] - deg[g(x)]
Remainder (शेषफल)
r(x)
What's left after division
deg[r(x)] < deg[g(x)]
💡 Think of it like number division: 17 ÷ 5 = 3 remainder 2, so 17 = 5 × 3 + 2. Same idea for polynomials!
📝 Worked Example Step-by-Step
Divide: p(x) = x³ – 3x² + 5x – 3   by   g(x) = x² – 2
Step 0 of 5
Step 1 — Setup (व्यवस्था)
Write both polynomials in standard form (descending powers). Make sure no term is missing.
Dividend p(x): x³ – 3x² + 5x – 3
Divisor g(x): x² + 0x – 2
Step 2 — First Division (पहला चरण)
Divide the leading term of dividend by leading term of divisor:
x³ ÷ x² = x   ← first term of quotient
Multiply back: x × (x² – 2) = x³ – 2x
Subtract: (x³ – 3x² + 5x – 3) – (x³ – 2x) = –3x² + 7x – 3
Step 3 — Second Division (दूसरा चरण)
Now divide the new leading term –3x² by x²:
–3x² ÷ x² = –3   ← second term of quotient
Multiply back: –3 × (x² – 2) = –3x² + 6
Subtract: (–3x² + 7x – 3) – (–3x² + 6) = 7x – 9
Step 4 — Stop! (रुकें)
The remaining polynomial 7x – 9 has degree 1, which is less than degree of g(x) = 2. So we stop here!
Quotient q(x) = x – 3
Remainder r(x) = 7x – 9
∵ deg(7x–9) = 1 < deg(x²–2) = 2 ✓
Step 5 — Verify! (सत्यापन)
Use the formula: p(x) = g(x) × q(x) + r(x)
(x²–2)(x–3) + (7x–9)
= x³–3x²–2x+6 + 7x–9
= x³–3x²+5x–3 ✓   Same as p(x)!
📊 Visual Long Division Layout
Quotient → x – 3
g(x):x²–2x³–3x²+5x–3 ← p(x)
– x³ + 0x² – 2x 
   –3x² + 7x – 3
– –3x² + 0x + 6 
    7x – 9 ← Remainder r(x)

a) deg[r(x)] must equal deg[g(x)]
b) deg[r(x)] must be less than deg[g(x)]
c) deg[r(x)] must be greater than deg[p(x)]
d) r(x) must always be zero
📌 Key Points to Remember
✅ Formula: p(x) = g(x) × q(x) + r(x)
✅ Condition: deg[r(x)] < deg[g(x)] OR r(x) = 0
✅ If r(x) = 0, then g(x) is a factor of p(x)
✅ Degree of quotient = deg[p(x)] – deg[g(x)]
✅ Always arrange polynomials in descending order
✅ If any term is missing, write it with coefficient 0
💡 याद रखें: शेषफल (Remainder) की घात हमेशा भाजक (Divisor) की घात से कम होनी चाहिए!
