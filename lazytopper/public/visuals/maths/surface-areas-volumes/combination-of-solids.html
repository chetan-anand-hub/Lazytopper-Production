🎯 What is Combination of Solids? CONCEPT

Many real-life objects are made by joining two or more basic solids. We calculate their surface area and volume by breaking them into known shapes.

💡 Key Insight: जब दो ठोस जोड़े जाते हैं, तो जुड़ने वाला भाग (जैसे आधार) surface area में नहीं जुड़ता!
