🎯 What is Combination of Solids?

Real-world objects are often made by joining two or more basic solids. We calculate their Surface Area & Volume by combining individual shapes.

∵ The combined shape shares surfaces — so Total Surface Area = Sum of visible surfaces only! (shared/hidden surfaces are excluded)

⚠️ Common Mistakes / सावधान!

❌ Wrong: Adding ALL surfaces including shared/hidden ones
✅ Right: Only count visible/exposed surfaces
❌ Wrong: Using h instead of l (slant height) in cone CSA formula
✅ Right: Cone CSA = πrl, where l = √(r²+h²)
💡 Tip: For Volume of combination, simply ADD volumes (no surface sharing for volume!)
