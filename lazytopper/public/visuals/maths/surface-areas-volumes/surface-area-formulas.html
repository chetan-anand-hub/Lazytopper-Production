📖 CSA vs TSA Core Concept
Curved Surface Area (CSA) — area of only the curved surface(s), excluding flat ends.
Total Surface Area (TSA) — area of ALL surfaces including flat ends.
TSA = CSA + Area of flat faces

वक्र पृष्ठीय क्षेत्रफल = केवल घुमावदार भाग | कुल पृष्ठीय क्षेत्रफल = सभी सतहें मिलाकर

📋 Worked Example

Q: A cone has base radius 7 cm, height 24 cm. Find its CSA and TSA. (Use π = 22/7)

1
Find slant height: l = √(r² + h²)
l = √(7² + 24²) = √(49 + 576) = √625 = 25 cm
2
Calculate CSA:
CSA = πrl = (22/7) × 7 × 25 = 22 × 25 = 550 cm²
3
Calculate TSA:
TSA = πr(r + l) = (22/7) × 7 × (7 + 25) = 22 × 32 = 704 cm²
