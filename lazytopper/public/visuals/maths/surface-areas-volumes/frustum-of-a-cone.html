💡 What is a Frustum? शंकु का छिन्नक

When a cone is cut by a plane parallel to its base, the part between the cutting plane and the base is called a Frustum (छिन्नक). Think of a bucket, a drinking glass, or a lampshade!

📐 Key Formulas सूत्र

Slant Height
Volume
Surface Area
Slant Height (तिरछी ऊँचाई) l
l = √(h² + (R - r)²)

Where h = vertical height, R = radius of larger base, r = radius of smaller base. This comes from the Pythagoras theorem applied to the cross-section.

Volume (आयतन) V
V = ⅓ × π × h × (R² + r² + R·r)

The volume is found by subtracting the volume of the smaller cone (removed) from the larger cone (original).

Curved Surface Area (वक्र पृष्ठीय क्षेत्रफल)
CSA = π(R + r)l
Total Surface Area (कुल पृष्ठीय क्षेत्रफल)
TSA = π(R + r)l + πR² + πr²

TSA = Curved part + Large circular base + Small circular top.

📝 Worked Example हल किया उदाहरण

Problem: A bucket is in the form of a frustum of a cone. The radii of the top and bottom are 28 cm and 21 cm, and the height is 45 cm. Find the volume and the total surface area. (Use π = 22/7)

💡 Exam Tips परीक्षा युक्तियाँ

✅ Always find slant height l first using Pythagoras theorem before calculating surface area.
⚠️ TSA includes both circular bases (top + bottom). Don't forget to add πr² and πR²!
📌 For a bucket open at top: use CSA + πR² (only bottom base — no top lid).
❗ Volume formula: ⅓πh(R² + r² + Rr) — memorize all three terms inside the bracket!
