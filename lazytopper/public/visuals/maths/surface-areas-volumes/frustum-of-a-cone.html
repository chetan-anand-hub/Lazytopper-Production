📖 What is a Frustum? | छिन्नक क्या है?

When a cone is cut by a plane parallel to its base, the portion between the cutting plane and the base is called the Frustum of a Cone (शंकु का छिन्नक).

∴ Frustum = Original Cone − Small Cone Bucket / Flower pot shape

📐 Key Formulas | मुख्य सूत्र
Slant Height (तिरछी ऊँचाई)
l = √(h² + (R−r)²)
Volume (आयतन)
V = π h/3 (R²+r²+Rr)
Curved Surface Area
CSA = π(R+r)l
Total Surface Area
TSA = π(R+r)l + πR² + πr²

Where R = large radius, r = small radius, h = height, l = slant height, π ≈ 3.14159

Problem: A bucket is in the shape of a frustum. Large radius R = 20 cm, small radius r = 10 cm, height h = 30 cm. Find Volume, CSA, and TSA. (π = 22/7)

1 Given: R = 20 cm, r = 10 cm, h = 30 cm
2 Find Slant Height:
l = √(h² + (R−r)²)
l = √(30² + (20−10)²)
l = √(900 + 100)
l = √1000 = 10√10 ≈ 31.62 cm
3 Volume:
V = πh/3 × (R²+r²+Rr)
V = (22/7) × 30/3 × (400+100+200)
V = (22/7) × 10 × 700
V = 22 × 10 × 100
V = 22000 cm³
4 Curved Surface Area:
CSA = π(R+r)l
CSA = (22/7) × (20+10) × 10√10
CSA = (22/7) × 30 × 31.62
CSA ≈ 2978 cm²
5 Total Surface Area:
TSA = CSA + πR² + πr²
TSA = 2978 + (22/7)(400 + 100)
TSA = 2978 + (22/7) × 500
TSA = 2978 + 1571.4
TSA ≈ 4549.4 cm²
✓ ∴ Volume = 22000 cm³, CSA ≈ 2978 cm², TSA ≈ 4549 cm²

Step 1 of 6

∵ Frustum = Big Cone − Small Cone

Let the big cone have height H and slant L. The small cone (cut off) has height H−h and slant L−l.

By similar triangles: R/H = r/(H−h)

Volume of frustum:
V = (1/3)πR²H − (1/3)πr²(H−h)
Simplifies to: V = πh/3 (R²+r²+Rr)

Full cone minus small cone = Frustum

Question: For a frustum with R=6cm, r=3cm, h=4cm, what is the slant height?

Solution: l = √(h² + (R−r)²) = √(4² + (6−3)²) = √(16+9) = √25 = 5 cm ✓
