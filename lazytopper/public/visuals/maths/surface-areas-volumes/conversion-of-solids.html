📖 Worked Example – Step by Step
Question: A copper sphere of radius 9 cm is melted and recast into small spheres of radius 3 cm. Find the number of small spheres formed.
1Find Volume of Big Sphere (बड़े गोले का आयतन)
V = (4/3) × π × R³
V = (4/3) × π × 9³
V = (4/3) × π × 729
V = 972π cm³
2Find Volume of Small Sphere (छोटे गोले का आयतन)
v = (4/3) × π × r³
v = (4/3) × π × 3³
v = (4/3) × π × 27
v = 36π cm³
3Apply Volume Conservation (आयतन संरक्षण)
n × v = V
n × 36π = 972π
n = 972π ÷ 36π
n = 27
✓Answer (उत्तर)
∴ Number of small spheres = 27

∵ Volume is conserved when solid is melted and recast
