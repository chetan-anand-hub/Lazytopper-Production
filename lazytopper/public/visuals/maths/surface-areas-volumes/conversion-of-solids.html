💡 The Big Idea

आयतन संरक्षण का नियम (Law of Volume Conservation)

When a solid is melted and recast into another shape,
the volume stays the same — only the shape changes!

Volumeold = Volumenew

∴ पुराने आकार का आयतन = नए आकार का आयतन

📝 Worked Example (CBSE Style)
Q: A metallic sphere of radius 4.2 cm is melted and recast into small spheres of radius 0.6 cm. Find the number of small spheres. (एक धातु का गोला पिघलाकर छोटे गोले बनाए जाते हैं)
Step 0 / 4
STEP 1 — Write Given Information (दिया गया)
Large sphere radius R = 4.2 cm
Small sphere radius r = 0.6 cm
Number of small spheres = n (to find)
STEP 2 — Apply Volume Conservation (आयतन संरक्षण)
Volume of large sphere = n × Volume of each small sphere
(4/3)πR³ = n × (4/3)πr³
STEP 3 — Simplify (सरलीकरण)
(4/3)π cancels from both sides
n = R³ ÷ r³ = (4.2)³ ÷ (0.6)³
n = 74.088 ÷ 0.216 = 343
✅ ANSWER (उत्तर)
∴ Number of small spheres = 343
Verification: (4.2/0.6)³ = 7³ = 343 ✓
