💡 What is the Factorisation Method? (गुणनखंड विधि)

A quadratic equation ax² + bx + c = 0 can be solved by splitting the middle term (बीच का पद) into two parts, then grouping to find factors.

ax² + bx + c = 0
Find two numbers p & q such that:
🔴 शर्त 1 — Product (गुणनफल)
p × q = a × c
Product of p and q equals a×c
🟢 शर्त 2 — Sum (योग)
p + q = b
Sum of p and q equals b (middle term)
💡 Product Tip: a×c is called the product condition. List all factor pairs of |a×c| — both positive & negative combinations!
💡 Sum Tip: Among all factor pairs of a×c, find the pair whose sum equals b (the coefficient of x).

🧠 Smart Trick — याद रखो!

For ax² + bx + c = 0, the split trick always works when:

b² − 4ac ≥ 0 (Discriminant / विविक्तकर must be non-negative)

Signs pattern to find p & q quickly:

+bx, +c
Both p,q are positive
−bx, +c
Both p,q are negative
+bx, −c
Larger is positive
−bx, −c
Larger is negative
