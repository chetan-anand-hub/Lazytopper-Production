🔑 Core Concept अवधारणा

A quadratic equation has the form:

ax² + bx + c = 0  (a ≠ 0)

To factorise by splitting the middle term, we find two numbers p and q such that:

Sum
p + q = b
Coefficient of x (middle term)
Product
p × q = ac
Product of a & c
💡 याद रखो: Split bx into px + qx, then group and factor out common terms.

🟦 Area Model – Why Splitting Works

Think of x² + 5x + 6 as an area split into 4 rectangles:

∴  x² + 5x + 6 = (x + 2)(x + 3)

The two numbers 2 and 3: sum = 5 = b, product = 6 = c

🚶 Step-by-Step Walkthrough

Solve: 2x² − 7x + 3 = 0
1Identify a, b, c
a = 2,  b = −7,  c = 3
2Calculate a × c
a × c = 2 × 3 = 6
Need two numbers with product = 6 and sum = −7
3Find the pair (p, q)
Pairs with product 6: (1,6), (2,3), (−1,−6), (−2,−3)
✓  −1 + (−6) = −7  &&  (−1)×(−6) = 6
∴ p = −1, q = −6
4Split the middle term
2x² − 7x + 3
= 2x² − x − 6x + 3
= x(2x − 1) − 3(2x − 1)
= (2x − 1)(x − 3)
5Find roots (मूल)
2x − 1 = 0  ⟹  x = ½
x − 3 = 0    ⟹  x = 3
∴ Solutions: x = ½  or  x = 3 ✓
