🔑 Quadratic Formula (श्रीधराचार्य सूत्र)

Roots (मूल) of ax² + bx + c = 0

x = (−b ± √(b²−4ac)) / 2a

Also called Sridharacharya Formula | b²−4ac is the Discriminant (D)

D > 0
2 distinct real roots
दो भिन्न वास्तविक मूल
D = 0
2 equal real roots
दो समान मूल
D < 0
No real roots
कोई वास्तविक मूल नहीं
α+β, αβ
Sum = −b/a
Product = c/a

📌 Key Takeaways Summary

🟦Degree: Quadratic equations have degree 2 (highest power of variable is 2)
🟩Roots / Zeros (मूल): Values of x that satisfy the equation. Max 2 roots possible.
🟨Parabola: Graph of y = ax²+bx+c is always a parabola. Opens up if a>0, down if a<0.
🟥Discriminant D = b²−4ac: Determines nature of roots without actually solving.
⭐∵ α+β = −b/a ∴ αβ = c/a (Vieta's relations — useful for verification)
📝 परीक्षा टिप: विविक्तकर (Discriminant) पहले निकालें — इससे पता चलता है कि मूल वास्तविक हैं या नहीं!
