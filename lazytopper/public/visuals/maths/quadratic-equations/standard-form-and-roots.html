Worked Example (हल किया उदाहरण) — Click to Reveal
Solve the equation:
x² - 5x + 6 = 0
Here a=1, b=-5, c=6
Step 1
Identify a, b, c
👆 Click to reveal
Comparing x² - 5x + 6 = 0 with ax² + bx + c = 0:

a = 1 b = -5 c = 6
Step 2
Calculate Discriminant D = b² - 4ac
👆 Click to reveal
D = (-5)² - 4(1)(6)
D = 25 - 24
D = 1 > 0 ✓
∴ Two distinct real roots exist! (दो भिन्न वास्तविक मूल हैं)
Step 3
Apply Quadratic Formula
👆 Click to reveal
x = (-b ± √D) / 2a
x = (5 ± √1) / 2
x = (5 ± 1) / 2

x₁ = (5+1)/2 = 3
x₂ = (5-1)/2 = 2
Step 4
Verify the Roots (सत्यापन)
👆 Click to reveal
For x = 3: (3)² - 5(3) + 6 = 9 - 15 + 6 = 0 ✓
For x = 2: (2)² - 5(2) + 6 = 4 - 10 + 6 = 0 ✓

✓ Roots verified!

Sum of roots α + β = 3 + 2 = 5 = -b/a = -(-5)/1 = 5 ✓
Product of roots α × β = 3 × 2 = 6 = c/a = 6/1 = 6 ✓
Vieta's Formulas (मूलों का सम्बन्ध)
Sum of Roots (मूलों का योग)
α + β = -b/a
= -(coefficient of x) / (coefficient of x²)
Product of Roots (मूलों का गुणनफल)
α × β = c/a
= constant term / coefficient of x²
💡 If roots are α and β, the quadratic equation is:
x² - (α+β)x + αβ = 0
