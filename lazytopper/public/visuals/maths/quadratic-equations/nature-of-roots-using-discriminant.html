📐 Discriminant & Nature of Roots

CBSE Class 10 • Quadratic Equations • विवेचक (Discriminant)

For ax² + bx + c = 0, the Discriminant (विवेचक) is:
D = b² − 4ac
The value of D tells us about the nature of roots without solving!
D > 0 → Real & Distinct D = 0 → Real & Equal D < 0 → No Real Roots

📊 Quick Summary (CBSE Formula Table)

Condition Nature of Roots
D = b² − 4ac > 0 Real & Distinct (α ≠ β)
D = b² − 4ac = 0 Real & Equal (α = β = −b/2a)
D = b² − 4ac < 0 No Real Roots (अवास्तविक)
💡 याद रखें (Remember): Discriminant "विवेचक" tells us the nature WITHOUT finding actual roots! Use: x = (−b ± √D) / 2a for actual roots.
