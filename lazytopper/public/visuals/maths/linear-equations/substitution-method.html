📐 Substitution Method | प्रतिस्थापन विधि

Class 10 Mathematics — Pair of Linear Equations in Two Variables

🔑 Core Idea | मूल विचार

Step 1: Express one variable in terms of the other
(एक चर को दूसरे चर में व्यक्त करो)

Step 2: Substitute (प्रतिस्थापित करो) into the second equation

Step 3: Solve the single-variable equation → get one value

Step 4: Back-substitute → get the other value

∴ Solution = ordered pair (x, y)
💡 Why it works: A linear equation with 2 unknowns has ∞ solutions. But when we fix one variable's expression, the second equation gives us the unique solution!

Worked Example | हल किया उदाहरण

📘 Solve:   2x + 3y = 11  &  x − 2y = −3

🔍 Strategy: The second equation has x alone (coefficient = 1), so it's easiest to express x from Eq.2 and substitute in Eq.1!
1Write down the equations
Equation 1:   2x + 3y = 11    …(i)
Equation 2:   x − 2y = −3    …(ii)
We have 2 equations, 2 unknowns (x & y). ∴ Unique solution exists!
2Express x from Equation (ii) | x को अलग करो
x − 2y = −3
∴   x = −3 + 2y    …(iii)
✅ Now x is expressed as a function of y!
3Substitute (iii) into Equation (i) | प्रतिस्थापन
2(x) + 3y = 11   → replace x with (−3 + 2y)
2(−3 + 2y) + 3y = 11
−6 + 4y + 3y = 11
7y = 11 + 6 = 17
∴   y = 17 ÷ 7 = 17/7
🎉 One variable solved! Now let's find x.
4Back-substitute to find x | x का मान निकालो
From (iii):   x = −3 + 2y
x = −3 + 2 × (17/7)
x = −3 + 34/7
x = −21/7 + 34/7
∴   x = 13/7
5Verify the solution ✓ | सत्यापन
Check in Eq.(i):   2(13/7) + 3(17/7) = 26/7 + 51/7 = 77/7 = 11 ✅
Check in Eq.(ii):   13/7 − 2(17/7) = 13/7 − 34/7 = −21/7 = −3 ✅
∴ Solution:   x = 13/7,   y = 17/7
🏆 Both equations satisfied! Solution is correct.
Important Tips | महत्वपूर्ण सुझाव
✅ Choose wisely
Pick the equation where a variable has coefficient 1 or −1 for easier calculation
✅ Always Verify
Substitute back into both original equations to check your answer!
⚠️ Sign Errors
Be careful with negative signs when expressing and substituting variables!
📝 CBSE Tip
Label your equations as (i), (ii), (iii)… for full marks in board exams!

📋 The 4-Step Formula
Step 1:   Choose easier equation → Express one variable
Step 2:   Substitute expression into the OTHER equation
Step 3:   Solve → get value of one variable
Step 4:   Back-substitute → get other variable → Verify ✓
