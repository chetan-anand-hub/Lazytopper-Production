📐 Substitution Method (प्रतिस्थापन विधि)

Pair of Linear Equations in Two Variables

CBSE Class 10 Mathematics

💡 What is the Substitution Method?

In this method, we express one variable in terms of the other using one equation, and then substitute (replace) it into the second equation. This gives us one equation with one variable, which is easy to solve!

🔑 Key Idea: Replace one variable → Get one equation → Solve → Back-substitute
🚶 Step-by-Step Walkthrough Worked Example
Equation 1 (समीकरण १)
2x + y = 7
Equation 2 (समीकरण २)
x − y = 2
1
CHOOSE AN EQUATION
Choose a variable to isolate

Pick the equation where isolating a variable is easiest. Here, from Equation 2: x − y = 2, it is easy to express x alone.

Equation 2:   x − y = 2
∴  x = 2 + y    ← isolate x
💡 We chose Eq.2 because the coefficient of x is 1 — no division needed!
2
SUBSTITUTE (प्रतिस्थापित करें)
Replace x in the other equation
Equation 1:   2 x + y = 7

↓ Replace x with (2 + y)

2(2 + y) + y = 7
∵ x = 2 + y, we put (2 + y) wherever we see x in Equation 1
3
SIMPLIFY (सरल करें)
Solve for the remaining variable
2(2 + y) + y = 7
4 + 2y + y = 7     ← expand brackets
4 + 3y = 7
3y = 7 − 4
3y = 3
y = 1  🎯
∴ We have found the value of y by solving one simple equation!
4
BACK-SUBSTITUTE
Find the value of the other variable

Put y = 1 back into the expression we found in Step 1:

x = 2 + y
x = 2 + 1
x = 3  🎯
∴ By replacing y with 1, we instantly get the value of x!
✓
VERIFY (सत्यापन)
Check your answer in BOTH equations
x = 3, y = 1

Eq.1: 2(3) + (1) = 6 + 1 = 7 ✓
Eq.2: (3) − (1) = 2 ✓

📌 Exam Tips (परीक्षा के लिए सुझाव)

🎯Always choose the equation and variable where the coefficient is 1 — avoids fractions!
✅Always verify your answer by substituting in BOTH equations — easy marks!
🔄If coefficients aren't 1, that's okay — use fractions carefully and simplify.
📝Write: "From Eq.(1), x = ..." clearly in exams for full step marks (पूर्ण चरण अंक).
⚠️If you get 0 = 0, equations have infinite solutions. If 0 = non-zero, no solution!
