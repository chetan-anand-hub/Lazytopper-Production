🔑 Core Concept (मुख्य अवधारणा)

a₁x + b₁y + c₁ = 0
a₂x + b₂y + c₂ = 0
दो रेखाओं का प्रतिच्छेदन बिंदु ही हल है | The intersection point of two lines is the solution

✅ Consistent

Lines Intersect
at ONE point

a₁/a₂ ≠ b₁/b₂

Unique Solution

❌ Inconsistent

Lines are
Parallel

a₁/a₂ = b₁/b₂ ≠ c₁/c₂

No Solution

♾️ Dependent

Lines Coincide
(Same line)

a₁/a₂ = b₁/b₂ = c₁/c₂

Infinite Solutions
