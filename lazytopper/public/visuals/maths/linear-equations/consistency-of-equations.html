📐 Pair of Linear Equations — Consistency (संगतता)

CBSE Class 10 Maths | Chapter 3 | Interactive Visual Explainer

🔑 मुख्य सूत्र (Key Ratios)

a₁x + b₁y + c₁ = 0
a₂x + b₂y + c₂ = 0
Compare ratios: a₁/a₂  |  b₁/b₂  |  c₁/c₂
✅

Consistent
(संगत)

Unique Solution
Lines Intersect

a₁/a₂ ≠ b₁/b₂

❌

Inconsistent
(असंगत)

No Solution
Lines Parallel

a₁/a₂ = b₁/b₂ ≠ c₁/c₂

♾️

Dependent
(आश्रित संगत)

Infinite Solutions
Lines Coincide

a₁/a₂ = b₁/b₂ = c₁/c₂

✅ Consistent — Unique Solution (एक अद्वितीय हल)

The two lines intersect at exactly one point. The system has a unique solution. This happens when a₁/a₂ ≠ b₁/b₂. ∴ The system is called consistent (संगत).
