✅ Consistent (Intersecting Lines)

a₁/a₂ ≠ b₁/b₂

The lines intersect at exactly ONE point. The system has a unique solution.

∴ एक अद्वितीय हल (unique solution) होता है।

📊 Quick Reference Table — त्वरित संदर्भ

Condition Ratio Lines Solution
✅ Consistent a₁/a₂ ≠ b₁/b₂ Intersecting Unique
❌ Inconsistent a₁/a₂ = b₁/b₂ ≠ c₁/c₂ Parallel None
∞ Dependent a₁/a₂ = b₁/b₂ = c₁/c₂ Coincident Infinite

💡 Tip: Check a₁/a₂ first. If equal, compare b₁/b₂. Then c₁/c₂ to decide between inconsistent and dependent.
