🔵 Areas Related to Circles

Combined Figures Area | संयुक्त आकृतियाँ | CBSE Class 10

📐 मुख्य सूत्र (Key Formula)

छायांकित क्षेत्रफल = बड़ी आकृति − छोटी आकृति
Shaded Area = Larger Shape − Shape to Subtract
∴ Area = A(outer) − A(inner)  |  Circle Area = π × r²
💡 Tip: Always identify what to ADD and what to SUBTRACT. Draw the shapes separately first!

📖 Worked Example — छायांकित क्षेत्र Step by Step

A square of side 14 cm has a circle inscribed in it. Find the shaded area (area of square NOT covered by circle). Use π = 22/7.
