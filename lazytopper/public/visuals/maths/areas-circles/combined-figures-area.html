संयुक्त क्षेत्रफल (Combined Shaded Area)
Shaded Area = Total Area − Unshaded Area
Area = A(whole) − A(removed part)
∴ हम घटाव (subtraction) से छायांकित क्षेत्र निकालते हैं
💡 Trick: Combined figure = Big shape − Small shape(s). Always identify what is ADDED and what is REMOVED!

📝 Worked Example हल किया उदाहरण
Question: A square of side 14 cm has a circle inscribed in it. Find the area of the shaded region (corners of the square not covered by the circle). Use π = 22/7.
Step 1 — पहचान (Identify Shapes)
We have a Square with side = 14 cm, and an inscribed Circle.
∵ Circle is inscribed, diameter = side of square
∴ diameter = 14 cm → r = 7 cm
Step 2 — Square का क्षेत्रफल
Area of Square = side²
= 14 × 14 = 196 cm²
Step 3 — Circle का क्षेत्रफल
Area of Circle = π r²
= (22/7) × 7 × 7
= 22 × 7 = 154 cm²
Step 4 — घटाव (Subtract)
Shaded Area = Area of Square − Area of Circle
= 196 − 154
= 42 cm²
✅ उत्तर (Answer)
∴ Area of shaded region = 42 cm²
याद रखें: हमेशा बड़े आकार में से छोटे को घटाएं!
