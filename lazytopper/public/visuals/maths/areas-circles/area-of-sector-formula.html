Why does the formula work?
1 A full circle has angle 360° at its centre and area π × r².
2 A sector is a "slice" of the circle making angle θ° at the centre.
3 The sector covers fraction θ/360 of the full circle.
4 ∴ Area of Sector = (θ/360) × π × r²
5 Special case: If θ = 360°, Area = (360/360) × πr² = πr² ✓ (whole circle!)
Worked Example हल किया उदाहरण
❓ Find the area of a sector of a circle with radius 7 cm and angle 60°. (Use π = 22/7)
1 Given: r = 7 cm, θ = 60°, π = 22/7
2 Formula: Area = (θ/360) × π × r²
3 Substituting: Area = (60/360) × (22/7) × 7²
4 Simplify: = (1/6) × (22/7) × 49
5 Calculate: = (1/6) × 22 × 7 = (1/6) × 154
6 ∴ Area = 25⅔ cm² ≈ 25.67 cm² ✅

Key Points to Remember याद रखें
✔Sector area = (θ/360) × πr² — always use angle in degrees
✔For CBSE exams, use π = 22/7 unless told otherwise
✔Minor sector: θ < 180° | Major sector: θ > 180°
✔Arc length = (θ/360) × 2πr (related formula)
∵Sector area is always ≤ full circle area (πr²)
