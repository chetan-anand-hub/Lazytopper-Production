📐 वृत्त खंड का क्षेत्रफल | Area of Sector

CBSE Class 10 Mathematics · Areas Related to Circles

📌 मुख्य सूत्र | Key Formula
Area of Sector = θ ÷ 360 × πr²
θ = angle at centre (in degrees)  |  r = radius
Think of it as: (fraction of full circle) × (area of full circle)
🍕 Pizza Analogy (पिज़्ज़ा सादृश्य)

A full circle is like a whole pizza (360°). A sector is one slice! If you cut the pizza into equal slices of angle θ, then:

Your slice's area = (θ/360) × Total Pizza Area = (θ/360) × πr²

Semi-circle (θ=180°)
½πr²

Quarter (θ=90°)
¼πr²
90/360 = 1/4
∵ A sector is a fraction of the full circle. ∴ its area = that fraction × full circle area.
When θ = 360°: Area = (360/360) × πr² = πr² ✓ (full circle!)

∴ The area of the sector = 18.86 cm² (approx)
💡 परीक्षा युक्तियाँ | Exam Tips
📝 Remember (याद रखें):

• Always check if π = 22/7 or 3.14 is given
• θ must be in degrees for this formula
• Area of sector ≠ Area of segment (segment = sector − triangle)
• If θ = 90°, use ¼πr² directly → saves time!
• Units of area are always square units (cm², m², etc.)

🔑 Formula Trick: Area of Sector = (θ/360) × πr² — memorize as "angle fraction times full area"
