📝 Worked Example (हल उदाहरण)
Question: A circle has radius 14 cm. Find the area of a sector with angle 60°, the arc length, and the area of the corresponding minor segment. (Use π = 22/7)
STEP 1 — Given
Radius r = 14 cm, Central angle θ = 60°, π = 22/7
STEP 2 — Arc Length
ℓ = θ/360 × 2πr
= 60/360 × 2 × 22/7 × 14 = 1/6 × 88 = 44/3 ≈ 14.67 cm
STEP 3 — Area of Sector
A_sector = θ/360 × πr²
= 60/360 × 22/7 × 14² = 1/6 × 22/7 × 196 = 1/6 × 616 = 308/3 ≈ 102.67 cm²
STEP 4 — Area of Triangle OAB
For θ = 60°, triangle OAB is equilateral (OA = OB = r = 14)
A_△ = ½r²sin60° = ½ × 196 × (√3/2) = 49√3 ≈ 84.87 cm²
STEP 5 — Area of Minor Segment
A_segment = A_sector − A_△
= 308/3 − 49√3 = 102.67 − 84.87 ≈ 17.80 cm²
💡 Key Concepts
Sector (त्रिज्यखंड): The region between two radii and the arc. Like a "pizza slice". Minor sector → smaller angle; Major sector → larger angle.
Segment (वृत्तखंड): The region between a chord and the arc. Minor segment → smaller region; Major segment → larger region. ∴ Segment = Sector − Triangle
Arc (चाप): Part of the circumference. Minor arc → subtends angle θ; Major arc → subtends angle (360° − θ). Full circle → 2πr.
Remember: When θ = 180° → Semicircle. When θ = 360° → Full circle. Area of sector ∝ θ (proportional to angle).
