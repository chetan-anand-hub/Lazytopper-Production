📐 Key Formulas क्षेत्रफल के सूत्र

Area of Sector = (θ/360°) × π r²
∵ Sector is (θ/360) fraction of full circle
Length of Arc = (θ/360°) × 2πr
Arc length is same fraction of circumference
Area of Segment = Area of Sector − Area of △AOB
∴ Segment = Sector − Triangle
Area of △AOB= ½ × r² × sin θ
Full Circleθ = 360°, Area = πr²

✏️ Worked Example Step-by-Step हल

Question: A circle has radius 14 cm. Find the area of the sector if the angle at the centre is 60°. Also find the area of the corresponding minor segment. (Use π = 22/7)
📌 Given:
r = 14 cm, θ = 60°, π = 22/7
Step 1: Area of Sector
= (θ/360°) × π r²
= (60/360) × (22/7) × 14²
= (1/6) × (22/7) × 196
= (1/6) × 616 = 102.67 cm² ≈ 102⅔ cm²
Step 2: Area of △AOB (isosceles, OA=OB=r=14)
= ½ × r² × sin 60°
= ½ × 196 × (√3/2)
= ½ × 196 × 0.866
= 84.87 cm²
Step 3: Area of Minor Segment
= Area of Sector − Area of △AOB
= 102.67 − 84.87
= 17.8 cm² (approximately)
∴ Answer:
Area of Sector ≈ 102⅔ cm² | Area of Segment ≈ 17.8 cm²

🔄 Minor vs Major लघु बनाम दीर्घ

⬆ Minor (लघु)
θ < 180°
Smaller arc
Smaller area
∠ at centre < 180°
⬇ Major (दीर्घ)
θ > 180°
Larger arc
Larger area
∠ at centre > 180°
∴ Minor Angle + Major Angle = 360° | Minor Arc + Major Arc = 2πr
