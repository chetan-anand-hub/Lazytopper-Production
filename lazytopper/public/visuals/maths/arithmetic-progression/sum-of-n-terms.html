📐 Arithmetic Progressions — Sum of n Terms

CBSE Class 10 Mathematics | सूत्र (Sūtra) | समांतर श्रेढ़ी का योग

🔑 मुख्य सूत्र (Main Formula) S_n
समांतर श्रेढ़ी के n पदों का योग
S_n = n/2 × (2a + (n−1)d)
Also: S_n = n/2 × (a + l)  |  where l = last term
a = first term  |  d = common difference  |  n = number of terms  |  l = last term (अंतिम पद)

💡 Gauss की युक्ति (Pairing Trick) Intuition

The formula comes from a clever trick: pair the first + last, second + second-last... each pair has the same sum!

📝 Worked Example Step-by-Step
Question: Find the sum of the first 12 terms of the AP: 5, 8, 11, 14, ...
प्रश्न: AP 5, 8, 11, 14, ... के प्रथम 12 पदों का योग ज्ञात करो।
STEP 1 — पहचानो (Identify)
Identify the values from the AP: 5, 8, 11, 14, ...
a = 5  |  d = 8 − 5 = 3  |  n = 12
STEP 2 — सूत्र लिखो (Write Formula)
Write the formula for sum of n terms:
S_n = n/2 × (2a + (n−1)d)
STEP 3 — मान रखो (Substitute)
Put n=12, a=5, d=3 into the formula:
S₁₂ = 12/2 × (2×5 + (12−1)×3)
STEP 4 — सरल करो (Simplify)
Simplify inside the bracket:
S₁₂ = 6 × (10 + 11×3) = 6 × (10 + 33) = 6 × 43
STEP 5 — ∴ उत्तर (Answer)
Calculate the final answer:
∴ S₁₂ = 6 × 43 = 258 ✓

🔄 Alternative Formula When last term is known
S_n = n/2 × (a + l)
l = last term (अंतिम पद) = a + (n−1)d

∵ (because) when we pair first+last = a+l, and there are n/2 such pairs:
S_n = (n/2) × (a + l) — यही Gauss की युक्ति है!

💡 Quick Tip: Use S_n = n/2 (2a+(n−1)d) when d is known. Use S_n = n/2 (a+l) when both first and last terms are known.
🏆 Quick Check Practice

AP: 3, 7, 11, 15, ... — Find S₁₀ (sum of 10 terms)

Solution: a=3, d=4, n=10
S₁₀ = 10/2 × (2×3 + 9×4) = 5 × (6+36) = 5 × 42 = 210 ✓
