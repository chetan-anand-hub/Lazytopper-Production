📐 Arithmetic Progressions (समांतर श्रेढ़ी)

AP Definition & Common Difference — CBSE Class 10 Mathematics

मुख्य सूत्र (Key Formula)
d = an+1 − an
Common Difference = Any Term − Its Preceding Term
∴ d = a₂ − a₁ = a₃ − a₂ = a₄ − a₃ = ...

A sequence is an AP ∵ the difference between consecutive terms is constant

Worked Example (हल किया उदाहरण)

Question: Is 5, 8, 11, 14, 17 an AP? Find d.

Step 1
Write down the given sequence:
a₁ = 5, a₂ = 8, a₃ = 11, a₄ = 14, a₅ = 17
Step 2
Find differences between consecutive terms:
a₂ − a₁ = 8 − 5 = 3
Step 3
Check remaining differences:
a₃ − a₂ = 11 − 8 = 3  |  a₄ − a₃ = 14 − 11 = 3  |  a₅ − a₄ = 17 − 14 = 3
Step 4
∵ All differences are equal (सभी अंतर समान हैं)
∴ The sequence is an AP with d = 3 ✅
