🔍 Visual Proof — Step by Step Click to Reveal
1

Start with a Right Triangle (समकोण त्रिभुज)

Draw a triangle with a 90° angle (∠C = 90°). The sides are: a (base/आधार), b (perpendicular/लम्ब), and c (hypotenuse/कर्ण).

∠C = 90° → समकोण त्रिभुज
2

Draw Squares on Each Side (प्रत्येक भुजा पर वर्ग)

Build a square on each side of the triangle. The square on side a has area a², on b has area b², on c has area c².

Area of square = side × side = side²
3

The Key Insight (मुख्य विचार)

The blue square (c²) on the hypotenuse has the SAME total area as the amber + green squares (a² + b²) combined!

🟨 a² + 🟩 b² = 🟦 c²
4

The Theorem (प्रमेय)

∵ In a right triangle, the square of the hypotenuse equals the sum of squares of the other two sides.

∴ a² + b² = c²
5

Finding the Hypotenuse (कर्ण ज्ञात करना)

To find the hypotenuse c, take the square root of (a² + b²):

c = √(a² + b²)

💡 Key Points to Remember (याद रखें)
📌 The theorem applies ONLY to right triangles (समकोण त्रिभुज)
📌 Hypotenuse (कर्ण) is ALWAYS opposite the 90° angle
📌 Hypotenuse is the longest side of a right triangle
📌 Converse: If a²+b²=c², then the triangle IS right-angled (∵ विलोम प्रमेय)
📌 ∴ c = √(a²+b²), a = √(c²−b²), b = √(c²−a²)
