📐 Basic Proportionality Theorem (BPT)

थेल्स प्रमेय | Thales' Theorem | CBSE Class 10 Mathematics - Chapter 6: Triangles

🔵 प्रमेय (Theorem) BPT / Thales

Statement: If a line is drawn parallel to one side of a triangle to intersect the other two sides in distinct points, then the other two sides are divided in the same ratio.

AD/DB = AE/EC

∵ DE ∥ BC in △ABC, where D is on AB and E is on AC

△ Triangles ∥ Parallel Lines ⊕ Ratio & Proportion ∴ BPT

📝 Worked Example — Step by Step परीक्षा में आता है!

🔢 Problem:

In △ABC, DE ∥ BC. If AD = 4 cm, DB = 6 cm, and AE = 8 cm, find EC.

✅ Final Answer: EC = 12 cm

∴ By BPT, AD/DB = AE/EC → 4/6 = 8/EC → EC = 12 cm

🔁 Converse of BPT उलटा प्रमेय

Converse Statement:

If a line divides any two sides of a triangle in the same ratio, then the line is parallel to the third side.

Is DE ∥ BC? Check the ratios:

✓

If AD/DB = AE/EC (e.g., 3/5 = 6/10 = 0.6) → YES, DE ∥ BC

✗

If AD/DB ≠ AE/EC (e.g., 3/5 ≠ 4/9) → NO, DE is not ∥ BC

AD/DB = AE/EC ⟺ DE ∥ BC

BPT and its Converse work both ways (Biconditional)

📌 Key Reminders परीक्षा Tips

📐 Remember: BPT only works when the line is strictly parallel to the base. Always state "∵ DE ∥ BC" before writing the ratio.
✍️ Notation: Write △ABC clearly. D on AB, E on AC. Then AD/DB = AE/EC by BPT.
⚠️ Common Mistake: Don't write AB/AD = AC/AE (that's wrong order). Use the correct form: AD/DB = AE/EC.
🎯 Also valid: AB/AD = AC/AE (whole over part) — this is the alternate form of BPT. Both are acceptable in proofs!
