Areas of Similar Triangles  Class 10 | CBSE

📐 प्रमेय (Theorem) Theorem 6.6

▶ CBSE Theorem Statement
The ratio of the areas of two similar triangles is equal to the square of the ratio of their corresponding sides.
If △ABC ~ △PQR, then:
ar(△ABC) / ar(△PQR) = (AB/PQ)² = (BC/QR)² = (CA/RP)²
Also equal to the square of the ratio of corresponding altitudes, medians, angle bisectors
∵ Triangles are similar → corresponding sides are proportional → areas follow the square of that ratio

✅ Notice: Area Ratio always equals k² — no matter what scale factor you choose!

🔍 Why k² ? — Visual Understanding

Area of △ = ½ × base × height
When k is the scale factor: new base = k×b, new height = k×h
∴ New area = ½ × (kb) × (kh) = k² × (½ × b × h) = k² × original area

📌 Quick Summary — याद रखो!

▸Similar (सर्वसम नहीं, समरूप) means same shape, angles equal, sides proportional
▸Side ratio = k → Area ratio = k²
▸To find side ratio from area ratio: take √ (square root)
▸Applies to: sides, altitudes, medians, angle bisectors — all squared!
★∵ both base and height scale by k → area scales by k²
