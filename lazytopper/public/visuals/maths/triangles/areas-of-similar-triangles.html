प्रमेय (Theorem 6.6)
If △ABC ~ △PQR, then:
ar(△ABC) / ar(△PQR) = (AB/PQ)² = (BC/QR)² = (AC/PR)²
∴ Area Ratio = (Corresponding Side Ratio)²

💡 Key Concepts to Remember
∵ Similar triangles have equal angles & proportional sides
∴ Area scales as the SQUARE of side ratio
If k = side ratio, then k² = area ratio
Perimeter ratio = k, but area ratio = k²
Proof Sketch:
For △ABC ~ △PQR with ratio k = AB/PQ:
• Draw altitudes h₁ (from A) and h₂ (from P)
• △ABD ~ △PQS ∴ h₁/h₂ = AB/PQ = k
• ar(△ABC) = ½ × BC × h₁
• ar(△PQR) = ½ × QR × h₂
• Ratio = (BC × h₁)/(QR × h₂) = (BC/QR) × (h₁/h₂) = k × k = k²
📝 Worked Example (Step-by-Step)
Problem: △ABC ~ △DEF. If BC = 6 cm, EF = 4 cm, and ar(△DEF) = 24 cm², find ar(△ABC).
Step 1 — Identify what's given
△ABC ~ △DEF (similar triangles)
Corresponding sides: BC = 6 cm, EF = 4 cm
Area of △DEF = 24 cm²
Step 2 — Write the theorem
By Theorem 6.6 (Areas of Similar Triangles):
ar(△ABC) / ar(△DEF) = (BC/EF)²
Step 3 — Substitute values
Putting BC = 6, EF = 4, ar(△DEF) = 24:
ar(△ABC) / 24 = (6/4)² = (3/2)² = 9/4
Step 4 — Solve for ar(△ABC)
Cross-multiplying:
ar(△ABC) = 24 × (9/4) = 6 × 9 = 54 cm²
✅ Answer
∴ Area of △ABC = 54 cm²
Notice: sides ratio = 3:2, area ratio = 9:4 (square!)
