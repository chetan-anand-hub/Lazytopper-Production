🎯 प्रमेय What are Similar Triangles?

Two triangles △ABC and △DEF are similar (△ABC ~ △DEF) if:

∠A = ∠D, ∠B = ∠E, ∠C = ∠F
AND
AB/DE = BC/EF = CA/FD

Corresponding angles are equal AND corresponding sides are proportional

💡 Similar ≠ Congruent — Similar triangles have the same shape but may have different sizes

AA Criterion (Angle-Angle): If two angles of one triangle are equal to two angles of another triangle, the triangles are similar.

∵ If ∠A = ∠D and ∠B = ∠E, then ∠C = ∠F (∵ sum of angles = 180°) ∴ △ABC ~ △DEF

~
∠A = ∠D (α) and ∠B = ∠E (β) ∴ △ABC ~ △DEF (AA Criterion)

SSS Criterion (Side-Side-Side): If all three sides of one triangle are proportional to all three sides of another triangle, the triangles are similar.

If AB/DE = BC/EF = CA/FD, then △ABC ~ △DEF

~
6/4 = 5/3.3 = 5/3.3 = 1.5 (all equal) ∴ △ABC ~ △DEF (SSS Criterion)

SAS Criterion (Side-Angle-Side): If two sides of one triangle are proportional to two sides of another and the included angle is equal, the triangles are similar.

If AB/DE = AC/DF and ∠A = ∠D (included angle), then △ABC ~ △DEF

~
AC/DF = 6/3 = 2, AB/DE = 4/2 = 2, ∠A = ∠D ∴ △ABC ~ △DEF (SAS Criterion)
