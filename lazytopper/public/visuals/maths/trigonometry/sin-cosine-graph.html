🔑 Key Properties

SIN θ
▸ Range: −1 to 1
▸ sin 0° = 0, sin 90° = 1
▸ Starts at 0, peaks at 90°
▸ Odd function: sin(−θ) = −sin θ
▸ Period = 360°
COS θ
▸ Range: −1 to 1
▸ cos 0° = 1, cos 90° = 0
▸ Starts at 1, reaches 0 at 90°
▸ Even function: cos(−θ) = cos θ
▸ Period = 360°
RELATION
▸ cos θ = sin(90° − θ)
▸ sin θ = cos(90° − θ)
▸ sin²θ + cos²θ = 1
▸ cos graph = sin graph
  shifted left by 90°

🎯 CBSE Board Exam Tips

  • Always remember: sin 0° = 0 and cos 0° = 1 — the starting points differ!
  • The identity sin²θ + cos²θ = 1 is used in almost every proof question.
  • Both graphs have the same shape (wave), just shifted by 90°.
  • Maximum value = 1, Minimum value = −1 for both sin and cos.
  • In Class 10, angles are restricted to 0° to 90° — both sin and cos are non-negative here.
