🔑 Key Properties to Remember

📘 sin θ
  • sin 0° = 0
  • sin 90° = 1 (maximum)
  • sin 180° = 0
  • sin 270° = −1 (minimum)
  • sin 360° = 0
  • Odd function: sin(−θ) = −sin θ
📙 cos θ
  • cos 0° = 1 (maximum)
  • cos 90° = 0
  • cos 180° = −1 (minimum)
  • cos 270° = 0
  • cos 360° = 1
  • Even function: cos(−θ) = cos θ
🔗 Relationship: cos θ = sin(90° − θ)  |  sin²θ + cos²θ = 1  |  cos θ is sin θ shifted left by 90°

⭐ CBSE Board Exam Tips

📝 Tip 1: The graph of cos θ is the graph of sin θ shifted 90° to the left.
📝 Tip 2: Both graphs have the same shape (wave), same range [−1, 1], same period 360°.
📝 Tip 3: sin θ = 0 at 0°, 180°, 360° → these are x-intercepts of sin graph.
📝 Tip 4: cos θ = 0 at 90°, 270° → these are x-intercepts of cos graph.
📝 Tip 5: Remember: "Sin Starts at zero, Cos starts at one" — easy memory trick!
