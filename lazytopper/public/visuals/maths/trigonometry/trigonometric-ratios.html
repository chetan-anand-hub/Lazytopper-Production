📖 CBSE-style Worked Example

Question: In △ABC, right-angled at B, AB = 5 cm, BC = 12 cm. Find sin A, cos A, tan A.


Step 1: Find AC (Hypotenuse) using Pythagoras Theorem:

AC² = AB² + BC² = 5² + 12² = 25 + 144 = 169

∴ AC = 13 cm


Step 2: Identify sides with respect to angle A:

• Opposite to A = BC = 12 cm

• Adjacent to A = AB = 5 cm

• Hypotenuse = AC = 13 cm


Step 3: Apply the ratios:

sin A = Opp/Hyp = 12/13 ≈ 0.923

cos A = Adj/Hyp = 5/13 ≈ 0.385

tan A = Opp/Adj = 12/5 = 2.4


Verification: sin²A + cos²A = (12/13)² + (5/13)² = 144/169 + 25/169 = 169/169 = 1 ✓
