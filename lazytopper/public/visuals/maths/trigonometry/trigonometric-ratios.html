📘 sin θ
= Opposite (लंब) / Hypotenuse (कर्ण)
= BC / AC
📗 cos θ
= Adjacent (आधार) / Hypotenuse (कर्ण)
= AB / AC
📙 tan θ
= Opposite (लंब) / Adjacent (आधार)
= BC / AB
∴ tan θ = sin θ / cos θ
