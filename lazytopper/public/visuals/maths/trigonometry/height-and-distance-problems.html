🔺 Angle of Elevation (उन्नयन कोण)

When you look UP at an object, the angle formed between the horizontal line and your line of sight is called the Angle of Elevation.

tan θ = Height (h) / Distance (d) = Perpendicular / Base
∴ h = d × tan θ  |  d = h / tan θ  |  θ = tan⁻¹(h/d)

🔻 Angle of Depression (अवनमन कोण)

When you look DOWN at an object, the angle formed between the horizontal line and your line of sight is called the Angle of Depression.

tan θ = h / d   (same formula as elevation!)
The angle of depression from top = angle of elevation from bottom (alternate angles, ∵ parallel lines)
∠ Depression = ∠ Elevation Alternate Interior Angles Parallel Lines Property

💡 Key insight: When a line of sight goes from top to bottom or bottom to top between the same two points, both angles are equal (alternate angles theorem)!

✏️ Worked Example (हल किया गया उदाहरण)
📝 Problem:
A tower stands on level ground. From a point 40 m away from the base of the tower, the angle of elevation of the top of the tower is 60°. Find the height of the tower.
Step 1: Draw and Label the Diagram


Given: Distance (d) = 40 m, Angle of Elevation (θ) = 60°
Find: Height of tower (h) = ?
Step 2: Identify the correct trigonometric ratio

In right △ABC:
• We know: Base (d) = 40 m
• We want: Perpendicular (h)
• The ratio involving Perpendicular and Base is... tan θ!

∴ We use: tan θ = Perpendicular / Base = h / d
Step 3: Substitute the values

tan 60° = h / 40

From trig table: tan 60° = √3

∴ √3 = h / 40
Step 4: Solve for h

√3 = h / 40

h = 40 × √3

h = 40√3 m

h ≈ 40 × 1.732 ≈ 69.28 m
✅ Step 5: Write the Answer

∴ The height of the tower = 40√3 m ≈ 69.28 m

Verification: tan 60° = 69.28/40 = 1.732 ≈ √3 ✓

∠ of Elevation = 60° tan θ formula used Answer: 40√3 m

💡 Quick Tips for Height & Distance (परीक्षा के लिए)
1. Always draw a neat diagram first!
2. Mark the right angle (90°) at the base of the tower/pole
3. Angle of elevation = Angle of depression (alternate angles)
4. Choose tan when you know two sides (h and d)
5. Use sin
