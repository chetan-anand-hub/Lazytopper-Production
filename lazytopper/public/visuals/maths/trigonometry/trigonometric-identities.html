📖 प्रमाण (Proof) — Step by Step

Click each step to reveal the proof using Pythagoras Theorem:

Step 1 — Pythagoras प्रमेय
In a right triangle: p² + b² = h²
Step 2 — h² से भाग दें (Divide by h²)
Dividing both sides by h²: p²/h² + b²/h² = 1
Step 3 — Definitions लागू करें
We know: sinθ = p/h and cosθ = b/h
So: (p/h)² + (b/h)² = 1
Step 4 — सिद्ध हुआ ✓
∴ sin²θ + cos²θ = 1  ✅ Proved! (सिद्ध)
📋 तीन मुख्य सर्वसमिकाएं (3 Identities)

Identity 2
Identity 3
sin²θ + cos²θ = 1
From this we get: sin²θ = 1 − cos²θ
Also: cos²θ = 1 − sin²θ
याद करें: "Sine squared plus Cosine squared equals ONE" — always!
1 + tan²θ = sec²θ
Divide p² + b² = h² by b² (base²)
⟹ p²/b² + 1 = h²/b²
⟹ tan²θ + 1 = sec²θ ✅
∴ sec²θ − tan²θ = 1
Trick: sec²θ − tan²θ = 1 (sec is bigger than tan)
1 + cot²θ = cosec²θ
Divide p² + b² = h² by p² (perpendicular²)
⟹ 1 + b²/p² = h²/p²
⟹ 1 + cot²θ = cosec²θ ✅
∴ cosec²θ − cot²θ = 1
Trick: cosec²θ − cot²θ = 1 (cosec is bigger than cot)
✏️ हल किया उदाहरण (Worked Example) CBSE Type

प्रश्न: If sinθ = 3/5, find cosθ and verify sin²θ + cos²θ = 1

Step 1 — दिया गया (Given)
sinθ = 3/5 → This means Perpendicular = 3, Hypotenuse = 5
Step 2 — Base निकालें (Find Base)
By Pythagoras: b² = h² − p² = 5² − 3² = 25 − 9 = 16
∴ b = 4
Step 3 — cosθ निकालें
cosθ = b/h = 4/5
Step 4 — सत्यापित करें (Verify)
sin²θ + cos²θ = (3/5)² + (4/5)² = 9/25 + 16/25 = 25/25 = 1 ✅
