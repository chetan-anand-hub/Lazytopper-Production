🧮 Geometric Proof (Pythagoras) प्रमाण

P²+B²=H² (Pythagoras) → Divide by H² → sin²θ+cos²θ=1

📝 Step-by-Step Proof चरण-दर-चरण

Click "Next Step" to reveal each step of the proof of sin²θ + cos²θ = 1

1

Consider a right △ABC with ∠B = 90°, ∠A = θ

2

By Pythagoras Theorem: AB² + BC² = AC² i.e., B² + P² = H²

3

sin θ = P/H,   cos θ = B/H

4

∴ sin²θ = P²/H²   and   cos²θ = B²/H²

5

Adding: sin²θ + cos²θ = P²/H² + B²/H² = (P²+B²)/H²

6

∵ P²+B²=H² (Pythagoras) ∴ (P²+B²)/H² = H²/H² = 1 ✓

7

∴ sin²θ + cos²θ = 1   (सिद्ध / Proved ✓)

✏️ Worked Example हल उदाहरण
Q: If sin θ = 3/5, find cos θ using the identity.
Step 1: Use identity: sin²θ + cos²θ = 1
Step 2: (3/5)² + cos²θ = 1
    ∴ 9/25 + cos²θ = 1
Step 3: cos²θ = 1 − 9/25 = 25/25 − 9/25 = 16/25
Step 4: ∴ cos θ = √(16/25) = 4/5 ✓
(Since θ is acute, cos θ > 0)
🔗 Deriving sec²θ − tan²θ = 1

Starting from sin²θ + cos²θ = 1, divide entire equation by cos²θ:

sin²θ/cos²θ  +  cos²θ/cos²θ  =  1/cos²θ
tan²θ  +  1  =  sec²θ
∴ sec²θ − tan²θ = 1 ✓
💡 Similarly divide by sin²θ to get: cosec²θ − cot²θ = 1
