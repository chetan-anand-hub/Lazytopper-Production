वास्तविक संख्याएँ | CBSE Class 10 Mathematics | Chapter 1

📐 Key Formula प्रमेय
HCF(a, b) × LCM(a, b) = a × b
This works for any two positive integers a and b
💡 HCF (म.स.) = Product of common prime factors with lowest powers
💡 LCM (ल.स.) = Product of all prime factors with highest powers
