√2

Is √2 a rational number? Ancient mathematicians assumed YES — and found a contradiction! This is called Proof by Contradiction (विरोधाभास प्रमाण)

What? Rational vs Irrational

Click each type to see its property:

p/q Rational

Can be written as p/q where p,q are integers, q≠0. Example: 1/2, 3, -5/7

√ Irrational

CANNOT be written as p/q. Decimal never repeats or terminates. Example: √2, π, √3

💡 Key Insight: You can never find an exact fraction pq that equals √2. But how do we PROVE this?

प्रमेय Proof: √2 is Irrational

Method: विरोधाभास (Contradiction)

Assume the opposite of what we want to prove, then show it leads to a contradiction!

Step 0 / 6

1मान लीजिए — Assumption (विरोधाभास के लिए)

Assume (मान लीजिए) that √2 is rational.

√2 = pq    where p, q ∈ ℤ, q ≠ 0

Also assume p and q have no common factors — i.e., the fraction is in its lowest form (न्यूनतम रूप).

∴ HCF(p, q) = 1

2Square Both Sides — दोनों पक्षों का वर्ग

Squaring both sides:

(√2)² = (p/q)²  ⟹  2 = p²/q²
∴ p² = 2q²

This means p² is divisible by 2.

3p is Even — p सम संख्या है

Since p² is divisible by 2, and if a prime divides p², it divides p:

2 | p²  ⟹  2 | p

∴ p = 2m for some integer m

Theorem used: If a prime number p divides a², then p divides a.
4Substitute p = 2m — प्रतिस्थापन

Put p = 2m into p² = 2q²:

(2m)² = 2q²  ⟹  4m² = 2q²
∴ q² = 2m²

This means q² is also divisible by 2.

∴ By same logic, q is also even (q = 2k for some integer k)

5विरोधाभास! — Contradiction!
⚡ CONTRADICTION (विरोधाभास):

We showed: p is even AND q is even
∴ Both p and q have 2 as a common factor

But we assumed: HCF(p, q) = 1 (no common factors!)

This is a contradiction! ✗
6∴ निष्कर्ष — Conclusion

∵ Our assumption leads to a contradiction,

∴ Our assumption was WRONG!

√2 ≠ p/q for any integers p, q

∴ √2 is IRRATIONAL (अपरिमेय) ✓
