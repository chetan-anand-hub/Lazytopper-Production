🔑 Key Concept (मुख्य अवधारणा)

Every rational number p/q has one of two types of decimal expansion:
p/q → Terminating OR Non-Terminating Repeating
∵ q has only 2's and 5's as prime factors → Terminating decimal

📊 Two Types — Click to Explore

✅ Terminating (सांत)

Decimal ends after finite digits
0.5   1.25   3.125
q = 2ᵃ × 5ᵇ only

🔄 Non-Terminating Repeating (असांत आवर्ती)

Decimal repeats a pattern forever
0.333…   0.142857…
q has prime ≠ 2 or 5

📌 The Golden Rule (प्रमेय 1.5)

Let x = p/q be a rational number such that q ≠ 0
✅ x is TERMINATING ↔ q = 2ⁿ × 5ᵐ (n,m ≥ 0)
🔄 x is NON-TERMINATING REPEATING ↔ q has prime factor other than 2 & 5
∴ Irrational numbers are Non-Terminating Non-Repeating
e.g. √2 = 1.41421356… (never repeats!)
