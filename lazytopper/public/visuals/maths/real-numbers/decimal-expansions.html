मुख्य प्रमेय (Key Theorem)
p/q is terminating ⟺ q = 2ⁿ × 5ᵐ
where p, q are integers, q ≠ 0, and p/q is in lowest terms (HCF = 1)

✅ Terminating Decimal (सांत दशमलव)

Decimal expansion that ends after a finite number of digits.

3/8 = 0.375
Condition: Denominator q (in lowest terms) has ONLY factors 2 and/or 5
q = 2ⁿ × 5ᵐ (n,m are whole numbers ≥ 0)
Examples: 1/2, 7/20, 11/125, 3/8, 9/16

🔄 Non-Terminating Repeating (असांत आवर्ती)

Decimal that goes on forever but has a repeating block (bar notation).

1/3 = 0.3
Condition: Denominator q has factors OTHER than 2 and 5
q has prime factors like 3, 7, 11, 13…
Examples: 1/3, 1/7, 5/6, 2/11, 7/12
All rational numbers are either terminating OR non-terminating repeating
💡 Remember: An irrational number (जैसे √2, π) has a non-terminating, non-repeating decimal expansion — it never ends and never repeats!
