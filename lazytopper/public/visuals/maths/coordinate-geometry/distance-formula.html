📐 Distance Formula

CBSE Class 10 · Coordinate Geometry

Distance between two points P(x₁,y₁) and Q(x₂,y₂)
PQ = √[ (x₂−x₁)² + (y₂−y₁)² ]
Derived from the Pythagorean Theorem

💡 Key Concepts
📌 The formula comes from Pythagoras' Theorem: a² + b² = c²
📌 Horizontal distance = |x₂ − x₁| (base of right triangle)
📌 Vertical distance = |y₂ − y₁| (height of right triangle)
📌 The hypotenuse is the straight-line distance PQ
📌 Distance is always non-negative (√ gives +ve root)
