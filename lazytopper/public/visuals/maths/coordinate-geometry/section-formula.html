⚡ Section Formula (खंड सूत्र)

Point P divides A(x₁,y₁) and B(x₂,y₂) in ratio m : n

P(x, y) =   (mx₂ + nx₁) / (m+n)  ,  (my₂ + ny₁) / (m+n)
🔵 Midpoint Formula:  M = ((x₁+x₂)/2 , (y₁+y₂)/2)
∵ आंतरिक विभाजन (Internal Division) | विभाजन बिंदु (Division Point)

Example: Find point P dividing A(1,3) and B(7,9) in ratio 2:1

A = (1, 3)
B = (7, 9)
m:n = 2:1

✅ Final Answer (∴ उत्तर)

P = (5, 7) divides A(1,3) and B(7,9) in ratio 2:1 internally

Example: Midpoint of A(−2, 4) and B(6, −2)

A = (−2, 4)
B = (6, −2)
Midpoint → m = n
Formula
Apply Midpoint Formula
Midpoint M = ((x₁+x₂)/2 , (y₁+y₂)/2)
M = ((−2+6)/2 , (4+(−2))/2) = (4/2 , 2/2) = (2, 1)

✅ Midpoint (मध्यबिंदु)

M = (2, 1) is the midpoint of A(−2,4) and B(6,−2)

💡 Key Points to Remember

📌 Internal Division (आंतरिक विभाजन)
P lies BETWEEN A and B. Both m and n are positive.
📌 Midpoint Special Case
When m = n = 1, Section Formula becomes Midpoint Formula. ∴ Midpoint is a special case!
📌 Memory Trick (याद करें)
x = (m×x₂ + n×x₁)/(m+n) → m goes with the FAR point x₂, n goes with the NEAR point x₁
📌 CBSE Exam Tip
Always write: "Let P(x,y) divide AB internally in ratio m:n" before applying formula. ∵ full marks require proper steps!
