# NCERT Key Points — Triangles (Class 10)

## Similarity criteria
- AA (or AAA): two angles equal in both triangles.
- SAS: ratio of two sides equal and included angle equal.
- SSS: all three sides in proportion.

## Corresponding parts
- Once triangles are similar, corresponding angles are equal.
- Corresponding sides are in proportion (CPST).

## Basic Proportionality Theorem (BPT)
- If a line is parallel to one side of a triangle, it divides the other two sides in the same ratio.
- Converse: if a line divides two sides in the same ratio, it is parallel to the third side.

## Area relation (similar triangles)
- Ratio of areas equals square of ratio of corresponding sides.

## Pythagoras (right triangle)
- Hypotenuse^2 = sum of squares of other two sides.
- Applies only for right-angled triangles.
