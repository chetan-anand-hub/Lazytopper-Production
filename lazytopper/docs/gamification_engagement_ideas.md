# Gamification & Engagement Ideas (S23)

This document captures the gamification strategies proposed in Task S23.  It introduces the following ideas:

1. **Power Hour Rush Challenge** – A weekly mini‑challenge that encourages students to study during their personalised power hour.  Completing the challenge unlocks a special badge and is highlighted in Weekly Wrapped.
2. **Daily Mix Collectible Cards** – Collectible cards awarded for completing Daily Focus Mixes.  Card sets unlock cosmetic rewards and are showcased in Weekly Wrapped.
3. **TopicBoss Titles & HPQ Mastery Bands** – Titles such as “Trigo Titan” or “Carbon Commander” that reward high performance in specific chapters.  Progress bars and thresholds encourage mastery on must‑crack questions.
4. **Soft Squad Goals (Optional)** – A future social extension that aggregates mix completions and badges at the squad level.

These ideas integrate seamlessly with existing modules (streaks, badges, Daily Mix, Weekly Wrapped, Match %) and provide clear data/event requirements for implementation.  See `LazyTopper_S23_Gamification_and_Engagement_Ideas.md` in the root for the full proposal.
